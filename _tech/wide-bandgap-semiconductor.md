## What This Is

Semiconductors with bandgap >2 eV (GaN ~3.4 eV, SiC ~3.3 eV) enabling higher voltage, frequency, and temperature operation than silicon for EV inverters, fast chargers, and RF power.. **Achieved.**

Early commercial products exist. Market is forming with limited but growing deployment. Our research database tracks **19 independent sources** on this technology.

> Thermo Fisher application note on wide bandgap power MOSFET failure analysis confirms GaN and SiC in active production for EV and IoT applications; specific EFA+PFA workflow developed for these materials.
