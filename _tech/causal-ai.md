Causal AI is a branch of machine learning that moves beyond pattern recognition (correlation) to understand cause-and-effect relationships. It allows AI systems to reason about "what-if" scenarios (counterfactuals) and interventions.

Key attributes:
- **Structural Causal Models (SCM):** Using graph-based representations to define causal dependencies.
- **Explainability:** Inherently provides a rationale for decisions based on causal pathways.
- **Robustness:** Better at generalizing to new environments where correlations might change but causal laws remain.
- **Unlocks:** Autonomous scientific discovery, more reliable AI agents, and precise policy intervention modeling.
