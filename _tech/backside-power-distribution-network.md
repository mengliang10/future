## What This Is

Power rails routed on the backside of the wafer (below active devices) to reduce IR drop and free frontside routing tracks; enables density and power efficiency at 2nm and below; Intel/TSMC active development..

Pilot deployments and early-stage commercial testing underway. Technology is proven at small scale. Our research database tracks **1 independent source** on this technology.

> Thermo Fisher advanced logic white paper identifies backside metal layers for power distribution as a new challenge for failure analysis labs; adoption of new FA processes required at advanced nodes.
