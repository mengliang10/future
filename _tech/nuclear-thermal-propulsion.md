NTP systems use a nuclear reactor to heat a propellant (typically liquid hydrogen) to extreme temperatures, exhausting it through a nozzle to generate thrust. NTP offers twice the efficiency of the best chemical rockets.

Key attributes:
- **High Specific Impulse (Isp):** ~900 seconds vs ~450 seconds for chemical propulsion.
- **Rapid Mars Transit:** Reducing one-way trip time from 7-9 months to 2-3 months.
- **Crew Safety:** Shorter transit times significantly reduce radiation exposure and microgravity effects for astronauts.
- **Active Projects:** NASA/DARPA DRACO (Demonstration Rocket for Agile Cislunar Operations) targeted for 2027 launch.
