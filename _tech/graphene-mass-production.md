Graphene is a single layer of carbon atoms in a hexagonal lattice. It is 200 times stronger than steel, highly conductive, and transparent. While its properties are well-known, mass-producing high-quality, large-area graphene at low cost has been the primary barrier.

Key attributes:
- **Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD):** The leading method for producing large-area, high-quality graphene films.
- **Roll-to-Roll Processing:** Scaling manufacturing to industrial throughput.
- **Applications:** High-frequency transistors (terahertz computing), ultra-efficient batteries, and advanced composite materials.
- **Unlocks:** Post-silicon electronics and revolutionary material strength-to-weight ratios.
