## What This Is

Digital money that executes automatically on predefined conditions (escrow-on-delivery, milestone payouts, automated compliance) via smart contracts..

Early commercial products exist. Market is forming with limited but growing deployment. Our research database tracks **1 independent source** on this technology.

> Siemens implemented programmable money across 190 countries: 50% reduction in bank accounts, 70% less management effort, 80% automation of cash application, $20M+ annual savings. Accenture 2026.
