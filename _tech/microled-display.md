## What This Is

Self-emissive display using arrays of microscale (1-100 µm) LEDs; offers OLED-level contrast with higher brightness and longer lifetime; primary challenge is mass transfer yield and colour conversion at scale..

Pilot deployments and early-stage commercial testing underway. Technology is proven at small scale. Our research database tracks **1 independent source** on this technology.

> Thermo Fisher display white paper describes metrology needs for next-generation light-conversion efficiency and display architectures; MicroLED implied as pathfinding development target alongside quantum dot.
