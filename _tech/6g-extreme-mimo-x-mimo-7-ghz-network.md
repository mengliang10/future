## What This Is

256-port massive MIMO base station operating in the 7 GHz upper mid-band for 6G; achieves 8-layer spatial multiplexing and 3+ Gbps per user over 100 MHz. Demonstrated in Samsung field trials Seoul & Plano TX (2026). WRC-23 internationally allocated 7.125 GHz for IMT..

Working prototypes demonstrated. Engineering challenges remain before commercial deployment. Our research database tracks **29 independent sources** on this technology.

> WRC-23 internationally allocated 7.125 GHz band for IMT (6G). US government repurposing memo, EU and Korea active spectrum planning.
