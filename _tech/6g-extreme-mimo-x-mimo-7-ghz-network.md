## What This Is

256-port massive MIMO base station operating in the 7 GHz upper mid-band for 6G; achieves 8-layer spatial multiplexing and 3+ Gbps per user over 100 MHz. Demonstrated in Samsung field trials Seoul & Plano TX (2026). WRC-23 internationally allocated 7.125 GHz for IMT..

Working prototypes demonstrated. Engineering challenges remain before commercial deployment. Our research database tracks **16 independent sources** on this technology.

> Samsung 256T256R 7 GHz prototype field-tested in Seoul and Plano TX. Achieved >3 Gbps single-user downlink over 100 MHz with 8-layer 256QAM. Testbed exists (Fig 3); results are measured not simulated.
