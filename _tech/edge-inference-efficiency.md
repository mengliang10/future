## What This Is

As centralized data centers hit the "Physics Wall" (energy and cooling constraints), the AI moat is fragmenting. Companies are pivoting to localized inference and "Chain Abstraction" to reduce the $100B+ capex burden. This involves moving compute to the "Edge" — closer to the user, where power is more distributed and latency is lower.
