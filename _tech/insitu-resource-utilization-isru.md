---

ISRU is the practice of collecting, processing, and using materials found on other planetary bodies (the Moon, Mars, asteroids) to replace materials that would otherwise be brought from Earth.

Key attributes:
- **Oxygen Production:** Extracting O2 from Martian CO2 or Lunar regolith.
- **Propellant Synthesis:** Using the Sabatier reaction to create methane and oxygen from Martian atmosphere and ice.
- **3D Printing Regolith:** Using local soil to print habitats and landing pads.
- **Unlocks:** Sustainable long-term presence on the Moon and Mars by "living off the land."
