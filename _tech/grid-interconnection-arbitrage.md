## What This Is

The "Grid Lockout" of 2026 has created a massive premium for companies with existing baseload power and approved interconnection queue positions. As US grid interconnection wait times exceed 36-60 months in key regions (PJM, ERCOT), the "Physical Wall" of AI has turned electricity into a de-facto infrastructure asset class.

> "Eventually the cost of intelligence, the cost of AI, will converge to the cost of energy." - Sam Altman, 2025. Today, we see this manifest as hyperscalers pay 2x national averages to restart retired nuclear plants (e.g., Three Mile Island).
