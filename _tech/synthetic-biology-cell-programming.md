Cell programming involves designing and "installing" genetic circuits into living cells to give them new functions. This effectively turns cells into biological factories or programmable sensors/therapeutics.

Key attributes:
- **Genetic Circuit Design:** Using Boolean logic gates (AND, OR, NOT) implemented with proteins and DNA.
- **Bio-Manufacturing:** Engineering yeast or bacteria to produce complex chemicals, fuels, or textiles (e.g., spider silk).
- **Living Medicines:** Cells that can detect a disease marker and produce a localized therapeutic response.
- **Horizontal Platform:** Relies on automated bio-foundries and AI-driven protein design.
