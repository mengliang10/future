## Investment Thesis
Intel is the highest-risk, highest-potential re-rating story in semiconductors. The bear case (Intel Foundry fails, x86 market share continues to erode to ARM, Gaudi3 gains no traction) is well-understood and largely priced in. The bull case requires three things to go right simultaneously: Intel 18A yields at competitive parity with TSMC N2, at least one major external customer wins for Intel Foundry, and Gaudi3/Gaudi4 captures meaningful AI accelerator share. Intel's silicon photonics business is genuinely undervalued: the longest commercial CPO history and the most direct play on co-packaged optics adoption. Position sizing should reflect the binary nature of the foundry execution risk.
