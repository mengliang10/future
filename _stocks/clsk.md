## Investment Thesis
CleanSpark is the ESG-friendly Bitcoin miner — nearly 100% renewable energy sourcing, vertically integrated operations, and an aggressive acquisition strategy targeting distressed mining assets post-halving. They grow by buying cheap and operating efficiently.

### Key Drivers
- **Renewable Energy:** Near-100% clean energy sourcing attracts ESG mandates.
- **M&A Consolidation:** Acquiring mining facilities at distressed prices accelerates hash rate growth.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Industry-leading power usage effectiveness ratios.

### Risk Factors
- **Integration Risk:** Rapid acquisitions create integration and operational complexity.
- **BTC Volatility:** Mining profitability collapses if BTC falls below ~$40K.
