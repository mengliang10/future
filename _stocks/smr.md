## Investment Thesis
NuScale Power is developing the world's first NRC-approved small modular reactor design. SMRs are the answer to the AI era's power problem: data centres need reliable, carbon-free baseload that wind and solar cannot provide. NuScale's VOYGR plant design can be factory-built and deployed in 3 years vs 10+ for traditional nuclear — changing the economics entirely.

### Key Drivers
- **NRC Approval:** Only SMR design with full NRC design certification — a multi-year regulatory moat.
- **AI Data Centre Demand:** Microsoft, Google, and Amazon all exploring nuclear for data centre baseload power.
- **Export Potential:** Romania, Poland, and Philippines are evaluating NuScale deployments.

### Risk Factors
- **Project Cancellations:** UAMPS Carbon Free Power Project cancellation (2023) highlights commercial risk.
- **Pre-Revenue:** No deployed plants; revenue is licensing and services — cash burn until first deployment.
