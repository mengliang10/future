## Investment Thesis
MicroStrategy is a leveraged Bitcoin holding vehicle with a software business attached. CEO Michael Saylor's thesis: borrow at low fixed rates, buy Bitcoin, hold forever. The premium-to-NAV is a feature, not a bug — it reflects equity holders' conviction in the strategy. MSTR is the only way to get levered Bitcoin exposure inside a regulated equity account.

### Key Drivers
- **BTC Accumulation:** 214,000+ BTC on balance sheet (May 2025). Every equity raise = more BTC.
- **Institutional Wrapper:** S&P 500 / Nasdaq-100 inclusion = passive fund buying = price support.
- **Leverage Amplification:** 2–3× Bitcoin beta in bull markets as premium expands.

### Risk Factors
- **Forced Liquidation Risk:** If BTC falls below debt covenants, selling pressure compounds.
- **Premium Collapse:** In bear markets the NAV premium inverts — massive drawdowns.
