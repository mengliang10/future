## Investment Thesis
Vistra is the largest competitive power generator in the US — a mix of nuclear, natural gas, and rapidly growing battery storage. Their Illinois nuclear fleet benefits from the same hyperscaler demand driving Constellation. Vistra is cheaper on a per-MWh basis and has a more diversified generation mix, making it the value play in the nuclear power trade.

### Key Drivers
- **Illinois Nuclear Fleet:** 6 nuclear units providing reliable baseload — all in areas of high data centre demand.
- **Battery Storage Leader:** 6+ GWh of battery storage capacity — the largest in the US.
- **Power Price Leverage:** In competitive electricity markets, rising power prices flow directly to earnings.

### Risk Factors
- **Market Power Prices:** Earnings are sensitive to regional electricity market prices (ERCOT, PJM).
- **Concentrated Generation Mix:** Nuclear concentration means any plant issue has outsized impact.
