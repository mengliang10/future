## Investment Thesis
Beam Therapeutics uses base editing — a more precise version of CRISPR that makes single-letter DNA changes without cutting the double helix. This dramatically reduces off-target effects and opens a wider range of disease targets. Beam's technology was developed from the Broad Institute's foundational research; their partnership with Pfizer validates the platform.

### Key Drivers
- **Base Editing Precision:** Targets single base pairs without DNA double-strand breaks — safer, more precise than classical CRISPR.
- **Pfizer Partnership:** $300M+ collaboration provides validation, capital, and potential milestone payments.
- **Sickle Cell + Liver Disease Pipeline:** Multiple programs targeting high-unmet-need diseases with clear regulatory precedent.

### Risk Factors
- **Clinical Stage Risk:** No approved products; every IND filing is a binary event.
- **Cash Runway:** Pre-revenue company managing burn across multiple clinical programs.
