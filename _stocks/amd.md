## Investment Thesis
AMD is the only credible alternative to NVIDIA in the AI accelerator market. Their MI300X and MI325X series offer superior memory capacity at a more competitive TCO, making them the primary choice for "second-sourcing" by Hyperscalers.

### Key Drivers
*   **MI300X Ramp:** Successfully scaling production to multi-billion dollar revenue.
*   **Client PC Recovery:** Strong leadership in AI PCs (Ryzen AI) capturing market share from Intel.
*   **DC Integration:** Leveraging Xilinx assets to provide integrated networking and compute solutions.

### Risk Factors
*   **Market Share Concentration:** Difficulty in breaking NVIDIA's software (CUDA) moat.
*   **Supply Chain Dependencies:** Reliance on TSM for both logic and advanced packaging.
