## Investment Thesis
Synopsys is the largest EDA vendor by revenue, holding a near-duopoly with Cadence. Their acquisition of ANSYS deepens their exposure to system-level simulation (electronics + physical). They are a primary beneficiary of the custom silicon trend, as companies like Microsoft, Amazon, and Meta use Synopsys tools to design their in-house AI chips.
