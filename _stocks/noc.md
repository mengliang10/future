## Investment Thesis
Northrop Grumman is the prime contractor for the US Space Force, GBSD (nuclear deterrent replacement), B-21 stealth bomber, and James Webb Space Telescope. As space becomes a contested domain and nuclear modernisation accelerates, Northrop's backlog is nearly irreplaceable. A consistent dividend grower with strategic programme lock-in.

### Key Drivers
- **B-21 Raider:** Next-generation stealth bomber — a generational programme that will span decades.
- **Space Systems:** Satellite intelligence, missile warning, and space domain awareness contracts.
- **GBSD (Sentinel):** Ground-based nuclear deterrent replacement — a $100B+ programme spanning 30 years.

### Risk Factors
- **Cost Overruns:** Fixed-price development contracts on B-21 carry risk of margin compression.
- **Budget Cycles:** Pentagon budget negotiations create quarterly uncertainty.
