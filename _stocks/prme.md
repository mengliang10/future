Prime Medicine is pioneering "Prime Editing," a precise gene-editing technology often called "search-and-replace" for the genome. Unlike traditional CRISPR-Cas9 which makes double-strand breaks, Prime Editing can make any type of base substitution, insertion, or deletion without breaking the DNA backbone, significantly reducing off-target risks.

Key technical advantages:
- **Prime Editing IP:** Foundational IP for the next generation of gene editing beyond Cas9.
- **Versatility:** Capable of correcting ~90% of known disease-causing genetic mutations.
- **Precision:** Minimal unintended insertions or deletions (indels) compared to earlier technologies.
