## Investment Thesis
Uber is the largest ride-hailing and food delivery platform globally, and uniquely positioned as the distribution layer for autonomous vehicle deployment. Rather than building its own AV technology (after divesting ATG in 2020), Uber partners with AV companies: Waymo in Austin and Atlanta, Motional in Las Vegas, and WeRide. This asset-light AV strategy means Uber benefits from AV cost reduction (no driver payment) without carrying AV development risk. In parallel, Uber's core ride-hailing business is growing 18%+ annually with improving driver economics and expanding international markets. Key risk: AV partners may eventually bypass Uber to build direct consumer apps; Waymo One already operates independently in San Francisco and Phoenix.
