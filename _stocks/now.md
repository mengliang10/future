## Investment Thesis
ServiceNow is the enterprise workflow automation platform with the strongest AI monetisation narrative in SaaS. Now Assist (built on generative AI) is embedding LLM-driven automation into ITSM, HR, legal, and finance workflows. Enterprise customers already paying for the platform see Now Assist as an accelerator of existing ROI — not a new budget line — which reduces friction to adoption. Revenue growing 22%+ annually; Rule of 40 score consistently above 50. Largest deals (multi-year, multi-product) are getting larger as ServiceNow expands from IT into cross-enterprise AI workflow. Key risk: premium valuation (45x+ NTM revenue in up cycles) means any guidance miss creates outsized multiple compression. Competition from Microsoft (Power Automate with Copilot) is the primary structural threat.
