## Investment Thesis
Snap's long-term bet is augmented reality — they have invested $3B+ in AR hardware and software infrastructure. Spectacles AR glasses (5th generation) are the most advanced consumer AR device not made by Apple or Meta. Their AR Lens platform with 300K+ developer-created lenses is the largest AR development ecosystem. The social media app generates cash that funds the AR platform.

### Key Drivers
- **Spectacles Platform:** Generation 5 Spectacles are the leading consumer AR glasses — developer seed programme in 2024.
- **AR Developer Ecosystem:** More AR experiences than any competitor; SnapKit integrates AR into third-party apps.
- **Gen Z Engagement:** Snapchat remains the dominant messaging platform for 13–24 year olds in the US and Europe.

### Risk Factors
- **Revenue Volatility:** Digital advertising revenue is highly cyclical; Apple ATT permanently impaired targeting.
- **AR Timeline Risk:** Consumer AR mass adoption may be 8–10 years away; Snap may run out of patience.
