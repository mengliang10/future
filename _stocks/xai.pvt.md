xAI is Elon Musk's AI venture, founded to build "truth-seeking" AI and compete with OpenAI and Google. The company's primary product is Grok, integrated into the X (formerly Twitter) platform. xAI recently completed the "Colossus" training cluster in Memphis, featuring 100,000 NVIDIA H100 GPUs, currently the world's most powerful AI supercomputer.

Key technical advantages:
- **Colossus Supercomputer:** Massive internal compute capacity reducing dependency on hyperscalers.
- **Real-time Data Access:** Direct integration with X's real-time information flow.
- **Rapid Iteration:** High-speed development cycle from founding to frontier-class models (Grok-3) in under 18 months.
