## Investment Thesis
Arista is the dominant vendor for high-speed Ethernet switching in AI data centres. As GPU clusters scale from 8K to 100K+ accelerators, low-latency, non-blocking fabric becomes the binding constraint on training throughput. Arista's EOS software and its portfolio of 400G/800G switches position it as the primary beneficiary of the 'Ultra Ethernet Consortium' standard gaining share against InfiniBand. Every new hyperscaler AI cluster build is a potential Arista win. Revenue growing 20%+ annually with 35%+ operating margins and a fortress balance sheet. The key risk: NVIDIA InfiniBand holds deep integration advantages in existing Hopper/Blackwell installations, and custom merchant silicon from Broadcom could compress Arista ASPs.
