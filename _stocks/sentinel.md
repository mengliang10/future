## Investment Thesis
SentinelOne is the AI-native endpoint security platform: built from scratch around machine learning, not retrofitted rules engines. Singularity platform ingests all security telemetry, applies AI in real time, and responds autonomously. They are the fastest-growing pure-play cybersecurity company by ARR and are taking enterprise market share from CrowdStrike post-Falcon outage.

### Key Drivers
- **AI-Native Architecture:** No human analyst bottleneck: AI detects and responds in milliseconds.
- **Data Platform Expansion:** Singularity Data Lake is a security data lake competing with Splunk: expanding TAM beyond endpoint.
- **Market Share Opportunity:** CrowdStrike's 2024 outage opened enterprise evaluation cycles; SentinelOne is the primary beneficiary.

### Risk Factors
- **Profitability Timeline:** Investing heavily in growth; path to sustained profitability is multi-year.
- **CrowdStrike Competition:** CRWD has greater brand recognition and a larger partner ecosystem.
