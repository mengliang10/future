Blue Origin is Jeff Bezos's aerospace company, focused on lowering the cost of access to space. Their primary vehicles are New Shepard (suborbital) and New Glenn (orbital heavy-lift). The company is also developing the Blue Moon lunar lander for NASA's Artemis program and the Orbital Reef commercial space station.

Key technical advantages:
- **BE-4 Engines:** Liquid methane/oxygen engines powering New Glenn and ULA's Vulcan rocket.
- **New Glenn Reusability:** Designed for 25+ reflights with vertical landing on a ship.
- **Cislunar Infrastructure:** Integrated focus on lunar landing and long-term space habitats.
