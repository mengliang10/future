## Investment Thesis
Okta is the identity security platform leader: the 'front door' to enterprise applications. As organisations adopt zero-trust architecture, identity verification becomes the primary security control plane. Okta's Workforce Identity and Customer Identity (Auth0) products serve both the employee access and external customer authentication markets. After a difficult 2023–2024 (Okta itself was breached in 2023, damaging trust with CISOs), the company is rebuilding its security posture and customer confidence. Revenue growth has slowed to ~12% as enterprise budgets faced scrutiny; the recovery thesis depends on re-accelerating net retention and new logo adds. Key risk: Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) is free for Microsoft customers and aggressively gaining share in the Microsoft-centric enterprise stack.
