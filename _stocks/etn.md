## Investment Thesis
Eaton is the hidden beneficiary of the AI data centre power surge. As GPU racks scale from 15kW to 100kW+ per rack (GB200 NVL72), power distribution and management infrastructure becomes safety-critical and highly specialised. Eaton's PDUs (power distribution units), UPS systems, and busway products are deployed in every major hyperscaler data centre. The company has a 3-4 year order backlog in data centre electrical equipment. Also exposed to grid modernisation capex driven by EV adoption and renewable integration. Key risk: project timing slippage if data centre builds slow; Vertiv and Schneider Electric compete directly in the same segment.
