## Investment Thesis
Quanta is the largest specialty electrical contractor in North America, building and maintaining the transmission and distribution infrastructure that powers data centres, renewable energy projects, and grid modernisation. Every new hyperscaler data centre requires high-voltage transmission line work: Quanta's core competency. The company has a multi-year backlog driven by utility T&D spend, renewable interconnection, and data centre power delivery. Revenue growing 15%+ organically; margins expanding as skilled labour becomes scarce and projects become more complex. Key risk: weather delays and project timing create quarterly revenue lumpiness. Labour shortages (electricians, linemen) could constrain throughput despite strong demand.
