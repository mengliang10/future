1X (formerly Halodi Robotics) is an OpenAI-backed humanoid robotics company based in Norway. Their primary platforms are "EVE" (wheeled) and "NEO" (bipedal). 1X distinguishes itself with high-torque, low-gear-ratio motors that allow for safe human interaction and compliant physical behavior.

Key technical advantages:
- **Compliance & Safety:** Direct-drive-like actuators allow the robot to be physically pushed and moved by humans without damage.
- **Neo Biped:** Focus on home-use bipedal robots with soft, human-like aesthetics.
- **Learning from Demonstration:** Heavy use of teleoperation and neural network training for task mastery.
