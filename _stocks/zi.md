## Investment Thesis
ZoomInfo is the B2B intelligence platform — who to call, when to call them, what they care about. Their data on 100M+ professionals and 20M companies, enriched with intent signals (who's searching for what right now), turns cold outreach into warm targeting. In a world where AI writes the emails, ZI tells you who to send them to.

### Key Drivers
- **Intent Data Moat:** Proprietary web activity tracking across millions of B2B buying journeys.
- **AI Integration:** Copilot product automates prospecting workflows, increasing seat value.
- **SMB + Enterprise:** Serves both with different product tiers — broad TAM.

### Risk Factors
- **Churn Pressure:** SMB cohorts show elevated churn in difficult macro; enterprise renewal rates are the health metric.
- **Privacy Regulation:** GDPR-equivalent regulations in new markets may restrict data collection.
