## Investment Thesis
Constellation is the largest nuclear power operator in the US — 21 reactors, 10 sites, producing 10% of US carbon-free electricity. The Microsoft deal to restart Three Mile Island Unit 1 (2028) proved that hyperscalers will pay a significant premium for 24/7 carbon-free baseload. Constellation is the most direct listed bet on nuclear renaissance driven by AI power demand.

### Key Drivers
- **Microsoft Power Purchase Agreement:** 20-year PPA for Crane Clean Energy Center (Three Mile Island restart) sets the template for hyperscaler-nuclear partnerships.
- **Existing Fleet Value:** Nuclear plants have sunk costs paid; marginal production cost is $20–30/MWh vs $80+ for gas peakers.
- **Policy Tailwind:** IRA nuclear production tax credits and bipartisan support for nuclear extension.

### Risk Factors
- **Regulatory Licence Extensions:** Each plant requires NRC approval for life extension.
- **Grid Reliability Costs:** Maintenance surprises at ageing plants create one-time cost hits.
