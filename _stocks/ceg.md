## Investment Thesis
Constellation Energy is the largest nuclear operator in the United States. Long-term power purchase agreements with Microsoft (Crane Clean Energy Center, reopened Three Mile Island Unit 1) and negotiations with additional hyperscalers provide revenue visibility at premium pricing ($80-100/MWh). Nuclear capacity is effectively irreplaceable in the near term — no comparable clean baseload comes online for years. Constellation has pricing power on new PPA negotiations that it has not had since deregulation. The risk is regulatory change to the production tax credit framework.
