Tokyo Electron (TEL) is the world's third-largest semiconductor manufacturing equipment supplier. It is the dominant provider of coating/developing equipment for lithography and holds significant market share in etch and deposition tools. TEL is a critical partner for ASML and the leading-edge fabs (TSMC, Samsung, Intel).

Key technical advantages:
- **EUV Coater/Developer:** Near-monopoly on the chemical processing tools that wrap around ASML's EUV lithography machines.
- **Cryogenic Etch:** Leading developer of cryogenic etching for high-aspect-ratio features in 3D NAND and future logic.
- **Advanced Packaging:** Tools for TSMC's CoWoS and other 2.5D/3D integration technologies.
