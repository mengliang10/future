## Investment Thesis
Roblox is the world's largest user-generated virtual world — 88M daily active users spending an average of 2+ hours per day in immersive 3D experiences. The platform is the generator; developers build; Roblox takes 25%. As the generation that grew up in Roblox enters adulthood, the platform's monetisation model matures from virtual goods to digital fashion, concerts, and brand experiences.

### Key Drivers
- **Demographic Tailwind:** Gen Z and Gen Alpha users are aging into higher purchasing power.
- **Developer Economy:** 4M+ creators build on Roblox; top developers earn millions annually.
- **Advertising Platform:** Immersive ads and brand experiences are a new incremental revenue stream.

### Risk Factors
- **Safety Concerns:** Child safety issues and regulatory scrutiny are ongoing risks.
- **Competition:** Fortnite (Epic), Minecraft (Microsoft), and new platforms compete for teenage time.
