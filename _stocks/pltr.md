## Investment Thesis
Palantir is the enterprise AI agent deployment platform for organisations that cannot put their data in a hyperscaler's training pipeline. Defence agencies, intelligence services, and regulated enterprises need AI decision-making on their proprietary, classified, or compliance-sensitive data. Palantir AIP provides exactly this — and the switching costs once a defence customer integrates Palantir into operational decision workflows are enormous. Revenue growth is accelerating, driven by AIP commercial adoption adding to the stable government contract base. The market has historically underestimated how durable Palantir's government revenue is and how large the commercial AIP opportunity is.
