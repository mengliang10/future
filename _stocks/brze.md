## Investment Thesis
Braze orchestrates personalised customer messaging across push, email, SMS, in-app, and web: at the speed and scale legacy marketing clouds cannot match. Their real-time data pipeline architecture is technically superior to Salesforce Marketing Cloud and Adobe Campaign, winning enterprise deals through performance benchmarks.

### Key Drivers
- **Real-Time Personalisation:** Triggers messages within 300ms of user action: a technical moat that legacy vendors cannot easily replicate.
- **Multi-Channel Expansion:** Each new channel (WhatsApp, RCS, OTT) is a new revenue opportunity.
- **AI Layer:** Sage AI optimises send time, channel, and content: improving engagement rates and proving ROI.

### Risk Factors
- **Enterprise Sales Cycles:** Long sales cycles and procurement scrutiny slow revenue growth.
- **Competitive Pressure:** Salesforce, Adobe, and Klaviyo all compete for the same budget.
