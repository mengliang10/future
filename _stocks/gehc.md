## Investment Thesis
GE HealthCare is a global leader in medical imaging and diagnostics. They are aggressively integrating AI into their scanners (MRI, CT) to improve image quality and diagnostic speed. The use of "Synthetic Medical Data" allows them to train more robust and unbiased models, particularly in rare diseases or underrepresented patient populations.
