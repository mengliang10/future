## Investment Thesis
D-Wave is the only commercially operational quantum computer company, running a quantum annealing approach rather than gate-based universal quantum computing. The Advantage2 system (7,000+ qubits) is available via D-Wave's Leap cloud service and has been deployed for logistics optimisation, drug discovery, and financial modelling. Quantum annealing solves a narrower class of problems than universal QC but does so today, not in 2030+. Customers include Volkswagen, USSC, Mastercard, and NTT. Key risk: quantum annealing is a niche approach that may be displaced by gate-based universal QC before it achieves commercial scale. Speculative micro-cap — high volatility, sentiment-driven trading around quantum news.
