## Investment Thesis
ARM Holdings is the royalty model that wins regardless of which chip architecture wins. ARM processor IP is embedded in essentially every smartphone, most data centre servers (growing rapidly), every connected IoT device, and increasingly in AI accelerators and robot controllers. IBM and ARM have a formal collaboration on dual-architecture hardware for AI workloads. NVIDIA Grace CPUs (ARM Neoverse V2) in GB200 NVL72 AI racks means ARM captures a royalty on every AI server sold with Grace. The royalty per chip increases with each architecture generation (ARMv9 carries higher royalties than v8). The risk is RISC-V open-source alternatives gaining momentum — real but slow-moving given ARM's ecosystem depth.
