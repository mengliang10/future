## Investment Thesis
Cameco is the world's largest listed uranium miner — the picks-and-shovels bet on nuclear renaissance. Every new nuclear plant, every SMR deployment, and every reactor life extension requires uranium. Cameco controls Tier 1 assets (Cigar Lake, McArthur River) with decades of reserve life and is the only pure-play uranium major accessible to most investors.

### Key Drivers
- **Uranium Supply Deficit:** Mine supply cannot keep pace with reactor demand; prices must rise to incentivise new mines.
- **Long-Term Contracts:** Cameco locked in multi-year contracts above spot — revenue visibility for 5+ years.
- **Westinghouse Partnership:** Joint ownership of Westinghouse (reactor services) provides exposure to the services market.

### Risk Factors
- **Uranium Price Volatility:** Revenue and earnings are directly tied to uranium spot and contract prices.
- **Mine Execution:** Cigar Lake is the world's highest-grade mine — production disruptions carry outsized impact.
