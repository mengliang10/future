## Investment Thesis
Unity is the engine that builds most of the world's games, simulations, and XR experiences. 70% of the top mobile games run on Unity. Every automotive visualisation, AEC digital twin, and VR training simulation uses Unity. The bet: as the metaverse, XR, and spatial computing expand, Unity's engine becomes the substrate layer: the Windows of virtual worlds.

### Key Drivers
- **Engine Market Share:** Dominant in mobile gaming; expanding in console and PC alongside Unreal.
- **Industry Verticals:** Automotive, film/animation, architecture, and defence simulation all pay premium for Unity Pro/Enterprise.
- **AI Integration:** AI tools for asset generation, animation, and NPC behaviour built into the engine.

### Risk Factors
- **Runtime Fee Controversy:** 2023 runtime fee announcement caused developer backlash; trust is rebuilding.
- **Unreal Competition:** Epic's Unreal Engine 5 is gaining market share in AAA and cinematic use cases.
