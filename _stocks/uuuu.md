## Investment Thesis
Energy Fuels is the largest uranium producer in the US — the only domestic producer with both conventional and ISR mining capacity. More uniquely, they are pivoting into rare earth element (REE) processing: their White Mesa Mill is the only licensed conventional uranium mill in the US and can process monazite sands to extract uranium AND rare earths simultaneously.

### Key Drivers
- **US Uranium Supply Security:** Domestic uranium production is a national security priority; government offtake agreements.
- **Rare Earth Pivot:** Dual uranium-rare earth processing is a structural moat — no comparable facility exists in the US.
- **Vanadium By-Product:** Vanadium for battery storage is a third revenue stream from existing operations.

### Risk Factors
- **Small Cap Volatility:** Sub-$1B market cap amplifies all commodity price moves.
- **REE Processing Complexity:** Rare earth separation chemistry is technically demanding; execution risk is real.
