## Investment Thesis
Cadence provides the software tools (EDA) required to design every complex chip in the world. As AI hardware moves to 2nm, 1.4nm, and beyond, the design complexity increases exponentially, driving higher license value and adoption of AI-driven design tools (JedAI). Cadence is integrating "Agentic RTL Verification" to automate the most time-consuming part of the chip design cycle.
