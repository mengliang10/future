## Investment Thesis

Sierra builds AI agents that handle customer conversations for large brands, and prices them against outcomes such as resolved tickets rather than per seat. That alignment matters because buyers can compare the agent's cost to the human team it replaces. The company's advantage is in evaluation, guardrails and integration with existing systems, not in owning a frontier model.

## Technology Exposure

- Customer-service AI agents
- Outcome-based pricing
- Enterprise integrations and guardrails

## What To Watch

- Resolution rates and escalation cost
- Enterprise expansion within accounts
- Price competition from model providers
