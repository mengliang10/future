## Investment Thesis

Toppan produces photomasks for logic and memory, along with packaging, decorative materials and security products. Its mask business benefits as leading-edge nodes require more mask layers and more complex optical proximity correction. The group's diverse portfolio smooths semiconductor cyclicality.

## Technology Exposure

- Photomasks for advanced nodes
- Packaging and decorative materials
- Security and identification products

## What To Watch

- Leading-edge mask demand
- Mask-shop capacity and pricing
- Non-semiconductor segment performance
