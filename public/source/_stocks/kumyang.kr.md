## Investment Thesis

Kumyang produces semiconductor precursors, specialty chemicals and battery materials, with a customer base among Korean manufacturers. Its precursor business follows memory node transitions, while battery materials add exposure to cell capacity growth. The group's diversified chemicals operations dilute margins.

## Technology Exposure

- Semiconductor precursors
- Battery materials
- Industrial chemicals

## What To Watch

- Precursor qualifications
- Battery-material capacity
- Raw-material prices
