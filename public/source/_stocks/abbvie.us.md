## Investment Thesis

AbbVie sells immunology, oncology, neuroscience and aesthetics products, and has grown its Skyrizi and Rinvoq franchises to offset Humira's patent cliff. It acquired Allergan for Botox and has been adding psychiatry and oncology assets. Execution on new launches has been strong, but leverage is high.

## Technology Exposure

- Immunology medicines
- Neuroscience and psychiatry
- Aesthetics and eye care

## What To Watch

- Growth of new immunology franchises
- Debt and acquisition integration
- Pricing pressure
