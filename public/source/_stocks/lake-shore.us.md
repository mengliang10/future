## Investment Thesis

Lake Shore Cryotronics makes temperature sensors, cryogenic controllers and probe stations used to characterise materials and devices at low temperature. Its instruments are standard equipment in quantum and condensed-matter labs. The company also supplies magnetic-field measurement systems.

## Technology Exposure

- Cryogenic temperature sensors and controllers
- Probe stations
- Magnetic measurement systems

## What To Watch

- Research and quantum-lab demand
- Semiconductor metrology demand
- Competition from larger instrument groups
