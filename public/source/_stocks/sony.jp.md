## Investment Thesis

Sony supplies roughly half of the global CMOS image sensor market and designs the sensors that feed advanced driver assistance and robotics vision systems. Games, music and pictures provide cash flow that funds semiconductor capex. The company's edge is process and packaging know-how in stacked sensors, a niche where scale in consumer electronics compounds.

## Technology Exposure

- CMOS image sensors for phones, cars and robots.
- Entertainment and gaming cash flow.

## What To Watch

- Sensor share in automotive and robotics.
- Capex intensity of new sensor fabs.
