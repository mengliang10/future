## Investment Thesis

Keysight supplies oscilloscopes, signal sources, network analysers and design software, and has built quantum control systems for superconducting and trapped-ion computers. Its instruments are used across the quantum stack, from cryogenic wiring to room-temperature electronics. The company also serves communications, aerospace and semiconductor customers.

## Technology Exposure

- RF and optical test instruments
- Design and simulation software
