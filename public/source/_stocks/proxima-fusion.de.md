Proxima Fusion is a spin-out of the Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics, working on a stellarator design that uses complex twisted magnetic coils to confine plasma steadily.

Stellarators are harder to build than tokamaks but are intrinsically more stable, which matters for continuous power production.

**Why it matters**

- Stellarator power-plant design

- High-temperature superconducting magnets

- Public research heritage
