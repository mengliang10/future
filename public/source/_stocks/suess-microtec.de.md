## Investment Thesis

SUSS MicroTec makes mask aligners, coaters, wafer bonders and inspection tools used in advanced packaging, MEMS and compound semiconductors. Its bonding and coating equipment is directly relevant to hybrid bonding and panel-level packaging. The company is smaller than the large toolmakers but focused on packaging niches.

## Technology Exposure

- Wafer bonding and debonding equipment
- Coaters and mask aligners
- Advanced packaging lithography

## What To Watch

- Hybrid-bonding adoption
- Advanced-packaging capex
- Order lumpiness
