## Investment Thesis

Powertech packages and tests memory devices, including stacked DRAM and modules, and has invested in through-silicon-via and wafer-level packaging for high-bandwidth applications. Its process knowledge in thin-wafer handling and stacking is directly relevant as HBM stacks grow taller. Memory capex and customer concentration drive results.

## Technology Exposure

- Memory packaging and testing
- Through-silicon-via and stacking processes
- Wafer-level packaging

## What To Watch

- HBM packaging capacity
- Memory customer capex
- Yield on advanced stacking
