## Investment Thesis

Kolon Industries produces aramid fibre, specialty films, resins and electronic materials, with applications in aerospace, batteries and displays. Its aramid business is one of the few non-US sources of high-strength fibre for ballistic and composite use. The group also has fashion and chemical businesses.

## Technology Exposure

- Aramid fibre and composites
- Specialty films for displays and batteries
- Resins and industrial materials

## What To Watch

- Aramid capacity and aerospace demand
- Film pricing
- Group debt and restructuring
