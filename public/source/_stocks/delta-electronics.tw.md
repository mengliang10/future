## Investment Thesis

Delta builds power supplies, cooling systems and EV powertrains, with data-centre power becoming its fastest-growing segment as AI racks demand more efficient conversion. Its advantage is efficiency at high wattage, where small losses compound into real electricity cost. It is a quieter beneficiary of AI capex than chipmakers.

## Technology Exposure

- Data-centre power supplies and cooling.
- EV powertrains and industrial automation.

## What To Watch

- Efficiency leadership at high power density.
- Data-centre order growth.
