Wing has operated commercial drone delivery since 2019 and is now part of Alphabet's Other Bets. It delivers food, groceries and pharmacy items from partner retailers in the Dallas-Fort Worth area and Canberra.

Its aircraft hover, transition to fixed-wing flight and lower packages on a tether, with remote operations managed from a central control room.

**Why it matters**

- Tether-based package delivery

- Retail partnerships in the US and Australia

- Remote fleet operations
