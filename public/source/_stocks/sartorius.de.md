## Investment Thesis

Sartorius sells bioreactors, filters, bags and laboratory balances used to develop and manufacture biologics. Its bioprocessing consumables scale with biologic output, and its lab business follows pharma research spending. The company invested heavily during the pandemic boom and has since faced a demand normalisation.

## Technology Exposure

- Bioprocessing consumables
- Bioreactors and filtration systems
- Laboratory instruments

## What To Watch

- Biologic production volumes
- Inventory normalisation at customers
- China demand
