## Investment Thesis

Stevanato produces glass primary packaging, syringes and automated inspection machines for pharmaceutical fill-finish lines. Its glass converting and device capabilities serve biologics and GLP-1 customers, and it is expanding US capacity. Utilisation of new plants is the near-term issue.

## Technology Exposure

- Glass vials and syringes
- Inspection and assembly machines
- Drug-delivery devices

## What To Watch

- Capacity ramp
- Biologic and GLP-1 demand
- Energy costs in Europe
