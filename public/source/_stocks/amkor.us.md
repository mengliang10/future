## Investment Thesis

Amkor is the largest US-listed outsourced packager, with a broad assembly and test footprint in Asia and a new Arizona plant intended to co-locate advanced packaging with US fabs. It serves automotive, communications and computing customers, and its advanced-packaging business is the growth engine as packages become more complex. Customer concentration and capex timing are the main risks.

## Technology Exposure

- Advanced packaging and system-in-package
- Automotive and industrial assembly and test
- US advanced packaging capacity

## What To Watch

- Arizona plant ramp and customer commitments
- Advanced packaging revenue mix
- Automotive demand
