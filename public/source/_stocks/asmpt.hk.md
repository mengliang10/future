## Investment Thesis

ASMPT supplies die-attach, wire-bonding, thermocompression and surface-mount equipment, and its advanced packaging tools are used for flip-chip and chiplet assembly. It is one of the few equipment vendors covering both mainstream and advanced packaging, giving it a wide view of package roadmaps. The company has been investing in hybrid bonding and panel-level tools.

## Technology Exposure

- Thermocompression and die-attach bonders
- Wire-bonding and surface-mount equipment
- Advanced packaging and hybrid bonding tools

## What To Watch

- Hybrid-bonding adoption
- Advanced packaging capex
- SMT demand cycle
