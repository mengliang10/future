## Investment Thesis

Nikon builds lithography scanners, inspection systems and precision optics, and also makes cameras and medical equipment. It competes with ASML and Canon in lithography, with strength in older-node and specialty tools, and supplies optical components for advanced manufacturing. The company has been restructuring its camera and industrial businesses.

## Technology Exposure

- Lithography scanners
- Precision optics and encoders
- Cameras and medical systems

## What To Watch

- Lithography order recovery
- Advanced packaging lithography demand
- Restructuring progress
