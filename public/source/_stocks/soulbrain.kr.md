## Investment Thesis

Soulbrain supplies wet chemicals, etchants and precursors to Korean semiconductor and display manufacturers. It has been expanding into high-purity materials for advanced memory nodes, where trace-metal control is critical. Revenue is concentrated among a few large customers.

## Technology Exposure

- Wet chemicals and etchants
- Precursors and specialty materials
- Display process chemicals

## What To Watch

- Advanced-node qualification
- Korean memory capex
- Customer concentration
