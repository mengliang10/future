## Investment Thesis

WuXi Biologics makes antibody and biologics drugs for global pharma under contract, with a large share of revenue from North American clients. A US bill targeting certain Chinese biotech suppliers passed the House in 2024 but was not enacted, and the threat alone has pushed clients to dual-source. The company's capabilities are real; its valuation is a referendum on legislative risk.

## Technology Exposure

- Biologics drug substance and drug product manufacturing.
- Antibody discovery and development services.

## What To Watch

- US legislative action and client dual-sourcing.
- Utilisation of new capacity in Europe and Asia.
