## Investment Thesis

Quantinuum builds trapped-ion quantum computers and develops quantum software and cybersecurity products, with Honeywell as majority owner. Trapped ions offer high gate fidelity and all-to-all connectivity, and the company has published repeated improvements in error rates and qubit count. Its commercial model combines hardware access with software and encryption services.

## Technology Exposure

- Trapped-ion quantum processors
- Quantum software and compilers
- Post-quantum cryptography services

## What To Watch

- Logical-qubit demonstrations
- Error-correction progress
- Hardware access revenue
