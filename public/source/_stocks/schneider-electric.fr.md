## Investment Thesis

Schneider sells switchgear, uninterruptible power supplies and cooling systems, the equipment that connects AI racks to the grid. Data-centre orders have grown faster than the rest of the portfolio, and the company is expanding capacity in transformers and switchgear, both of which are in short supply globally. Grid interconnection queues are now its main constraint.

## Technology Exposure

- Data-centre power and cooling.
- Grid automation and electrical distribution.

## What To Watch

- Order backlog in data centres.
- Transformer and switchgear capacity expansion.
