## Investment Thesis

Advantest builds automated test equipment for logic, memory and system-level test, including the high-pin-count testers used for AI accelerators and HBM. Test time and pin count rise with package complexity, so AI demand increases tester content per device. The company also supplies test handlers and measurement instruments.

## Technology Exposure

- Automated test equipment
- Memory and HBM test
- System-level test and handlers

## What To Watch

- AI accelerator test demand
- Memory test capacity
- Cyclical order swings
