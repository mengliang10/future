## Investment Thesis

TE Connectivity makes connectors, relays, sensors and cable assemblies for vehicles, aircraft, satellites and data centres. Its aerospace and defence business supplies harsh-environment interconnects used in launch and satellite systems. The company is also a major supplier of high-speed connectors for AI data-centre hardware.

## Technology Exposure

- Aerospace and defence connectors
- High-speed data-centre interconnects
- Automotive sensors and connectors
