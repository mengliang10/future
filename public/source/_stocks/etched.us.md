Etched designs an ASIC dedicated to transformer inference rather than a general-purpose GPU. The company argues that hard-wiring the attention and feed-forward patterns buys a large efficiency gain.

The trade-off is flexibility: a transformer-specific chip must track model architecture changes, and the company is betting transformers remain dominant.

**Why it matters**

- Transformer-only inference ASIC

- High throughput per watt target

- Server-level deployment
