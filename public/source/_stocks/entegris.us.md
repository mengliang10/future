## Investment Thesis

Entegris makes filtration systems, high-purity containers, specialty coatings, CMP slurries and deposition materials that keep contamination out of advanced processes. Its products are consumable and specified into fab recipes, so revenue follows wafer starts and node complexity. The acquisition of CMC Materials added CMP slurries and pads.

## Technology Exposure

- Filtration and contamination control
- CMP slurries and pads
- Materials handling and specialty coatings

## What To Watch

- Fab utilisation
- CMP and filtration content growth per node
- Integration of acquired businesses
