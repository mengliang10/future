## Investment Thesis

L&F makes cathode active materials for lithium-ion batteries, with a focus on high-nickel products and a customer base among Korean cell makers. It has been expanding capacity and developing precursors and recycling to improve cost control. The business is exposed to EV demand and metal prices.

## Technology Exposure

- Cathode active materials
- Precursor development
- Battery recycling

## What To Watch

- EV demand
- Cathode pricing and metal costs
- Capacity utilisation
