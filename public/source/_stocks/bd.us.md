## Investment Thesis

Becton Dickinson makes injection devices, infusion pumps, vascular access products and diagnostic systems for hospitals and labs. Its syringes and prefillable devices serve vaccine and biologic manufacturers, and its diagnostic arm covers infectious disease and cancer. Hospital capital budgets and procedure volumes drive results.

## Technology Exposure

- Prefillable syringes and injection devices
- Infusion and vascular access
- Diagnostic systems

## What To Watch

- Hospital capital spending
- Procedure volumes
- Portfolio separation
