## Investment Thesis

Intellia develops in-vivo CRISPR therapies delivered by lipid nanoparticles, aiming to edit genes directly inside the body for transthyretin amyloidosis and hereditary angioedema. It also works on ex-vivo cell therapies. Safety data from systemic editing are the critical milestone for the field.

## Technology Exposure

- In-vivo CRISPR therapies
- Lipid-nanoparticle delivery
- Ex-vivo cell engineering

## What To Watch

- Regulatory path for in-vivo editing
