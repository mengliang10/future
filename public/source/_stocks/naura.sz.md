## Investment Thesis

NAURA supplies etch, deposition, cleaning and furnace equipment to Chinese fabs. As US, Japanese and Dutch toolmakers restricted advanced exports, domestic fabs were pushed toward local suppliers, and NAURA became the largest of them. The company's growth is therefore a direct function of Chinese capex and of how quickly its tools qualify at advanced nodes.

## Technology Exposure

- Etch and deposition tools for mature and advanced fabs.
- Domestic replacement of restricted imports.

## What To Watch

- Qualification progress at leading-edge fabs.
- Order growth versus Chinese fab capex cycle.
