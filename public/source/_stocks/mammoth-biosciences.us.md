## Investment Thesis

Mammoth Biosciences works on compact Cas enzymes that can be delivered more easily into tissues, and on CRISPR-based diagnostics. Its smaller editors may fit into viral vectors that full-size Cas9 cannot. The company partners with larger pharma for delivery and development.

- CRISPR diagnostics
