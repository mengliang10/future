## Investment Thesis

Perplexity replaced the ten-blue-links result page with a cited answer and a follow-up conversation, and sells subscriptions plus a developer API for its Sonar models. The product depends on live web retrieval rather than training data alone, which keeps answers current and makes the search index a strategic asset. Competition from Google's AI overviews and the general assistants is the central risk, and publisher licensing has become a cost of doing business.

## Technology Exposure

- Sonar models and search API
- Consumer and enterprise subscriptions
- Commerce and advertising experiments

## What To Watch

- Publisher licensing and copyright litigation
- Retention against free assistant answers
- Cost per answered query
