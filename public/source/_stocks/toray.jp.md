## Investment Thesis

Toray makes carbon fibre, specialty films, battery separator coatings, water-treatment membranes and engineering plastics. Its carbon fibre is used in aerospace, pressure vessels and launch vehicles, while films serve displays and electronics. The company is a key supplier to both space and electrification supply chains.

## Technology Exposure

- Carbon fibre and composites
- Battery separator and electrode materials
- Films and engineering plastics

## What To Watch

- Aerospace and launch demand
- Carbon-fibre pricing
- Battery-materials growth
