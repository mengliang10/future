## Investment Thesis

Enplas produces IC test sockets, sockets for burn-in and precision plastic components, with customers among chipmakers and test houses. Its sockets must handle fine-pitch packages and high frequencies, and it has been developing sockets for advanced packaging. The company also makes optical and automotive components.

## Technology Exposure

- IC test sockets and burn-in sockets
- Precision engineering plastics
- Optical components

## What To Watch

- Advanced-packaging test demand
- Handset and automotive demand
- Competition from Korean and Taiwanese suppliers
