Lyten manufactures a 3D graphene material and uses it in lithium-sulfur batteries, which avoid nickel and cobalt and can be much lighter than lithium-ion.

The company also sells graphene-enhanced composites and sensors, giving it revenue beyond the battery programme.

**Why it matters**

- Lithium-sulfur cells

- 3D graphene manufacturing

- Composites and sensors
