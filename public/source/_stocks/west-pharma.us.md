## Investment Thesis

West Pharmaceutical makes elastomer stoppers, seals and self-injection devices used to package and deliver injectable drugs. Its components must be compatible with biologics, and qualification is tied to the drug's regulatory filing. GLP-1 pens and biologics growth drive demand.

## Technology Exposure

- Elastomer seals and stoppers
- Self-injection devices
- Containment and delivery systems

## What To Watch

- Biologic and GLP-1 volumes
- Device programme wins
- Customer inventory cycles
