## Investment Thesis

Safran co-produces the LEAP engine with GE through CFM, which powers a large share of new narrowbody aircraft. Engines are sold at modest margin and earn decades of aftermarket revenue, so the installed fleet is the real asset. Supply-chain bottlenecks in castings and forgings limit the delivery ramp.

## Technology Exposure

- LEAP engines and aftermarket services.
- Defence propulsion and space equipment.

## What To Watch

- Engine delivery rates.
- Aftermarket pricing and shop-visit capacity.
