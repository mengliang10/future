## Investment Thesis

LG Chem produces cathode materials, battery precursors, specialty polymers and advanced materials alongside its petrochemical business. Its battery-materials arm supplies the wider LG battery chain and external customers, while the chemicals business remains cyclical. The company is investing in US and Korean cathode capacity.

## Technology Exposure

- Cathode active materials and precursors
- Specialty polymers and films
- Petrochemicals and industrial materials

## What To Watch

- Cathode capacity ramp
- Metal prices and pass-through contracts
- Petrochemical spreads
