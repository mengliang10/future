## Investment Thesis

Sarvam trains models for India's language diversity, where English-centric assistants fail on script, code-switching and dialect, and pairs them with voice interfaces for low-literacy users. Government-backed programmes and Indian enterprise deployments are the initial market. The bet is that localisation is a moat that global labs under-serve.

## Technology Exposure

- Indic language models
- Speech recognition and synthesis
- Public-service and enterprise deployments

## What To Watch

- Government contract conversion
- Funding against compute costs
- Coverage and quality across many languages
