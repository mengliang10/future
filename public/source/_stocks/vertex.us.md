## Investment Thesis

Vertex sells cystic fibrosis modulators that treat most patients with the disease, and is developing non-opioid pain drugs, gene-edited therapies and an encapsulated cell therapy for type 1 diabetes. Its CF franchise funds expansion, but growth depends on successful launches beyond CF. Pricing pressure and trial failures are the main risks.

## Technology Exposure

- Cystic fibrosis modulators
- Non-opioid pain medicines
- Cell and gene therapies for diabetes

## What To Watch

- Pain programme approval
- CF franchise competition
- Cell-therapy trial results
