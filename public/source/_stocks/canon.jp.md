## Investment Thesis

Canon makes lithography scanners, nanoimprint systems, cameras, printers and medical equipment. Its nanoimprint technology offers a non-optical route to fine patterning, and it continues to supply mature-node lithography tools. The company's diverse portfolio dilutes semiconductor cyclicality.

## Technology Exposure

- Lithography scanners
- Nanoimprint lithography
- Cameras, printers and medical systems

## What To Watch

- Nanoimprint adoption
- Mature-node lithography demand
- Office-equipment market
