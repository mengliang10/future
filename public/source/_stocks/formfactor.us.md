## Investment Thesis

FormFactor supplies probe cards used to test wafers and known-good dies, and cryogenic systems used in quantum-computing research and physics. Its probe cards must contact ever-finer pads on advanced packages, and its cryostats are part of the quantum-computing supply chain. Both segments benefit from rising test complexity.

## Technology Exposure

- Probe cards for wafer and die test
- Cryogenic test systems
- Measurement systems

## What To Watch

- Advanced-packaging test complexity
- Quantum-computing demand
- Probe-card competition
