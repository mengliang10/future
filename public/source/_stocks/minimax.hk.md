## Investment Thesis

MiniMax combines consumer applications with an API business built on its own M-series models, which gives it revenue outside enterprise contracts. Its video and audio generation tools have been the most visible products, and the listing in Hong Kong gave it capital to keep training at frontier scale. The share price has been extremely volatile since listing, reflecting how quickly the market re-prices Chinese model economics.

## Technology Exposure

- M-series foundation models
- Consumer apps and subscriptions
- Video and audio generation APIs

## What To Watch

- Consumer retention and paying conversion
- Model price competition
- Post-listing cash burn
