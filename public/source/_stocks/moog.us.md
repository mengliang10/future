## Investment Thesis

Moog produces actuators, servo valves and motion-control systems for aircraft, satellites, launch vehicles and industrial machinery. Its components steer thrust vector control and deploy structures, making it a quiet supplier to launch programmes. Defence and space demand has been growing faster than commercial aircraft.

## Technology Exposure

- Thrust-vector and flight-control actuation
- Satellite mechanisms and components
- Industrial motion control

## What To Watch

- Launch and satellite demand
- Defence budgets
- Commercial aircraft production
