## Investment Thesis

United Launch Alliance provides assured access to space for US government missions with Atlas V, Delta IV Heavy and the new Vulcan Centaur. Vulcan uses Blue Origin BE-4 engines, a dependency that has shaped its schedule. ULA is being reshaped as national-security launch moves to competitive awards.

## Technology Exposure

- Vulcan Centaur launch vehicle
- Atlas V and Delta IV missions
- National-security launch services

## What To Watch

- Vulcan cadence and certification
- BE-4 engine supply
- Ownership transition
