## Investment Thesis

Technoprobe designs and builds probe cards for wafer-level and final test, with a focus on high-pin-count, fine-pitch applications such as processors and advanced packaging. It has been expanding into MEMS probes and testing for chiplets. Customer concentration among large chipmakers is significant.

## Technology Exposure

- Probe cards for wafer test
- MEMS probe technology
- Advanced packaging test

## What To Watch

- Leading-edge chip test demand
- Customer concentration
- Capacity expansion
