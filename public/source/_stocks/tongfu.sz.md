## Investment Thesis

Tongfu Microelectronics assembles and tests processors and other chips, with a substantial portion of revenue tied to AMD through a joint venture in China. It has been expanding advanced packaging capability for domestic and multinational customers, supported by China's localisation agenda. Customer concentration is high and tool access is a constraint.

## Technology Exposure

- Flip-chip and advanced assembly
- Processor and graphics assembly and test
- Domestic packaging capacity

## What To Watch

- AMD programme volumes
- Advanced packaging qualification
- Export-control exposure
