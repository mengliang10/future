## Investment Thesis

Kureha produces polyvinylidene fluoride binders, specialty resins and pharmaceutical materials, with battery binders becoming a growth driver. PVDF binder holds electrode particles together and is sensitive to purity and consistency. The company also makes carbon products and agrochemicals.

## Technology Exposure

- PVDF battery binders
- Specialty resins and films
- Pharmaceutical and agrochemical materials

## What To Watch

- Battery-binder demand
- PVDF pricing and regulation
- Specialty chemicals margins
