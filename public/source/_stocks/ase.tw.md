## Investment Thesis

ASE assembles and tests a large share of the world's chips and has pushed furthest into advanced packaging with its VIPack platform, fan-out and panel-level processes. As chipmakers move to chiplets and 2.5D integration, the OSAT role shifts from commodity assembly toward co-design of the package. ASE's scale lets it absorb capex that smaller packagers cannot.

## Technology Exposure

- Advanced packaging, fan-out and 2.5D integration
- Wire-bond and flip-chip assembly
- Final test and system-in-package

## What To Watch

- Panel-level packaging ramp
- Advanced packaging share of revenue
- Utilisation through the handset cycle
