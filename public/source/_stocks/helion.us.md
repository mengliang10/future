## Investment Thesis

Helion Energy is developing a compact fusion reactor using field-reversed configuration and direct energy recovery, with a power-purchase agreement with Microsoft for a future plant. Its approach targets electricity directly rather than steam turbines. Technical milestones and funding determine credibility.
