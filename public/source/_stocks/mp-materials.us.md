## Investment Thesis

MP Materials mines and processes rare earths at Mountain Pass, California, and is building magnet manufacturing in the US with automotive and defence customers. It is the most visible attempt to rebuild a domestic rare-earth-to-magnet chain. Ramp-up, pricing and policy support determine the outcome.

## Technology Exposure

- Rare-earth concentrate and oxides
- Neodymium-iron-boron magnets
- US magnet manufacturing

## What To Watch

- Magnet plant ramp
- Rare-earth pricing
- Government offtake and funding
