## Investment Thesis

NRG Energy is one of the largest retail energy providers and independent power producers in the US, with significant generation presence in ERCOT and PJM. As data center expansion drives structural growth in electricity demand, power producers with existing baseload and flexible generation assets command substantial pricing power and capacity contract premiums.
