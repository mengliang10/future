## Investment Thesis

DeepSeek released open-weight models in late 2024 and a reasoning model in January 2025 that matched leading US systems on several benchmarks at a fraction of the reported training spend. Its published V3 figures describe roughly 2.8 million H800 GPU-hours, about US$5.6m of compute at market rates. The models are downloadable, which makes the cost curve a competitive fact rather than a vendor claim.

## Technology Exposure

- Open-weight reasoning and coding models.
- Cost-efficient training and inference recipes.

## What To Watch

- Whether reported training costs include the full research programme.
- Sustained model quality without access to the newest accelerators.
