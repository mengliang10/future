## Investment Thesis

Urenco provides uranium enrichment services from plants in the UK, Netherlands and Germany, and is expanding capacity as Western utilities diversify away from Russian supply. It also produces medical isotopes. Ownership is split among governments and utilities.

## Technology Exposure

- Uranium enrichment
- Fuel-cycle services
- Medical isotopes

## What To Watch

- Enrichment capacity additions
- Utility contract demand
- Energy costs
