## Investment Thesis

NAVER runs the dominant search portal in Korea and has invested in HyperCLOVA X, a Korean-language model positioned as national AI infrastructure. The strategic argument is linguistic and regulatory: Korean-language quality and domestic data residency are things global models do not optimise for. Execution risk sits in the cost of training and in the governance of its Japanese messaging affiliate.

## Technology Exposure

- Korean-language foundation models.
- Search, commerce and cloud infrastructure.

## What To Watch

- Enterprise adoption of HyperCLOVA X.
- Cost of compute without domestic accelerator supply.
