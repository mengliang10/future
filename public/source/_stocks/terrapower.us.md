## Investment Thesis

TerraPower is developing the Natrium sodium-cooled fast reactor with molten-salt energy storage, and has begun construction of a demonstration plant in Wyoming. It also works on fuel fabrication and medical isotopes. Regulatory approval and cost control are the main hurdles.

## Technology Exposure

- Sodium fast reactor
- Molten-salt storage
- Advanced fuel development

## What To Watch

- Demonstration plant construction
- NRC licensing
- Cost and schedule
