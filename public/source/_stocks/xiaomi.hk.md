## Investment Thesis

Xiaomi sells phones, appliances and, since 2024, cars, using a single brand and retail footprint to cross-sell. It shipped its first sedan in 2024 and an SUV in 2025, and in May 2025 announced a self-designed 3nm application processor. That combination — consumer scale, automotive entry and in-house chips — is unusual outside China, and it makes Xiaomi the clearest test of whether device margins can fund a car business.

## Technology Exposure

- Connected devices and on-device AI.
- EVs and in-house SoC design.

## What To Watch

- Vehicle gross margin before scale credits.
- Whether in-house silicon reduces cost or just dependency.
