## Investment Thesis

Orano mines uranium, converts and enriches it, and handles fuel fabrication and recycling. Its enrichment and recycling capacity make it one of the few non-Russian fuel-cycle suppliers in the West. The company is state-controlled and central to European fuel-security policy.

## Technology Exposure

- Uranium mining
- Conversion and enrichment
- Fuel recycling

## What To Watch

- Enrichment capacity expansion
- European fuel-security policy
- Mining project performance
