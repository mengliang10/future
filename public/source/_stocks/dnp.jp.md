## Investment Thesis

Dai Nippon Printing makes photomasks for semiconductor and display lithography, along with optical films, battery pouches and packaging. Its mask business serves leading-edge logic and memory customers, where defect control is critical. The broader portfolio is being reshaped toward electronics materials.

## Technology Exposure

- Photomasks and imprint templates
- Optical films for displays
- Battery pouches and packaging

## What To Watch

- Advanced mask demand
- Display film pricing
- Portfolio restructuring
