## Investment Thesis

Sanofi sells vaccines, immunology medicines, rare-disease therapies and consumer health products, and has been spinning off consumer health to focus on innovative biopharma. Its Dupixent franchise is a major growth driver, and it is expanding in vaccines and neurology. Pipeline setbacks have weighed on sentiment.

## Technology Exposure

- Immunology and inflammation
- Vaccines
- Rare disease and neurology

## What To Watch

- Dupixent growth and competition
- Vaccine demand
- Pipeline execution
