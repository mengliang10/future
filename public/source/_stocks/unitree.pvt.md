## Investment Thesis

Unitree sells quadruped robots and humanoids at prices that undercut Western and Japanese alternatives by an order of magnitude, with its G1 humanoid launched at roughly US$16,000. The company began a domestic listing process in 2025. Its robots are widely used in research labs, which makes it the de facto hardware standard for embodied-AI experiments outside the largest corporate labs.

## Technology Exposure

- Affordable humanoid and quadruped platforms for research.
- Actuators and control stacks for embodied AI.

## What To Watch

- Durability and safety in commercial deployment.
- Whether low prices survive component and tariff pressure.
