## Investment Thesis

Atom Computing builds neutral-atom quantum computers with large arrays of strontium atoms addressed by lasers. Its approach targets scale through atom-array size and reconfigurable connectivity, and the company has demonstrated arrays of hundreds of atoms. Error correction and system reliability remain the development focus.

## Technology Exposure

- Neutral-atom quantum processors
- Laser and optical control systems
- Atom-array technology

## What To Watch

- Error-correction demonstrations
- Array scaling
- Commercial access agreements
