## Investment Thesis

Hesai ships lidar sensors for robotaxis, ADAS and robotics, and has held the largest share of the automotive lidar market by revenue. Chinese automakers adopted lidar faster than US peers, giving Hesai volume and cost advantages, while US policy pressure and litigation have created uncertainty around its access to Western customers. Cost per channel, not range claims, is the metric that decides adoption.

## Technology Exposure

- Lidar for ADAS and robotaxi fleets.
- Solid-state sensor designs for mass-market vehicles.

## What To Watch

- Gross margin as prices fall.
- Access to US and European vehicle programmes.
