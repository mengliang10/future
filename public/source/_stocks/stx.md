## Investment Thesis

Seagate sells nearline drives for cloud and enterprise storage, where cost per terabyte still beats flash for bulk data. Its Mozaic platform uses heat-assisted magnetic recording to raise areal density, and qualification of high-capacity drives at hyperscalers drives the mix. The business is duopolistic with Western Digital, cyclical in pricing, and increasingly leveraged to AI data retention rather than PCs.

## Technology Exposure

- Nearline HDDs for cloud and AI storage
- HAMR technology and media
- Consumer and edge drives

## What To Watch

- HAMR volume ramp and yields
- Hyperscaler qualification and pricing
- NAND cost competition in warm tiers
