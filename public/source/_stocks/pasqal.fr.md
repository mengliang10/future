## Investment Thesis

Pasqal builds neutral-atom quantum processors, an approach that scales by trapping more atoms in optical tweezers. It has demonstrated arrays above a thousand atoms and sells cloud access and on-premises systems, with strong backing from French public programmes. Neutral atoms are promising for simulation, less proven for universal computation.

## Technology Exposure

- Neutral-atom processors.
- Quantum simulation for chemistry and materials.

## What To Watch

- Atom-array scaling and fidelity.
- Conversion of pilots into paid systems.
