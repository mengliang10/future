## Investment Thesis

Jacobs provides engineering, design and programme-management services for nuclear plants, semiconductor fabs, water systems and government facilities. Its nuclear work covers new-build support, decommissioning and fuel-cycle facilities. The business is people-intensive and tied to government and industrial capex.

## Technology Exposure

- Nuclear engineering and programme management
- Advanced manufacturing facility design
- Water and environmental engineering

## What To Watch

- Government nuclear budgets
- Semiconductor fab construction
- Contract margins
