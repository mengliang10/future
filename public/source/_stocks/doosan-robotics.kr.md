## Investment Thesis

Doosan Robotics listed in 2023 and sells collaborative arms used in manufacturing, food service and logistics. It is a smaller player than Fanuc or Universal Robots, and its strategy is to pair hardware with application software for tasks that traditional industrial robots cannot handle. Revenue remains modest relative to valuation, which reflects expectations about embodied AI rather than current orders.

## Technology Exposure

- Collaborative robot arms.
- Application software for service automation.

## What To Watch

- Order intake outside Korea.
- Whether AI features translate into paid deployments.
