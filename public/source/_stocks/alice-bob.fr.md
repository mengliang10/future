## Investment Thesis

Alice & Bob designs superconducting cat qubits that are intrinsically biased toward bit-flip protection, aiming to reduce error-correction overhead. The approach trades more complex hardware for simpler codes, and the company has published progress on cat-qubit lifetimes. It is one of several European quantum-hardware startups betting on a differentiated qubit modality.

## Technology Exposure

- Cat-qubit processors
- Quantum error-correction design
- Cryogenic hardware integration

## What To Watch

- Cat-qubit fidelity and lifetime
- Error-correction overhead reduction
- Funding and partnerships
