## Investment Thesis

WuXi AppTec provides discovery chemistry, preclinical testing, clinical development and manufacturing services to pharmaceutical and biotech customers worldwide. Its integrated platform shortens drug development timelines, and its low-cost chemistry capacity made it a default outsourcing partner. Geopolitical restrictions on Chinese biotech services are the central risk.

## Technology Exposure

- Discovery chemistry and preclinical services
- Clinical development
- Small-molecule and biologics manufacturing

## What To Watch

- US policy on Chinese biotech services
- Customer diversification
- Capacity utilisation
