## Investment Thesis

Hyundai Engineering & Construction builds nuclear power plants, oil and gas facilities, and infrastructure, and is part of the Korean consortium that exports APR-1400 reactors. Its nuclear experience is a differentiator in emerging-market bids. Construction margins and overseas awards drive results.
