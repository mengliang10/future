## Investment Thesis

Zeon produces specialty elastomers, battery binders, cyclo-olefin polymers and optical films. Its binders and dispersants are used in battery electrodes, while its transparent polymers serve displays and medical devices. The company has been expanding battery-materials capacity outside Japan.

## Technology Exposure

- Battery binders and dispersants
- Cyclo-olefin polymers and optical films
- Synthetic rubber and specialty elastomers

## What To Watch

- Battery-binder demand
- Optical-film pricing
- Raw-material costs
