## Investment Thesis

Mitsubishi Materials makes sputtering targets, semiconductor packaging materials, ceramic components and cutting tools. Its electronic-materials business supplies deposition targets and heat-dissipation parts for chips and power modules. Mining, cement and metals recycling add cyclicality.

## Technology Exposure

- Sputtering targets and electronic materials
- Ceramic and heat-dissipation components
- Cemented carbide tools and metals

## What To Watch

- Target demand and pricing
- Power-module materials
- Metal price swings
