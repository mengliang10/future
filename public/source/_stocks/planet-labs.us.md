Planet operates hundreds of small satellites that image the Earth daily, selling imagery and analytics to governments, agriculture, insurance and defence customers.

Its value is temporal coverage: seeing the same field, port or construction site every day rather than once a year.

**Why it matters**

- Daily global imaging

- Government and defence contracts

- Analytics on top of imagery
