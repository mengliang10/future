Astranis builds small satellites for geostationary orbit, aiming to deliver broadband capacity at a fraction of the cost of traditional large GEO platforms.

It sells dedicated capacity to telecom operators and governments, particularly in regions underserved by fibre.

- Small GEO satellites

- Dedicated broadband capacity
