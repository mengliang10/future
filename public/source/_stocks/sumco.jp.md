## Investment Thesis

SUMCO makes the polished and epitaxial silicon wafers on which nearly every chip is built, and its fortunes track global wafer starts and pricing. Long-term contracts cover much of its volume, but spot pricing and capacity additions still swing margins. The company is investing in larger-diameter and higher-quality wafers for leading-edge nodes.

## Technology Exposure

- 300mm polished and epitaxial wafers
- 200mm and smaller wafers
- Wafer reclaim and specialty substrates

## What To Watch

- Wafer contract pricing
- Fab utilisation and inventory
- Capacity additions across the industry
