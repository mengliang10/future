## Investment Thesis

Infineon makes power semiconductors — silicon carbide and silicon — used in vehicles, industrial drives and data-centre power supplies. Electrification raised content per vehicle, and AI racks raised content per server, both favourable trends. Chinese competition in mature power devices and cyclical automotive demand are the constraints.

## Technology Exposure

- SiC and IGBT power devices.
- Power management for data centres and renewables.

## What To Watch

- SiC pricing pressure from Chinese suppliers.
- Automotive production volumes in Europe.
