## Investment Thesis

Infleqtion builds neutral-atom quantum computers, atomic clocks and quantum sensors, using laser-cooled atoms as qubits and as precision instruments. Its portfolio spans computing and sensing, which broadens commercial exposure beyond gate-model quantum computing. The company has supplied clocks and sensors to government and defence customers.

## Technology Exposure

- Neutral-atom quantum processors
- Optical atomic clocks
- Quantum sensors and RF receivers
