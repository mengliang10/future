## Investment Thesis

First Light Fusion uses high-velocity projectiles to implode fuel targets, aiming for a simpler and cheaper route to fusion than lasers. It has demonstrated fusion reactions and is developing a pilot plant design. The approach remains unproven at power-plant scale.

- Projectile fusion
- Target design
- Pilot-plant development
