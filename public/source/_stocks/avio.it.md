## Investment Thesis

Avio produces solid rocket motors, liquid propulsion and the Vega launch vehicle, and supplies boosters and stages to Ariane 6. Its solid-propulsion expertise is also relevant to defence applications. Revenue depends on European institutional launch programmes and their cadence.

## Technology Exposure

- Vega launch vehicle
- Solid rocket motors and boosters
- Liquid propulsion and tactical systems

## What To Watch

- Ariane 6 and Vega cadence
- European Space Agency funding
- Defence propulsion demand
