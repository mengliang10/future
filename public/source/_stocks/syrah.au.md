## Investment Thesis

Syrah Resources mines graphite at Balama in Mozambique and produces anode material at its Vidalia plant in Louisiana. Natural-graphite anode supply is dominated by China, so a non-Chinese integrated source is strategically valuable to US battery supply chains. Ramp-up and financing have been the main risks.

## Technology Exposure

- Natural graphite concentrate
- Anode active material
- US anode capacity

## What To Watch

- Vidalia ramp and customer qualification
- Graphite pricing
- Financing and ownership changes
