## Investment Thesis

Outokumpu makes stainless and specialty steels, including the austenitic grades used in cryogenic tanks and rocket structures. Stainless steel is central to reusable launch vehicle designs because it is strong, weldable and cheap at cryogenic temperatures. The company's results remain tied to European industrial demand and alloy surcharges.

## Technology Exposure

- Austenitic and specialty stainless steel
- Cryogenic-grade plate and sheet
- Ferrochrome and alloy inputs

## What To Watch

- European industrial demand
- Nickel and alloy surcharges
- Energy costs
