## Investment Thesis

SAP's enterprise resource planning systems hold the operational data of large companies, which makes it a natural distribution channel for AI agents that act on invoices, supply chains and finance. Its cloud migration programme converted a licence business into recurring revenue. The risk is that AI reduces the number of seats it can charge for.

## Technology Exposure

- ERP and business AI agents.
- Cloud migration and sovereign cloud offerings.

## What To Watch

- Cloud backlog growth.
- Seat-based pricing under agentic automation.
