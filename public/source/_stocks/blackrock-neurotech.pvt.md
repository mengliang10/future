## Investment Thesis

Blackrock Neurotech's Utah array is the electrode used in most of the long-running human BCI research that established cursor control, robotic-arm use and speech decoding. The company sells research systems and is developing the MoveAgain platform for clinical use, with a decade of human safety data behind the array. Its challenge is that the Utah array is rigid and limited in channel count, while newer flexible arrays promise more.

## Technology Exposure

- Utah array electrodes and research systems
- MoveAgain clinical platform
- Neural signal processing hardware

## What To Watch

- Clinical approval for MoveAgain
- Competition from flexible high-channel arrays
- Grant and research funding dependence
