## Investment Thesis

Johnson Matthey makes catalysts for automotive and chemical processes, and supplies precious-metal materials and hydrogen fuel-cell components. Its expertise in platinum-group metals is relevant to electrolysers, fuel cells and emissions control. The company has been reshaping its portfolio toward higher-growth clean-air and hydrogen businesses.

## Technology Exposure

- Catalysts and precious-metal chemistry
- Fuel-cell and electrolyser materials
- Emissions-control systems

## What To Watch

- Hydrogen and fuel-cell demand
- Precious-metal prices
- Portfolio restructuring
