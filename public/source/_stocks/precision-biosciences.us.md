## Investment Thesis

Precision BioSciences develops gene-editing therapies using its ARCUS nuclease, with programmes in oncology, rare disease and in-vivo editing. It licenses the platform to partners and has narrowed its own pipeline to conserve cash. Clinical milestones and partnering are the value drivers.

## Technology Exposure

- ARCUS gene editing
- Allogeneic cell therapies
- In-vivo gene editing

## What To Watch

- Clinical data
- Partnering
- Financing
