Ayar Labs builds optical I/O chiplets that replace copper links between accelerators, memory and switches. Its TeraPHY chiplet is designed to be co-packaged with compute silicon.

The company works with foundries and system vendors to insert optical links into standard packaging flows.

**Why it matters**

- Optical I/O chiplets

- Co-packaged with compute

- Standards-based integration
