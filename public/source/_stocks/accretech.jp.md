## Investment Thesis

Accretech builds wafer grinders, dicing saws and inspection tools used to thin and singulate wafers before packaging. As packages stack more dies, wafer thinning and die quality become yield-critical, and the company's tools sit directly in that flow. It also supplies metrology for advanced packaging.

## Technology Exposure

- Wafer grinders and thinning tools
- Dicing saws
- Inspection and metrology

## What To Watch

- Advanced-packaging demand
- Wafer-thinning intensity
- Memory and logic capex
