## Investment Thesis

Murata dominates the multilayer ceramic capacitor market and supplies RF filters, modules and sensors for handsets, vehicles and data centres. MLCCs are the invisible passives that stabilise power delivery in every advanced package, and AI servers use far more of them per board. The company's ceramic and materials expertise underpins its cost and miniaturisation lead.

## Technology Exposure

- Multilayer ceramic capacitors
- RF filters and front-end modules
- Sensors and battery materials

## What To Watch

- AI-server MLCC content
- Handset and automotive demand
- Capacity utilisation and pricing
