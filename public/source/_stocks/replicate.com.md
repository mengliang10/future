## Investment Thesis

Replicate hosts community models and bills by usage, which made it a default way for developers to try open image and video generation without managing GPUs. The catalogue breadth is the differentiator, but hosting commodity weights is easy to undercut once a cloud offers the same endpoint. Its future depends on moving customers from experimentation to production workloads.

## Technology Exposure

- Hosted open-source model catalogue
- Usage-based inference API
- Fine-tuning and custom deployments

## What To Watch

- Conversion from trials to production
- Commoditisation of model hosting
- Margin on bursty GPU demand
