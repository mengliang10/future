## Investment Thesis

BWX Technologies builds naval nuclear reactors, nuclear components and fuel, and supplies commercial nuclear equipment and services. It is one of very few qualified suppliers of nuclear-grade components, which gives it a strategic position in both defence and civil nuclear. Backlog and government budgets drive results.

## Technology Exposure

- Naval nuclear reactors and components
- Nuclear fuel and materials
- Commercial nuclear services

## What To Watch

- Defence and naval programme funding
- SMR and advanced-reactor orders
- Nuclear-grade manufacturing capacity
