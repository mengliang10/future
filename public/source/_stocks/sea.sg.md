## Investment Thesis

Sea runs Shopee, the leading e-commerce marketplace in Southeast Asia and Brazil, alongside the Garena games business that funded its expansion. It has prioritised growth over margin, then pivoted to profitability, and now invests in live commerce and AI-driven logistics. Brazilian expansion adds a large but expensive second front.

## Technology Exposure

- E-commerce marketplace and logistics.
- Gaming and digital financial services.

## What To Watch

- Shopee take rate and logistics cost.
- Brazil investment versus Southeast Asian profitability.
