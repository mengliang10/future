## Investment Thesis

Framatome designs and maintains nuclear reactors, supplies fuel assemblies and components, and services the French and international reactor fleet. It is central to EDF's new-build and life-extension programmes. Ownership by EDF aligns it with French energy policy.

- Nuclear fuel assemblies
- Maintenance and life extension
