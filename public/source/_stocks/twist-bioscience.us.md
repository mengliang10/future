Twist Bioscience writes DNA using a semiconductor-based synthesis process, selling synthetic genes, oligo pools and antibody libraries to research and pharmaceutical customers.

Its platform underpins AI-guided protein engineering and synthetic biology workflows that need large numbers of designed sequences.

**Why it matters**

- High-throughput DNA synthesis

- Synthetic genes and libraries

- Antibody discovery
