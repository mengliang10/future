## Investment Thesis

Fanuc supplies factory automation — robots, controllers and CNC systems — and earns a substantial share of revenue from service and spare parts on its installed base. It is vertically integrated, building its own components, which supports reliability but slows cost reduction. Chinese competitors are closing the gap on low-payload robots, squeezing the entry segment.

## Technology Exposure

- Industrial robots and CNC controls.
- Service and maintenance annuity.

## What To Watch

- Price competition from Chinese robot makers.
- Robotics orders from automotive and electronics.
