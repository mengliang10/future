## Investment Thesis

Charles River supplies research animals, safety-assessment services and manufacturing support to pharma and biotech. Its preclinical testing business is a gatekeeper for IND filings, making it a levered play on biotech funding. Demand normalised sharply after the pandemic research boom.

## Technology Exposure

- Preclinical safety assessment
- Research models and services
- Cell and gene therapy testing

## What To Watch

- Biotech funding and study starts
- Pricing and competition
- Regulatory changes on animal testing
