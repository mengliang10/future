d-matrix designs inference accelerators that place compute next to memory to reduce the energy spent moving data. Its Corsair product targets low-latency inference such as recommendation and chat.

The architecture is digital rather than analog, which keeps it compatible with standard manufacturing and software tooling.

**Why it matters**

- In-memory compute for inference

- Low-latency serving

- Rack-scale deployment
