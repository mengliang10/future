## Investment Thesis

Photronics produces photomasks for semiconductor and flat-panel-display manufacturing, with plants in the US, Europe and Asia. Masks are a design-dependent consumable: every new chip or panel design needs a new set, so revenue follows design activity as well as unit volume. The company is smaller than its Japanese peers but geographically diversified.

## Technology Exposure

- Semiconductor photomasks
- Flat-panel-display masks
- High-end and mainstream mask sets

## What To Watch

- Design activity at fabless customers
- High-end mask capacity
- Display capex
