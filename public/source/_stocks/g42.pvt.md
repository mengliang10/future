## Investment Thesis

G42 is the UAE's flagship AI and cloud group, backed by sovereign capital and partnered with Microsoft, which invested US$1.5bn in 2024. It operates data centres and builds Arabic-language and domain models for government and energy clients. Its role is to make the UAE a compute and model jurisdiction rather than only a customer of foreign clouds.

## Technology Exposure

- National AI cloud and data centres.
- Arabic-language and domain models.

## What To Watch

- Compute capacity and chip access.
- Commercial contracts beyond government.
