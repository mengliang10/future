## Investment Thesis

Chart Industries builds cryogenic storage tanks, liquefiers, pumps and gas-handling equipment for industrial gas, hydrogen, LNG and space customers. Launch sites need large liquid-oxygen and liquid-nitrogen supply, and Chart's tanks and vaporisers sit in that infrastructure. Clean-energy projects add growth and cyclicality.

- Liquefaction and gas-processing equipment
- Hydrogen and LNG infrastructure
