## Investment Thesis

Fireworks sells a serving layer tuned for latency and throughput, with fine-tuning and deployment on open weights. Its pitch is that most production workloads need a small, adapted model served cheaply rather than a frontier API, and that the platform should hide the infrastructure. Competition from GPU clouds and model providers bundling inference is intense.

## Technology Exposure

- Serverless and dedicated inference
- Fine-tuning and model adaptation
- Enterprise deployment tooling

## What To Watch

- Price competition in inference
- Customer concentration among AI-native startups
- Margin on third-party GPU capacity
