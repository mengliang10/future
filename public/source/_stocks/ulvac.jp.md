## Investment Thesis

ULVAC builds vacuum pumps, deposition and etch equipment, and also supplies sputtering targets and specialty materials. Its tools serve semiconductor, display and battery manufacturing, giving it exposure to several capex cycles. The combination of equipment and materials is unusual and helps it tune processes jointly with customers.

## Technology Exposure

- Sputtering and deposition equipment
- Vacuum pumps and components
- Targets and specialty materials

## What To Watch

- Advanced packaging and power-device capex
- Display and battery equipment demand
- Competition from larger toolmakers
