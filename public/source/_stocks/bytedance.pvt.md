## Investment Thesis

ByteDance trains the Seed family of foundation models and distributes them to consumers inside Doubao and to developers through the Volcano Engine cloud, giving it a distribution channel that most model labs have to rent. The company's scale in recommendation systems and short video also makes multimodal generation a natural product extension rather than a research project. It remains private and sits inside the US-China technology restrictions that govern access to leading-edge accelerators.

## Technology Exposure

- Doubao consumer assistant and enterprise API
- Seed foundation and multimodal models
- Volcano Engine model hosting and inference

## What To Watch

- Accelerator access under export controls
- Monetisation of consumer AI features
- Regulatory pressure in the US and Europe
