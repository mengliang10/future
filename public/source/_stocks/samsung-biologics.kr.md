## Investment Thesis

Samsung Biologics operates large-scale mammalian-cell manufacturing plants in Incheon and provides contract development and manufacturing for global pharma and biotech customers. It has been adding capacity and moving into antibody-drug conjugates and mRNA. Order backlog and plant utilisation determine results.

## Technology Exposure

- Biologics contract manufacturing
- Antibody-drug conjugate capacity
- mRNA and gene-therapy services

## What To Watch

- Plant utilisation and new orders
- Capacity additions
- Competition and pricing
