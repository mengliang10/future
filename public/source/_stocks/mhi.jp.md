## Investment Thesis

Mitsubishi Heavy Industries builds the H3 launcher, rocket engines and components for the International Space Station, alongside power plants, aircraft and defence equipment. Its space business is strategically important to Japan's independent access to space. The group's scale means space is a small but high-profile segment.

## Technology Exposure

- H3 launch vehicle
- Rocket engines and space structures
- Power, defence and industrial machinery

## What To Watch

- H3 cadence and cost
- Japanese government space budget
- Energy and defence orders
