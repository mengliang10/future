## Investment Thesis

Nitto Denko makes adhesive tapes, protective films, polarising films and semiconductor-processing tapes. Its dicing and back-grinding tapes are used in wafer and package assembly, making it a quiet supplier to every advanced package. The company is also expanding in medical and environmental materials.

## Technology Exposure

- Semiconductor dicing and process tapes
- Polarising and optical films
- Medical and industrial adhesives

## What To Watch

- Advanced-packaging demand
- Display film pricing
- New medical materials
