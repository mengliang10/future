## Investment Thesis

Thermo Fisher sells instruments, reagents, consumables and services to pharma, biotech, academia and clinical labs, and provides clinical-trial logistics and manufacturing services. Its scale and installed base create recurring revenue, and it supplies much of the equipment used to develop and produce biologics. Biotech funding and pharma R&D budgets drive growth.

## Technology Exposure

- Laboratory instruments and reagents
- Biologics manufacturing and CDMO services
- Clinical-trial logistics

## What To Watch

- Pharma and biotech R&D spending
- Biotech funding environment
- China and tariff exposure
