## Investment Thesis

BE Semiconductor Industries makes die-attach, flip-chip and hybrid-bonding tools used in advanced packaging and chiplet assembly. Hybrid bonding is the key technology for stacking dies with direct copper-to-copper connections, and BESI is the leading tool supplier for it. Its results are a direct read on advanced-packaging capex.

## Technology Exposure

- Hybrid-bonding equipment
- Die-attach and flip-chip tools
- Packaging automation

## What To Watch

- Hybrid-bonding adoption in memory and logic
- Advanced packaging capex
- Customer concentration
