## Investment Thesis

Nova provides optical critical-dimension metrology and materials analysis used to control lithography, deposition and etch processes. Its tools measure structures at the nanometre scale, where small drifts cost yield. The company has been expanding into packaging and memory applications.

## Technology Exposure

- Optical CD metrology
- Materials and film metrology
- Advanced packaging metrology

## What To Watch

- Leading-edge node capex
- Metrology intensity per node
- Competition and pricing
