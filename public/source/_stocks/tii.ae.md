## Investment Thesis

TII is the applied-research arm of Abu Dhabi's Advanced Technology Research Council and releases Falcon models openly, using them to build a domestic AI research base and talent pipeline. State funding removes the need to monetise weights directly, so the output is measured in capability, adoption and sovereign positioning rather than revenue. Its compute access and stable funding make it an unusual competitor in open models.

## Technology Exposure

- Falcon open-weight models
- Applied research in AI and cryptography
- Sovereign AI capability building

## What To Watch

- Sustained state funding
- Adoption of Falcon outside the UAE
- Talent retention against commercial labs
