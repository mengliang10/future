## Investment Thesis

Magic Leap raised enormous sums on a consumer promise, then repositioned around enterprise with the Magic Leap 2 headset aimed at training, surgery, industrial design and remote assistance. Its optics and display stack remain among the more advanced in the industry, and enterprise deployments value the wider field of view and dimming. The problem is a small addressable market relative to the capital invested, and competition from cheaper glasses and from Meta's subsidised headsets.

## Technology Exposure

- Enterprise AR headsets and software
- Optics and display technology
- Healthcare and industrial deployments

## What To Watch

- Enterprise fleet size versus pilots
- Funding and strategic ownership
- Price pressure from subsidised competitors
