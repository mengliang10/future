## Investment Thesis

Boeing builds commercial aircraft, defence systems, satellites and the Space Launch System core stage, and co-owns United Launch Alliance. Its space portfolio includes crew spacecraft and satellite systems, though the company has faced well-publicised execution and quality problems. Aerospace recovery and defence programmes drive the stock.

## Technology Exposure

- Commercial aircraft
- Space Launch System and satellites
- Defence and services

## What To Watch

- Commercial aircraft delivery recovery
- Space programme cost overruns
- Defence contract performance
