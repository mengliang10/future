## Investment Thesis

Vicor builds power-conversion modules with high density and efficiency, used in AI data-centre boards, satellites and defence electronics. Its factorised power architecture lets customers distribute conversion close to the load, which matters as accelerator currents rise. The company is expanding into 48-volt data-centre power and space-qualified modules.
