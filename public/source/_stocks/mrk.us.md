## Investment Thesis

Merck sells oncology, vaccine, animal-health and hospital products, with Keytruda as the largest revenue contributor. The company is preparing for Keytruda's patent expiry by developing subcutaneous formulations and combination therapies, and it has been acquiring pipeline assets. Vaccine demand and litigation add variability.

## Technology Exposure

- Oncology and immuno-oncology
- Vaccines including HPV and pneumococcal
- Animal health and hospital products

## What To Watch

- Keytruda patent cliff and subcutaneous launch
- Pipeline and acquisition returns
- Vaccine demand
