ICEYE builds and operates small SAR satellites, which use radar rather than visible light, so they can image at night and through cloud cover.

Customers include insurers, governments and disaster-response agencies that need flood, fire and vessel monitoring.

**Why it matters**

- Small SAR satellites

- All-weather imaging

- Insurance and disaster response
