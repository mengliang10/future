## Investment Thesis

Kioxia is one of the largest NAND flash producers, with its BiCS vertical stacking technology and joint fabs with Western Digital in Yokkaichi and Kitakami. NAND pricing is cyclical and capital-intensive, and the company's margins swing with bit demand from smartphones, servers and AI storage tiers. Its listing gave it direct access to capital for the next layer-count transition, but it competes against Samsung, SK Hynix/Solidigm and Micron.

## Technology Exposure

- BiCS 3D NAND flash
- Data-centre and enterprise SSDs
- Joint fabrication with Western Digital

## What To Watch

- NAND bit pricing and inventory
- Layer-count transition capex
- AI storage demand versus consumer weakness
