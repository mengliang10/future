## Investment Thesis

Astroscale flew a demonstration mission in 2024 that rendezvoused with and inspected a derelict upper stage, a prerequisite for docking and removal. Governments and insurers are the natural customers, since debris risk is a shared cost that no single operator will pay to solve alone. Revenue depends on institutional contracts.

## Technology Exposure

- Debris inspection and removal services.
- Life-extension and servicing spacecraft.

## What To Watch

- Government contract awards.
- Technical readiness for docking with tumbling objects.
