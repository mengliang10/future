## Investment Thesis

Siemens combines factory automation hardware with industrial software for design and simulation, which lets it sell productivity rather than components. It is embedding AI into automation and has spun out its healthcare arm. The installed base in European manufacturing is a durable moat, but European industrial demand is structurally weak.

## Technology Exposure

- Industrial automation and digital-twin software.
- Grid and rail infrastructure.

## What To Watch

- Software growth versus hardware cyclicality.
- European industrial capex.
