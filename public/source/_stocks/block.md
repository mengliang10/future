## Investment Thesis

Block monetises small-merchant payments through Square, consumer payments and investing through Cash App, and bitcoin through mining and hardware. Its scale gives it a distribution channel for stablecoin and bitcoin rails that pure crypto companies lack, while its lending and banking ambitions add regulatory complexity. The investment debate is about margin discipline across a portfolio rather than crypto exposure alone.

## Technology Exposure

- Square seller payments and software
- Cash App consumer financial services
- Bitcoin mining and hardware

## What To Watch

- Cash App monetisation per user
- Stablecoin and bitcoin product adoption
- Credit losses in lending products
