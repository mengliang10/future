## Investment Thesis

Reliance's Jio transformed Indian mobile data through low prices and scale, and the group is now investing in AI data centres and domestic compute to serve Indian enterprises. Energy and petrochemicals still provide most cash flow, which funds the digital build-out. India's data-sovereignty rules favour domestic capacity.

## Technology Exposure

- Telecom network and data centres.
- Retail and digital services.

## What To Watch

- Data-centre commissioning and utilisation.
- Energy segment cash flow.
