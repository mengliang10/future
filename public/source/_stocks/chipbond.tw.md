## Investment Thesis

Chipbond provides gold and copper bumping, chip-on-film and wafer-level packaging, mainly for display-driver ICs. The business is tied to panel and handset cycles, with automotive displays adding a steadier stream. It has been diversifying into other bumping and packaging services as display growth matures.

## Technology Exposure

- Display-driver bumping and chip-on-film
- Wafer-level packaging
- Automotive display packaging

## What To Watch

- Panel and handset demand
- Automotive display qualification
- Gold and copper input costs
