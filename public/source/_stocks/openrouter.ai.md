## Investment Thesis

OpenRouter aggregates model endpoints and routes requests by price, latency or availability, taking a margin on usage. For developers it solves the switching problem: one key, many vendors, and fallbacks when a provider degrades. The risk is that labs and clouds can disintermediate a router by offering the same convenience directly.

## Technology Exposure

- Unified API across model providers
- Routing, fallback and price comparison
- Usage analytics and controls

## What To Watch

- Take rate under price competition
- Disintermediation by model providers
- Reliability of upstream endpoints
