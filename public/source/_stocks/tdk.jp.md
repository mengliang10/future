## Investment Thesis

TDK makes ceramic capacitors, inductors, magnetic heads, sensors and batteries, with a strong position in power inductors for AI servers and in consumer battery cells. Its magnetic materials and multilayer processes are core competencies that transfer across components. The company also supplies energy-storage and EV components.

## Technology Exposure

- Power inductors and MLCCs
- Magnetic materials and sensors
- Consumer and industrial batteries

## What To Watch

- AI-server power-component demand
- Battery-cell competition
- Automotive electronics growth
