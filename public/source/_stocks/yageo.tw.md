## Investment Thesis

Yageo is one of the largest passive-component makers, with resistors, MLCCs, inductors and sensors serving industrial, automotive and consumer customers. It has grown through acquisitions, including KEMET, to broaden its portfolio and geographic reach. Pricing and utilisation in commodity passives remain cyclical.

## Technology Exposure

- Chip resistors and MLCCs
- Inductors and sensors
- Industrial and automotive passives

## What To Watch

- Passive pricing cycle
- Automotive and industrial demand
- Acquisition integration
