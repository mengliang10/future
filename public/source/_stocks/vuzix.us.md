## Investment Thesis

Vuzix sells rugged smart glasses to warehouse, field-service and medical customers, and supplies waveguides and display engines to other manufacturers. Its M400 and M4000 lines are used for hands-free picking, remote assistance and telemedicine, where the value is workflow and durability rather than consumer design. The company has repeatedly promised a consumer product and repeatedly found that enterprise buyers and defence budgets pay first. Cash burn and dilution are the recurring constraint.

## Technology Exposure

- Enterprise smart glasses and support contracts
- Waveguide and display-engine supply
- Defence and public-sector wearables

## What To Watch

- Enterprise order flow outside pilot programmes
- Cash runway and dilution
- Competition from phone-tethered consumer glasses
