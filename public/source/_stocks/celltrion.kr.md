## Investment Thesis

Celltrion develops and manufactures biosimilars for autoimmune disease, oncology and ophthalmology, and sells them in Europe, the US and Asia. It has integrated manufacturing and is expanding into novel biologics. Pricing pressure in biosimilars and regulatory approvals drive results.

## Technology Exposure

- Biosimilar medicines
- Contract manufacturing
- Novel biologic pipeline

## What To Watch

- Biosimilar pricing and uptake
- Regulatory approvals
- Manufacturing costs
