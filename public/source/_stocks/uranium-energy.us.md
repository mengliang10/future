## Investment Thesis

Uranium Energy Corp holds uranium projects in the US, Canada and Paraguay, and owns a processing plant in Wyoming. It is restarting production to supply US utilities and government stockpiles. Uranium prices and permitting determine the pace.

- Uranium mining and processing
- US strategic uranium reserves
- Exploration projects

- Permitting
