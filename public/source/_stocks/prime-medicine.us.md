## Investment Thesis

Prime Medicine develops therapies based on prime editing, a CRISPR-derived method that can rewrite DNA without double-strand breaks. Its pipeline targets liver, blood and eye diseases, with partnerships supporting delivery and manufacturing. The technology is early and capital-intensive.

## Technology Exposure

- Prime editing therapies
- Delivery technology
- Partnership-funded programmes

## What To Watch

- First clinical data
- Delivery and specificity
- Funding
