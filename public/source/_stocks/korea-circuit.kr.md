## Investment Thesis

Korea Circuit makes printed circuit boards and package substrates, serving memory, handset and display customers. The company has been shifting mix toward semiconductor substrates as the PCB business matures, but it remains smaller than the leading Japanese and Taiwanese substrate makers. Execution on yield and qualification is the main variable.

## Technology Exposure

- Package substrates for memory and mobile
- High-density PCBs
- Flexible circuit boards

## What To Watch

- Substrate qualification progress
- Memory and handset demand
- Competitive pricing
