## Investment Thesis

Nippon Electric Glass produces glass substrates for displays, glass for automotive and medical applications, and specialty glass fibres. Display glass is the largest business and is tied to panel capacity and pricing. The company is developing glass compositions for semiconductor and battery applications.

## Technology Exposure

- Display glass substrates
- Specialty glass and glass fibre
- Glass for automotive and medical use

## What To Watch

- Panel capacity and pricing
- Specialty glass growth
- Energy costs
