## Investment Thesis

VAT Group produces vacuum valves and seals used in deposition, etch, lithography and inspection equipment. Its valves control gas flow and isolation in ultra-high-vacuum chambers, and the company holds a leading share of the market. Every new tool needs several valves, making it a leveraged play on equipment builds.

## Technology Exposure

- Ultra-high-vacuum valves
- Vacuum components and seals
- Service and spares

## What To Watch

- Semiconductor equipment build rates
- EUV and advanced-node capex
- Market-share defence
