## Investment Thesis

Jusung Engineering builds chemical and physical vapour deposition, etch and atomic-layer tools for semiconductor, display and solar customers. It has been developing tools for advanced memory and packaging processes. Revenue is lumpy and tied to a few large capex programmes.

## Technology Exposure

- CVD, PVD and ALD equipment
- Etch tools
- Display and solar equipment

## What To Watch

- Memory and logic capex
- New tool qualifications
- Order lumpiness
