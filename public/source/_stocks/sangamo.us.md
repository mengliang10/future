## Investment Thesis

Sangamo develops gene therapies using zinc-finger nucleases and AAV delivery, with programmes in haemophilia, lysosomal storage disease and neurology. It licenses its editing technology to partners and has faced funding constraints. Clinical and partnership progress determines survival.

## Technology Exposure

- Zinc-finger gene editing
- AAV gene therapy
- Technology licensing

## What To Watch

- Cash runway
