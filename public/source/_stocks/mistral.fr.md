## Investment Thesis

Mistral releases compact open-weight models alongside paid API and enterprise deployments, which lets it win developer adoption without giving away the whole product. Its positioning is explicitly European: data residency, EU AI Act familiarity and public-sector procurement are part of the offer, and governments that will not send workloads to US providers are a genuine addressable market. The constraint is compute, which is funded rather than earned.

## Technology Exposure

- Open-weight Mistral and Magistral models
- Le Chat assistant and paid API
- Sovereign and defence deployments

## What To Watch

- Funding versus compute requirements
- European public-sector contract wins
- Model quality against US and Chinese labs
