## Investment Thesis

CATL supplied roughly 38% of global EV batteries in 2024, more than any competitor, and has expanded from cells into licensing its technology to other manufacturers. It launched a sodium-ion product in 2025 aimed at cold climates and cost-sensitive segments, alongside faster-charging LFP chemistries. Growth now depends on European and North American plants, where permitting, tariffs and local-content rules matter as much as chemistry.

## Technology Exposure

- LFP and sodium-ion cells for mass-market vehicles.
- Grid-scale storage systems and licensing of cell technology.

## What To Watch

- Utilisation and pricing in a oversupplied cell market.
- Progress of European plants under tariff and local-content pressure.
