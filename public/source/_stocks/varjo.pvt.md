## Investment Thesis

Varjo builds headsets with human-eye resolution and mixed-reality passthrough for pilot training, automotive design and medical simulation, where fidelity is the purchase criterion. Its XR-4 line targets professional users who need to read instruments and text inside a virtual cockpit. The business depends on defence and aviation training budgets and on a small number of large customers, which makes revenue lumpy.

## Technology Exposure

- Professional VR/XR headsets
- Simulation and training software partnerships
- Mixed-reality passthrough systems

## What To Watch

- Defence and aviation training budget cycles
- Customer concentration
- Competition from lower-cost headsets at adequate fidelity
