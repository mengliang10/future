## Investment Thesis

Corning makes display glass, optical fibre, specialty glass and ceramic substrates, and its glass know-how is relevant to carriers and cores in advanced packaging. Display glass remains the largest business, with optical communications and automotive glass adding growth. The company invests through cycles to hold cost leadership.

## Technology Exposure

- Display glass substrates
- Optical fibre and connectivity
- Specialty glass and ceramic materials

## What To Watch

- Display panel demand
- Optical-fibre and data-centre demand
- Glass-substrate adoption in packaging
