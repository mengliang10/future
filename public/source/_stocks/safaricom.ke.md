## Investment Thesis

Safaricom dominates Kenyan mobile connectivity and owns M-PESA, the agent-based money transfer system used by most adults in the country. Payments and mobile data now contribute more than voice, and the company is expanding into Ethiopia. Regulatory pressure on M-PESA fees is the main constraint on revenue per user.

## Technology Exposure

- Mobile money and agent distribution.
- Connectivity and enterprise services.

## What To Watch

- M-PESA fee regulation.
- Ethiopian market execution.
- Data revenue growth.
