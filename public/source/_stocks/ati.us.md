## Investment Thesis

ATI produces titanium, nickel-based superalloys and specialty steels for aerospace, defence and medical customers. Its alloys go into jet engines, airframes and rocket components where high strength-to-weight and heat resistance are required. Aerospace build rates and certification cycles drive demand.

## Technology Exposure

- Titanium and nickel superalloys
- Specialty steel and forgings
- Aerospace and defence materials

## What To Watch

- Defence spending
- Raw-material and energy costs
