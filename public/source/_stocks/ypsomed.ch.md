## Investment Thesis

Ypsomed makes autoinjectors, pen injectors and insulin pumps, and supplies device platforms to pharmaceutical companies launching GLP-1 and other self-administered therapies. Its devices are customised per drug and manufactured in Switzerland. Device programme wins drive growth.

## Technology Exposure

- Autoinjectors and pen injectors
- Insulin pumps and infusion sets
- Contract device development

## What To Watch

- GLP-1 device contracts
- Capacity expansion
- Customer concentration
