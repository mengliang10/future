## Investment Thesis

Albemarle produces lithium from brine and hard-rock resources in Chile, Australia and the US, and sells battery-grade carbonate and hydroxide to cathode and cell makers. Lithium pricing has been volatile, and the company has been scaling conversion capacity in the US and Australia. Its results are a direct read on battery raw-material economics.

## Technology Exposure

- Battery-grade lithium carbonate and hydroxide
- Bromine and specialty chemicals
- Lithium conversion capacity

## What To Watch

- Lithium pricing
- Conversion plant ramp
- Permitting for new resources
