## Investment Thesis

Synchron's Stentrode is delivered through the jugular vein and expanded inside a blood vessel next to the motor cortex, avoiding open-brain surgery. That design trades channel count for procedural safety, which matters for patients with paralysis who need a reliable cursor and switching interface rather than high-resolution control. The company has run early human trials in the US and Australia and is pursuing a pivotal pathway.

## Technology Exposure

- Stentrode endovascular implant
- Cursor and device-control software
- Clinical trial programme

## What To Watch

- Pivotal trial enrolment and outcomes
- Durability of signal over years
- Competition from higher-channel implants
