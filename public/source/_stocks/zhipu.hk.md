## Investment Thesis

Zhipu, listed as Knowledge Atlas Technology, builds the GLM family and sells assistants, agents and private deployments into Chinese enterprises and government bodies. Its revenue mix is closer to systems integration than pure API sales, which brings services margin and procurement dependence. The company listed in Hong Kong in January 2026 and has used the market repeatedly to fund training.

## Technology Exposure

- GLM foundation models
- Enterprise agents and private deployments
- Public-sector and state-linked contracts

## What To Watch

- Losses against training spend
- Domestic price war with other Chinese labs
- Export-control effects on compute
