## Investment Thesis

Medpace runs clinical trials for biotech and small pharma, with a focus on oncology, cardiology and metabolic disease. Its customer base is more biotech-heavy than large CROs, making it sensitive to funding conditions. Backlog and cancellations are the key operating metrics.

## Technology Exposure

- Clinical trial management
- Oncology and metabolic trials
- Regulatory support

## What To Watch

- Biotech funding
- Backlog conversion
- Cancellations
