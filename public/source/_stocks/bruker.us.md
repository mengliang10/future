## Investment Thesis

Bruker sells mass spectrometers, nuclear magnetic resonance systems, X-ray instruments and microscopes for life-science, materials and semiconductor research. Its tools are used in structural biology, drug discovery and materials characterisation, and it has a growing cryogenic and quantum-instrumentation line. Research funding and lab capex drive results.

## Technology Exposure

- Mass spectrometry and NMR
- X-ray and microscopy instruments
- Cryogenic and quantum instrumentation

## What To Watch
