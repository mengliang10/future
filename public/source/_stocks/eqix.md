## Investment Thesis

Equinix leases colocation space and cross-connects in carrier-dense metros, which makes it the neutral meeting point for networks, clouds and enterprises. AI workloads need more power and cooling than traditional colocation, and the company is retrofitting and building xScale facilities with partners to serve hyperscale demand. Power availability, not land, is now the limiting factor for growth.

## Technology Exposure

- Colocation and cross-connect revenue
- xScale hyperscale joint ventures
- Interconnection and peering

## What To Watch

- Power secured for new capacity
- Pricing power in tight metros
- Capex intensity and development yields
