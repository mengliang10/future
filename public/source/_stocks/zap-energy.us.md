Zap Energy's z-pinch design uses a flowing plasma and its own magnetic field to confine and compress fuel, avoiding the large magnet sets that dominate other fusion approaches.

The company has built and run prototype devices and is working toward net energy gain.

**Why it matters**

- Sheared-flow z-pinch

- Magnet-free confinement

- Compact device architecture
