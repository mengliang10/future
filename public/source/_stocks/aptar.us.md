## Investment Thesis

AptarGroup produces pumps, valves, inhalers and injection devices for pharma, beauty and food customers. Its pharma segment supplies nasal and ophthalmic delivery systems and injectables, where device performance affects drug bioavailability. The business is diversified and less cyclical than pure pharma suppliers.

## Technology Exposure

- Nasal and ophthalmic delivery systems
- Injection and infusion devices
- Dispensing and sealing components

## What To Watch

- Pharma device demand
- Beauty and food packaging volumes
- Raw-material costs
