## Investment Thesis

Ebara supplies chemical-mechanical polishing tools, wafer-cleaning systems and vacuum pumps, with CMP becoming more important as nodes add layers and packaging adds planarisation steps. Its pumps are also used in deposition and etch tools. The company's diversified industrial pumps business smooths cycles.

## Technology Exposure

- CMP tools
- Wafer cleaning systems
- Vacuum pumps and industrial machinery

## What To Watch

- CMP step growth per node
- Advanced packaging planarisation
- Competition from larger toolmakers
