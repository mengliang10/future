## Investment Thesis

Cognition positioned Devin as an AI teammate that can plan, write, test and debug changes across a codebase, sold as a subscription to engineering organisations. The product pushed the industry from autocomplete toward delegated tasks, and the company has acquired adjacent tooling to broaden its stack. Reliability on large repositories and the trust required to let an agent merge code remain the binding constraints.

## Technology Exposure

- Devin autonomous engineering agent
- Enterprise engineering subscriptions
- Agent tooling acquisitions

## What To Watch

- Task completion rates on real repositories
- Enterprise retention after pilots
- Competition from incumbent coding platforms
