## Investment Thesis

Novo Nordisk makes insulin, GLP-1 therapies for diabetes and obesity, haemophilia products and growth-hormone treatments. Its semaglutide franchise made it Europe's most valuable company, and it is expanding manufacturing and developing oral and combination incretins. Pricing pressure, compounding pharmacies and competition shape the outlook.

## Technology Exposure

- GLP-1 and insulin therapies
- Haemophilia and rare-disease products
- Oral and next-generation obesity drugs

## What To Watch

- Manufacturing expansion
- US pricing and compounding competition
- Pipeline readouts
