## Investment Thesis

Guardant Health sells blood-based genomic tests for cancer treatment selection and recurrence monitoring, and is seeking broad reimbursement for colorectal-cancer screening. Its tests analyse circulating tumour DNA, avoiding tissue biopsy. Reimbursement and clinical evidence are the commercial gatekeepers.

- Tumour profiling
