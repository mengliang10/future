Agility Robotics was founded in 2015 as a spin-out of Oregon State University research. Its Digit robot is a roughly human-sized biped designed to move totes and cases through warehouse layouts that were built for people rather than machines.

The company operates a manufacturing facility in Salem, Oregon and has run warehouse pilots with logistics operators including GXO and Amazon. Digit is sold as a service with fleet-management software rather than as a one-off machine.

**Why it matters**

- Bipedal form factor fits existing aisles, stairs and conveyor heights

- Digit handles tote and case movement between fixed automation cells

- Commercial model bundles robot, software and support
