## Investment Thesis

JSR is one of the top suppliers of advanced photoresists, including ArF and EUV materials, and also makes synthetic rubber and display materials. Its 2024 take-private by the Japan Investment Corporation was framed around securing strategic materials technology and allowing longer-horizon investment. The company remains a critical node in the lithography supply chain.

## Technology Exposure

- ArF, KrF and EUV photoresists
- Display and optical materials
- Synthetic rubber and life-science materials

## What To Watch

- EUV resist adoption
- Post-buyout investment strategy
- Consolidation of the Japanese resist industry
