## Investment Thesis

ISC supplies test sockets and connectors for semiconductor test, including sockets for high-speed and fine-pitch packages. Its products are qualified with Korean and international chipmakers and benefit from rising test complexity. The company has been expanding into advanced-package and system-level test sockets.

## Technology Exposure

- IC test sockets
- Connectors and contactors
- Advanced-package test interfaces

## What To Watch

- AI and HBM test demand
- Customer qualifications
- Pricing competition
