## Investment Thesis

L3Harris supplies space payloads, sensors, communications systems and, after acquiring Aerojet Rocketdyne, rocket propulsion and munitions. Its propulsion business makes engines and solid motors used across launch and defence programmes. The company is integrating acquisitions while managing defence budget shifts.

## Technology Exposure

- Rocket propulsion and solid motors
- Space payloads and sensors
- Defence communications and electronics

## What To Watch

- Propulsion programme awards
- Defence budget priorities
- Acquisition integration
