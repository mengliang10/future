## Investment Thesis

Biogen sells multiple-sclerosis therapies andneurology products, and launched Alzheimer's antibody treatments that have had limited commercial uptake. It is cutting costs and acquiring assets in immunology and rare disease to diversify beyond a shrinking MS franchise. Execution and cash deployment are the key issues.

## Technology Exposure

- Multiple sclerosis therapies
- Alzheimer's antibodies
- Neurology and rare-disease pipeline

## What To Watch

- Alzheimer's adoption and reimbursement
- MS franchise erosion
- Acquisition strategy
