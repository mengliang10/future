## Investment Thesis

Paradromics is building the Connexus implant, aimed at thousands of recording channels with a fully implantable, wireless design. Its initial target is severe communication loss, where decoding speech from cortical activity could restore faster interaction than eye-tracking systems. The company has FDA breakthrough designation and is working through the preclinical and early clinical steps that precede any commercial claim.

## Technology Exposure

- Connexus high-channel implant
- Speech decoding for severe paralysis
- Wireless data and power link

## What To Watch

- First-in-human safety results
- Channel count and signal stability
- Manufacturing yield for implantable electronics
