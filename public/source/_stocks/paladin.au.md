## Investment Thesis

Paladin Energy restarted uranium production at Langer Heinrich in Namibia and holds development assets in Canada and Australia. It sells concentrate to utilities under long-term contracts, and its economics depend on realised prices and ramp-up. Namibian operations carry country and water risks.

- Restart and expansion projects
- Utility sales contracts

## What To Watch

- Uranium prices
- Namibian operating conditions
