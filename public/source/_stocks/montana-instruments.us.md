## Investment Thesis

Montana Instruments builds closed-cycle cryostats and low-vibration systems for quantum computing, materials science and optics. Its cryostats avoid liquid helium, which lowers operating cost and simplifies lab infrastructure. The company competes with dilution-refrigerator makers in the higher-temperature cryogenic range.

## Technology Exposure

- Closed-cycle cryostats
- Low-vibration cryogenic systems
- Optical and electrical measurement setups

## What To Watch

- Quantum and materials research demand
- Helium supply and cost
- Competition from dilution-refrigerator vendors
