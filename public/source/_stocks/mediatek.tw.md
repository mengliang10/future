## Investment Thesis

MediaTek designs smartphone processors, connectivity chips and custom application-specific silicon for cloud customers. It competes with Qualcomm in premium phones and has won AI accelerator work that uses Taiwan's advanced packaging capacity. Its position illustrates how value in AI hardware spreads beyond the GPU vendor to custom silicon and connectivity.

## Technology Exposure

- Smartphone and connectivity chips.
- Custom AI ASICs.

## What To Watch

- Custom silicon programme wins.
- Smartphone cycle and Chinese handset share.
