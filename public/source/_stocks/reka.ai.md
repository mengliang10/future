## Investment Thesis

Reka builds natively multimodal models that accept video, audio, images and text, and sells them to organisations that need to reason over media rather than documents. Its founders came from DeepMind and its positioning is applied: content moderation, media analysis and sovereign deployments. It competes with far larger labs on a narrow capability frontier.

## Technology Exposure

- Multimodal foundation models
- Video and audio understanding APIs
- On-premise and sovereign deployments

## What To Watch

- Differentiation against larger multimodal labs
- Enterprise and government contract wins
- Funding runway
