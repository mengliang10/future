## Investment Thesis

Firefly Aerospace operates the Alpha small launcher and is developing the Medium Launch Vehicle, along with lunar landers and orbital services. It serves commercial, civil and defence customers, and has won NASA lunar payload contracts. The company has moved toward public markets, giving investors a rare listed-comparable small launcher.

## Technology Exposure

- Alpha small launch vehicle
- Medium Launch Vehicle development
- Lunar landers and orbital services

## What To Watch

- Launch cadence and reliability
- Medium-launch development
- Government contract awards
