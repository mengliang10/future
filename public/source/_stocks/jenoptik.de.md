## Investment Thesis

Jenoptik makes precision optics, laser systems, optical metrology and traffic-safety equipment. Its photonics business supplies semiconductor lithography and inspection customers, and its lasers serve industrial manufacturing. The company has been refocusing on photonics after divesting non-core units.

## Technology Exposure

- Precision optics and micro-optics
- Laser systems and metrology
- Traffic and safety systems

## What To Watch

- Semiconductor lithography demand
- Industrial laser demand
- Portfolio focus
