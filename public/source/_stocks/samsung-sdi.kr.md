## Investment Thesis

Samsung SDI produces lithium-ion cells for electric vehicles, energy storage and consumer devices, and is developing solid-state cells. Its prismatic and cylindrical formats serve premium EV programmes, while the energy-storage business is growing with grid investment. Margin depends on cell pricing and raw-material costs.

## Technology Exposure

- EV battery cells
- Energy-storage systems
- Solid-state battery development

## What To Watch

- Solid-state pilot-line progress
- EV demand and cell pricing
- Raw-material cost pass-through
