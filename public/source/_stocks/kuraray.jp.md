## Investment Thesis

Kuraray makes polyvinyl alcohol, battery binders, optical films, synthetic leather and water-treatment membranes. Its binder materials help hold electrodes together, and its optical films serve display polarisers. The group is expanding into battery and environmental materials.

## Technology Exposure

- Battery binders and separators
- Optical and polariser films
- Water-treatment membranes

## What To Watch

- Battery-materials growth
- Display film demand
- Vinyl-acetate feedstock costs
