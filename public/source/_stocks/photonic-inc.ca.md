## Investment Thesis

Photonic develops silicon-based photonic quantum processors and quantum interconnects, aiming to combine telecom-band photonics with spin qubits in silicon. The platform leverages existing semiconductor manufacturing, which could make scaling cheaper than bespoke cryogenic hardware. The company is also working on quantum networking for distributed systems.

## Technology Exposure

- Silicon-photonic quantum processors
- Quantum interconnects and networking
- Spin-photon interfaces

## What To Watch
