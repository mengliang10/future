## Investment Thesis

Sarepta sells exon-skipping medicines for Duchenne muscular dystrophy and launched a gene therapy for the disease. Its franchise targets a small patient population with high prices, and safety events have drawn regulatory scrutiny. Manufacturing and label expansion are the key variables.

## Technology Exposure

- Exon-skipping therapies
- Duchenne gene therapy
- Rare-disease pipeline

## What To Watch

- Gene-therapy safety and labelling
- Payer access
- Manufacturing
