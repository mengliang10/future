## Investment Thesis

Quantum Machines builds the pulse-level control electronics and orchestration software that translate quantum algorithms into signals for qubits. Its controllers work across superconducting, trapped-ion and neutral-atom platforms, making it a platform-neutral supplier. Control hardware scales with qubit count, so it grows with every quantum roadmap.

## Technology Exposure

- Quantum control electronics
- Pulse generation and readout systems
- Control software and orchestration

## What To Watch

- Qubit-count growth at customers
- Multi-platform adoption
- Competition from instrument incumbents
