## Investment Thesis

Eli Lilly sells insulin, oncology and neuroscience products and has become the largest pharmaceutical company by market value on the strength of its GLP-1 receptor agonist franchise for diabetes and obesity. It is investing billions in manufacturing capacity because demand exceeds supply, and it is developing oral and next-generation incretins plus Alzheimer's and gene-based medicines. Manufacturing scale and payer access are the constraints.

## Technology Exposure

- Incretin therapies for diabetes and obesity
- Oncology and neuroscience pipelines
- Alzheimer's and gene-based medicines

## What To Watch

- Manufacturing capacity for incretins
- Payer coverage and pricing pressure
- Competition from Novo Nordisk and generics
