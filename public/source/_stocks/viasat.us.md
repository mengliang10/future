## Investment Thesis

Viasat provides satellite broadband, in-flight connectivity and secure government communications, and acquired Inmarsat to expand its global network. The business carries high capital intensity and integration risk, while aviation and defence demand provide growth. Competition from LEO constellations is intensifying.

## Technology Exposure

- Satellite broadband
- In-flight and maritime connectivity
- Government and defence networks

## What To Watch

- Aviation connectivity growth
- Inmarsat integration and leverage
- LEO competition
