## Investment Thesis

AvanStrate produces glass substrates for displays and specialty glass for electronics, with plants in Taiwan, Japan and Singapore. It supplies panel makers that require large-format substrates and is developing glass for advanced packaging and sensors. Ownership has changed several times, and the company reports privately.

## Technology Exposure

- Display glass substrates
- Specialty and ultra-thin glass
- Glass for sensors and packaging

## What To Watch

- Panel-maker demand
- Ultra-thin glass adoption
- Ownership and funding
