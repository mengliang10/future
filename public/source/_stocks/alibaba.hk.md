## Investment Thesis

Alibaba operates Aliyun, the largest public cloud in China, and publishes the Qwen family of open-weight models that much of the domestic AI ecosystem fine-tunes. In February 2025 management guided to more than RMB 380bn of AI and cloud capital expenditure over three years, which converts the cloud division from a software margin business into a capital-intensive utility. Commerce still funds the programme; the open question is whether inference revenue scales before depreciation does.

## Technology Exposure

- Large-scale training and inference capacity inside China.
- Distribution of Qwen weights to overseas developers who cannot buy US APIs at scale.

## What To Watch

- Cloud revenue growth against rising depreciation.
- Whether Qwen licensing converts into paid cloud demand rather than self-hosting.
