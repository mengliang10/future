GlobalFoundries operates fabs in the United States, Germany and Singapore, concentrating on process nodes and specialty technologies rather than the leading edge.

Its customers are in automotive, communications, industrial and power management, where reliability and long product lives matter more than density.

**Why it matters**

- Mature and specialty nodes

- RF and power management

- US and European capacity
