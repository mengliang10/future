## Investment Thesis

Mitsubishi Chemical produces battery electrolyte and anode materials, display films, semiconductor chemicals and industrial gases through group companies. Its portfolio is broad and cyclical, with battery and electronics materials as growth areas. The group has been divesting lower-margin businesses.

## Technology Exposure

- Battery electrolytes and anode materials
- Electronic and display materials
- Industrial chemicals and gases

## What To Watch

- Battery-materials demand
- Petrochemical spreads
- Divestment progress
