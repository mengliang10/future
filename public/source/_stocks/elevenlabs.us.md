ElevenLabs builds generative voice models for speech synthesis, dubbing and voice cloning, with controls for consent and provenance.

Its models are used by publishers, studios and call-centre operators. The company also sells conversational agents that combine speech recognition, a language model and speech output.

**Why it matters**

- Multilingual speech synthesis

- Dubbing and voice preservation

- Voice agents for service workflows
