## Investment Thesis

Thales supplies radar, avionics, secure communications and cybersecurity, and its research arm works on quantum sensors and post-quantum cryptography. Defence budgets in Europe are rising, which supports order intake across the group. Its cyber business sells the practical response to state-linked attacks rather than consumer software.

## Technology Exposure

- Defence electronics and secure communications.
- Cybersecurity and quantum-safe cryptography.

## What To Watch

- European defence budget execution.
- Quantum-sensing programme conversion.
