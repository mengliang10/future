## Investment Thesis

Camtek supplies optical inspection and metrology tools for semiconductor packaging, including bump, TSV and panel-level inspection. Its systems are used to catch defects before die stacking and bonding, where rework is impossible. Advanced packaging and HBM capacity are its main growth drivers.

## Technology Exposure

- Advanced-packaging inspection
- Bump and TSV metrology
- Panel-level inspection

## What To Watch

- HBM and advanced-packaging capex
- Panel-level adoption
- Competition from larger inspection vendors
