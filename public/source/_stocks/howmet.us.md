## Investment Thesis

Howmet Aerospace produces fasteners, investment-cast turbine components and engineered structures for aircraft and launch vehicles. Its fasteners and structural parts are used throughout airframes and rocket assemblies, where weight and reliability are critical. The company benefits from aerospace build rates and aftermarket demand.

## Technology Exposure

- Aerospace fasteners
- Investment-cast engine components
- Engineered structures

## What To Watch

- Aircraft build rates
- Defence and space demand
- Titanium and nickel costs
