## Investment Thesis

Fujifilm supplies photoresists, CMP slurries, process chemicals and image sensors under its electronics business, built from photographic chemistry know-how. Semiconductor materials have become one of its fastest-growing segments, and the company has been investing in US and Japanese production for advanced nodes. Healthcare and imaging provide the base.

## Technology Exposure

- Photoresists and CMP slurries
- Process chemicals and lithography materials
- Healthcare, imaging and business solutions

## What To Watch

- Semiconductor-materials capacity additions
- Advanced-node qualifications
- Healthcare segment profitability
