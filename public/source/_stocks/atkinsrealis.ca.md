## Investment Thesis

AtkinsRealis provides nuclear engineering, reactor life-extension and decommissioning services, and holds Candu reactor technology. It supports utilities in Canada and abroad and is involved in advanced-reactor studies. Its engineering and construction segments also serve infrastructure and power clients.
