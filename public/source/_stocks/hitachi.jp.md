## Investment Thesis

Hitachi builds nuclear reactors and components, rail systems, power equipment and digital infrastructure. Its nuclear business includes boiling-water reactor technology and maintenance, and it has been involved in UK new-build projects. The group's portfolio is broad, with energy transition and digital growth offsetting mature businesses.

## Technology Exposure

- Nuclear reactors and maintenance
- Rail and mobility systems
- Power grids and digital infrastructure

## What To Watch

- Grid and rail investment
- Portfolio restructuring
