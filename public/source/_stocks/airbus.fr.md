## Investment Thesis

Airbus builds commercial aircraft in a duopoly with Boeing, and its defence and space units provide counter-cyclical revenue. The company has taken losses on satellite programmes and is restructuring that division, while investing in high-altitude platforms and launcher cooperation. Aircraft delivery slots are sold years ahead, which makes the order book the key asset.

## Technology Exposure

- Commercial aircraft and aftermarket services.
- Satellites, launchers and defence electronics.

## What To Watch

- Space division losses and restructuring.
- Delivery ramp against supply-chain constraints.
