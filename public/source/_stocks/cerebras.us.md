Cerebras makes the Wafer Scale Engine, a single wafer-sized chip designed to keep model weights on-chip and avoid the memory bottlenecks of GPU clusters.

It sells systems to labs and enterprises and runs its own inference service, which has made it a visible alternative for very fast token generation.

**Why it matters**

- Wafer-scale engine

- Inference-as-a-service

- Large-model training systems
