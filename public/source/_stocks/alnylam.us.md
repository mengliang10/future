## Investment Thesis

Alnylam develops RNA-interference medicines that silence disease-causing genes, with approved therapies for rare diseases and a pipeline in cardiovascular and metabolic disease. Its GalNAc delivery chemistry allows subcutaneous dosing and is licensed to partners. Broad intellectual property and delivery know-how are its moat.

## Technology Exposure

- RNAi therapeutics for rare disease
- Cardiovascular and metabolic programmes
- Delivery chemistry and licensing

## What To Watch

- Pipeline readouts
- Manufacturing and pricing
- Competition from antisense and gene editing
