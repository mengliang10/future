## Investment Thesis

QuantumCTek sells quantum key distribution hardware and network equipment, largely to government and utility customers in China. Revenue is small and project-based, tied to national quantum communication networks rather than commercial demand. It is the clearest listed proxy for state-backed quantum networking, which is a different business from quantum computing.

## Technology Exposure

- QKD terminals and metropolitan network equipment.
- Quantum-safe communication for state and utility networks.

## What To Watch

- Project awards and recognition timing.
- Whether QKD wins against post-quantum cryptography in commercial networks.
