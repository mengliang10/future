## Investment Thesis

Neuracle develops minimally invasive brain-computer interfaces that place electrodes outside the cortex, and has run trials with paralysed patients who controlled cursors and robotic aids through decoded movement intent. The epidural approach trades signal quality for safety and durability. The company is one of the few outside the United States with chronic human implants in progress.

## Technology Exposure

- Communication and control for paralysed patients.
- A lower-risk surgical BCI pathway.

## What To Watch

- Cohort size and follow-up.
- Decoder stability over years.
- Regulatory approval for chronic use.
