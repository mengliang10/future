## Investment Thesis

Asahi Kasei makes battery separators, specialty resins, electronic materials and healthcare products. Its separator business supplies lithium-ion cell makers with coated membranes that affect safety and cycle life. The company also has a semiconductor-materials and automotive interiors presence.

## Technology Exposure

- Battery separators
- Engineering plastics and resins
- Healthcare and electronic materials

## What To Watch

- Separator capacity and pricing
- EV cell demand
- Healthcare segment performance
