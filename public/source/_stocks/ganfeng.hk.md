## Investment Thesis

Ganfeng Lithium extracts and refines lithium and also makes batteries and solid-state cells, giving it exposure across the chain. It holds resources in Australia, Argentina, Mexico and China, and supplies battery-grade compounds to cell makers worldwide. Lithium prices and project execution dominate results.

## Technology Exposure

- Lithium resources and refining
- Battery-grade lithium compounds
- Solid-state and consumer batteries

## What To Watch

- Lithium pricing
- Resource project execution
- Solid-state battery progress
