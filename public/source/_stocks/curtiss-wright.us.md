## Investment Thesis

Curtiss-Wright supplies reactor coolant pumps, valves, actuators and instrumentation to commercial and naval nuclear programmes, along with defence electronics and industrial equipment. Its nuclear business benefits from life-extension work at existing plants and from new reactor designs. Defence and energy budgets both matter.

- Nuclear instrumentation and controls
- Defence electronics and sensors
