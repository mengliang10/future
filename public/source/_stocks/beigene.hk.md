## Investment Thesis

BeiGene built a portfolio of oncology drugs, including the BTK inhibitor zanubrutinib, and expanded into US and European markets with its own commercial organisation. It renamed itself BeOne Medicines in 2025 and has been reducing losses as product revenue scales. It is the clearest example of a Chinese biotech crossing into global commercial operations rather than licensing assets out.

## Technology Exposure

- Global oncology commercial platform.
- Clinical development capacity in China and the West.

## What To Watch

- Product revenue growth and path to profitability.
- US pricing and reimbursement pressure.
- Pipeline readouts beyond the lead drug.
