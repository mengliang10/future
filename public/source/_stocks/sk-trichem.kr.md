## Investment Thesis

SK Trichem develops and produces precursors for deposition and etching, supplying Korean memory manufacturers as they localise advanced materials. Its position within the SK group gives it a captive customer relationship and visibility on node transitions. Capacity and qualification, rather than demand, set the growth pace.

## Technology Exposure

- Precursors for thin-film deposition
- Etch and cleaning chemistries
- Advanced memory-node materials

## What To Watch

- Qualification at next-generation nodes
- Korean memory capex
- Group restructuring
