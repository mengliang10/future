## Investment Thesis

IQVIA runs clinical trials and sells healthcare data, analytics and commercial services to pharma. Its datasets cover prescriptions, claims and clinical records, which support trial recruitment and market access. Revenue is contracted and less cyclical than lab tools, but biotech funding affects trial starts.

## Technology Exposure

- Clinical-trial design and execution
- Healthcare data and analytics
- Commercial and consulting services

## What To Watch

- Trial starts and biotech funding
- Data-privacy regulation
- Backlog conversion
