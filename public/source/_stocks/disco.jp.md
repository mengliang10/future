## Investment Thesis

Disco makes the grinders and dicers that thin wafers and separate dies, steps that became critical with high-bandwidth memory and chip stacking. Its tools process the thinnest wafers in production, and its margins reflect a narrow competitive field. As packaging becomes the main lever for AI performance, Disco's addressable role grows.

## Technology Exposure

- Wafer grinding and dicing equipment.
- Process know-how for HBM and chiplet packaging.

## What To Watch

- HBM capacity additions.
- Competition from Chinese toolmakers at mature steps.
