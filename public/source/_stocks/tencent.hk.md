## Investment Thesis

Tencent reaches more than a billion users through WeChat and monetises them through games, advertising and fintech. Its AI strategy is distribution-led: Hunyuan models are embedded in existing products rather than sold as standalone APIs. That keeps customer acquisition cheap but makes AI revenue hard to separate from the advertising and gaming lines that already exist, and it exposes the group to Chinese advertising cycles.

## Technology Exposure

- Consumer-scale inference across WeChat, games and payments.
- Enterprise AI through Tencent Cloud and Hunyuan API access.

## What To Watch

- Advertising growth attributable to AI-ranked inventory.
- Capex guidance and domestic accelerator supply.
