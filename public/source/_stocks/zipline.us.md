Zipline operates autonomous fixed-wing drones that drop packages by parachute or lower them on a tether. It started delivering blood and medicines in Rwanda and Ghana, where road access is slow.

Its Platform 2 aircraft serves retail and food delivery in the United States, working with partners such as Walmart. Operations combine aircraft, docking stations and air-traffic integration.

**Why it matters**

- Long-range fixed-wing drones

- Medical and retail delivery

- Own logistics and airspace software
