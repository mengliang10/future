Serve Robotics operates four-wheeled sidewalk robots for local food delivery, with Uber Eats as its anchor partner. It went public in 2024.

The robots drive autonomously on pavements with remote human supervision for edge cases, and the company sells delivery as a service to merchants and platforms.

**Why it matters**

- Sidewalk delivery fleet

- Uber Eats integration

- Remote supervision model
