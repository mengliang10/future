## Investment Thesis

PolyPeptide makes peptides for pharmaceutical and biotech customers, with capacity in Europe, the US and India. It benefits from the shift toward peptide-based metabolic and endocrine drugs, where manufacturing know-how is scarce. Capacity utilisation and customer concentration drive results.

## Technology Exposure

- Peptide contract manufacturing
- Generic peptide APIs
- Clinical and commercial supply

## What To Watch

- Utilisation of new capacity
- GLP-1 demand
- Pricing pressure
