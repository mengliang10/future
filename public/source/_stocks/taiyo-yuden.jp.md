## Investment Thesis

Taiyo Yuden produces multilayer ceramic capacitors, power inductors and RF modules for handsets, servers and automotive customers. It competes with Murata and Samsung Electro-Mechanics in passives, where miniaturisation and capacitance density set the roadmap. AI servers and electrified vehicles are the main content-growth drivers.

## Technology Exposure

- Multilayer ceramic capacitors
- Power inductors
- RF and communication modules

## What To Watch

- AI-server passive content
- Handset and automotive demand
- Capacity and pricing
