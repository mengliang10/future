## Investment Thesis

Hugging Face became the default distribution point for open-weight models: labs publish there, developers download there, and the Hub's download counts are the closest thing the open ecosystem has to a usage statistic. It monetises through enterprise hosting, private model repositories and compute partnerships, while the free public Hub carries the community. Its position is strategic because it sits between model producers and the companies deploying them.

## Technology Exposure

- Model and dataset hub
- Transformers and inference libraries
- Enterprise Hub and hosted inference

## What To Watch

- Monetisation of a largely free community
- Competition from cloud model catalogues
- Licensing and content moderation of hosted models
