## Investment Thesis

Pure Storage sells all-flash arrays and a subscription model that includes hardware refresh, which turns a capex purchase into an operating expense. Its FlashBlade line is used for AI training data and checkpoint storage, and the company has been positioning around energy efficiency and rack density as data-centre power becomes constrained. Competition comes from Dell, NetApp and cloud-native storage.

## Technology Exposure

- All-flash arrays and FlashBlade
- Evergreen subscription and services
- AI data pipeline storage

## What To Watch

- Subscription mix and renewal rates
- AI storage win rates against hyperscalers
- Gross margin under hardware cost pressure
