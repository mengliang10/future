## Investment Thesis

Iridium operates a cross-linked LEO constellation providing voice, data and IoT connectivity, including safety services and government communications. Its network is fully deployed, so capital intensity is lower than for constellation builders. Subscriber growth in IoT and aviation is the main value driver.

## Technology Exposure

- Satellite voice and data services
- IoT connectivity
- Government and aviation communications

## What To Watch

- IoT subscriber growth
- Government contract renewals
- Competition from direct-to-cell services
