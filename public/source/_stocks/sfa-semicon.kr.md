## Investment Thesis

SFA Semicon packages and tests memory devices for Korean customers and operates overseas assembly capacity. The business is closely tied to memory production volumes and to the group's automation and equipment heritage. Margins are sensitive to utilisation and to memory pricing negotiations.

## Technology Exposure

- Memory assembly and test
- Module assembly
- Overseas packaging capacity

## What To Watch

- Memory production volumes
- Utilisation and pricing
- Group restructuring
