## Investment Thesis

AstraZeneca sells oncology, respiratory, cardiovascular and rare-disease medicines, with a growing China business and a large pipeline of antibody-drug conjugates and bispecifics. It has expanded through acquisitions and partnerships, and its manufacturing footprint spans the UK, Europe, the US and Asia. China exposure and patent expiries are the main risks.

## Technology Exposure

- Oncology and antibody-drug conjugates
- Respiratory and cardiovascular medicines
- Rare disease and China operations

## What To Watch

- China revenue and policy
- Oncology pipeline readouts
- Patent expiries
