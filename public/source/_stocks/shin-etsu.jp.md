## Investment Thesis

Shin-Etsu produces silicon wafers, photoresists, synthetic quartz, silicones, PVC and rare-earth magnets, giving it exposure to several constrained supply chains at once. Its wafer and resist positions are built on process control rather than patents alone, and it has stayed profitable through semiconductor downturns. The group's breadth makes it a proxy for materials intensity across the industry.

## Technology Exposure

- Silicon wafers for logic and memory
- Photoresists and lithography materials
- Silicones, PVC and rare-earth magnets

## What To Watch

- Wafer pricing and capacity
- EUV resist qualification
- Rare-earth and energy costs
