## Investment Thesis

01.AI released competitive open-weight Yi models, then narrowed its ambition from training frontier models to building applications and backing other AI companies. The pivot reflects the cost of staying at the frontier without hyperscaler distribution. Its remaining relevance is as a test case for whether second-tier labs can find a viable niche in China's crowded market.
