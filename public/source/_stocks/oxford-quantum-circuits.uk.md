## Investment Thesis

Oxford Quantum Circuits builds superconducting processors and offers cloud access to its system, with a focus on enterprise and public-sector users in the UK. It is one of several European quantum companies competing for the same government budgets and the same limited pool of cryogenic engineers. Its differentiator is a compact architecture aimed at easier scale-up.

## Technology Exposure

- Superconducting processors and cloud access.
- Quantum software partnerships.

## What To Watch

- Logical-qubit progress.
- UK and EU procurement wins.
