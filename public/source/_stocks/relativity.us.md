## Investment Thesis

Relativity Space aims to build rockets with a high proportion of additively manufactured parts, using large-format metal printers to reduce part count and lead time. Its Terran R medium-lift vehicle is intended to be reusable. The company has shifted focus and timelines, making execution and funding the central questions.

## Technology Exposure

- Terran R reusable launch vehicle
- Large-format additive manufacturing
- Aerospace structures and engines

## What To Watch

- Terran R first flight
- Funding and cash runway
- Additive-manufacturing yields
