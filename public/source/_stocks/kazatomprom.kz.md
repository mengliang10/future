## Investment Thesis

Kazatomprom produces uranium through in-situ leaching in Kazakhstan and sells to utilities and traders worldwide. It is the largest low-cost uranium producer, and its output decisions influence global prices. Ownership, tax and logistics links to Russia are persistent risks.

## Technology Exposure

- Uranium mining and concentrate
- Uranium sales to utilities
- Rare-metal by-products

## What To Watch

- Production guidance
- Uranium prices
- Export routes and sanctions
