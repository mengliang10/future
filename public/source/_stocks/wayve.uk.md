## Investment Thesis

Wayve raised US$1.05bn in 2024 to develop an end-to-end driving system that learns from fleet data rather than relying on explicit rules. It licenses the stack to automakers rather than operating its own fleet, which shifts the capital burden to partners. The approach needs large, diverse driving data, and Europe's regulatory fragmentation complicates deployment.

## Technology Exposure

- End-to-end driving software.
- Licensing to automakers.

## What To Watch

- OEM licensing contracts.
- Validation and safety-case approval.
