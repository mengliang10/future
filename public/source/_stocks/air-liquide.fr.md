## Investment Thesis

Air Liquide supplies ultra-high-purity gases, precursors and gas-delivery systems to semiconductor fabs, and is a major hydrogen and industrial-gas producer. Its electronics business grows with fab capex, while long-term contracts underpin cash flow. The company is also investing in hydrogen electrolysis and carbon capture.

## Technology Exposure

- Electronic specialty gases and precursors
- Hydrogen and industrial gases
- Gas delivery and abatement systems

## What To Watch

- Electronics project awards
- Hydrogen investment returns
- Energy costs in Europe
