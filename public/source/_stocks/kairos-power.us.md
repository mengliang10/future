Kairos Power is developing a high-temperature reactor cooled by molten fluoride salt, with a design intended to be simpler and safer than conventional light-water plants.

It is building a demonstration reactor in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, with support from the US Department of Energy and a power purchase agreement with Google.

**Why it matters**

- Fluoride-salt cooling

- High-temperature reactor

- Demonstration build in Tennessee
