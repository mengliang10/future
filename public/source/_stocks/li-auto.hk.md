## Investment Thesis

Li Auto built its position on extended-range electric vehicles, which carry a petrol generator to remove charging anxiety for large family cars. It has invested in in-house autonomous driving stacks and a charging network, and its margins depend on strong option take-up rather than the base vehicle. The strategy works while Chinese buyers prize range and space over charging speed.

## Technology Exposure

- EREV powertrains and family vehicle platforms.
- In-house AD software and ADAS features.

## What To Watch

- Margin per vehicle as competition intensifies.
- Take-up of paid AD features.
