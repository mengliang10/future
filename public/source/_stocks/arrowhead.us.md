## Investment Thesis

Arrowhead develops RNAi therapies for liver, lung and central-nervous-system targets, using its TRiM delivery platform. It has partnered programmes with large pharmaceutical companies and is advancing its own metabolic and cardiovascular candidates. Clinical readouts and partnership economics drive the stock.

## Technology Exposure

- RNAi therapies
- TRiM delivery platform
- Metabolic and cardiovascular programmes

## What To Watch

- Clinical data
- Partner milestones
- Financing needs
