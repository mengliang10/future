Form Energy's iron-air battery stores energy through reversible rusting, using iron, water and air instead of lithium. The target is multi-day storage that complements lithium-ion, which is economic for a few hours.

Its first commercial project is with a utility in Minnesota, and the company is building manufacturing capacity in West Virginia.

**Why it matters**

- Iron-air chemistry

- 100-hour duration target

- Utility-scale projects
