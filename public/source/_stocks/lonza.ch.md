## Investment Thesis

Lonza provides contract development and manufacturing for biologics, cell and gene therapies, and small molecules. It operates mammalian-cell and microbial facilities worldwide, and its capsules and nutrition business supplies the pharmaceutical and consumer industries. Utilization of large-scale biologic capacity drives margins.

## Technology Exposure

- Biologics contract manufacturing
- Cell and gene therapy manufacturing
- Capsules and specialty ingredients

## What To Watch

- Capacity utilisation
- CDMO competition from Samsung Biologics and WuXi
- Capex and debt
