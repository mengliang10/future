Tenstorrent designs AI accelerators and RISC-V CPU cores, led by chip architect Jim Keller. It sells chips, systems and IP licences.

The strategy is to offer an open, licensable alternative to closed accelerator stacks, with a focus on inference and on customers who want to own part of the design.

**Why it matters**

- RISC-V CPU IP

- AI accelerators and systems

- Open software stack
