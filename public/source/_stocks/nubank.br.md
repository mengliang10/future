## Investment Thesis

Nubank built a branchless bank in Brazil and expanded into Mexico and Colombia, using app distribution and data-driven credit to undercut incumbent fees. It passed 100 million customers and turned profitable, which is rare among digital challengers. Growth now depends on lending mix and on holding credit losses steady as it moves upmarket.

## Technology Exposure

- Digital banking and credit.
- Payments and insurance distribution.

## What To Watch

- Credit loss provisions as the book seasons.
- Expansion economics in Mexico and Colombia.
