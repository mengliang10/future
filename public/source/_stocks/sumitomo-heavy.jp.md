## Investment Thesis

Sumitomo Heavy Industries builds cryocoolers, accelerators and precision machinery alongside ships, construction equipment and injection-moulding machines. Its cryocoolers are used in superconducting magnets, semiconductor tools and quantum systems. The industrial base is cyclical, but the cryogenic niche is strategically relevant.

## Technology Exposure

- Cryocoolers and cryogenic systems
- Accelerator and beamline components
- Industrial machinery and ships

## What To Watch

- Cryocooler demand from quantum and medical
- Industrial machinery cycle
- Energy costs
