## Investment Thesis

Bachem produces peptides for clinical trials and commercial drugs, including the long-acting incretin peptides behind obesity therapies. Its solid-phase and liquid-phase synthesis capacity is a constraint on the GLP-1 supply chain. The company has been expanding plants in Switzerland and the US.

## Technology Exposure

- Peptide active pharmaceutical ingredients
- Clinical-trial peptide supply
- Oligonucleotide and complex chemistry

## What To Watch

- Peptide capacity expansion
- GLP-1 demand
- Customer concentration
