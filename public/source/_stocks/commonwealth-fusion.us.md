## Investment Thesis

Commonwealth Fusion Systems is building the SPARC tokamak to demonstrate net energy gain using high-temperature superconducting magnets, with a planned ARC power plant. It has raised large private rounds and partners with MIT. Magnet performance and plasma physics are the key risks.

## Technology Exposure

- Tokamak fusion development
- High-temperature superconducting magnets
- Power-plant design

## What To Watch

- SPARC net-energy demonstration
- ARC financing
- Magnet manufacturing
