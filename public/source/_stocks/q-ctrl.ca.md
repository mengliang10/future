Q-CTRL builds control software that improves the performance of quantum hardware by shaping the signals that drive qubits, plus tools for quantum sensing and education.

Its approach is hardware-agnostic: the same control layer can run on superconducting, trapped-ion or neutral-atom machines.

**Why it matters**

- Qubit control optimisation

- Error suppression

- Quantum sensing applications
