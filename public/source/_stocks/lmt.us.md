## Investment Thesis

Lockheed Martin builds satellites, missile systems, aircraft and space sensors, and co-owns United Launch Alliance with Boeing. Its space business spans national-security satellites, hypersonic tracking and Orion crew systems. Defence budgets and programme execution dominate results.

## Technology Exposure

- National-security satellites and sensors
- Missile and missile-defence systems
- Orion and space exploration systems

## What To Watch

- Defence budget and continuing resolutions
- Satellite programme execution
- ULA ownership transition
