## Investment Thesis

Mirion Technologies produces radiation detectors, dosimetry and monitoring systems for nuclear plants, hospitals, defence and research labs. Safety and regulatory requirements make its instruments mandatory rather than discretionary. Nuclear plant life extensions and medical imaging growth support demand.

## Technology Exposure

- Radiation detection and monitoring
- Dosimetry and personal monitoring
- Nuclear-medicine instrumentation
