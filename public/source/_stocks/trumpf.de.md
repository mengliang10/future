## Investment Thesis

TRUMPF builds industrial lasers, machine tools and photonics systems, and supplies the drive laser used in ASML's EUV lithography tools through a long-standing partnership. Its laser expertise also serves battery welding, additive manufacturing and quantum applications. The company is family-owned and one of Europe's largest machine-tool makers.

## Technology Exposure

- EUV drive lasers
- Industrial lasers and machine tools
- Laser welding and additive systems
