## Investment Thesis

Nepes offers bumping, wafer-level packaging and test services, and has been developing panel-level packaging for larger AI packages. It sits among the smaller OSATs but works close to Korean fabless and memory customers, which gives it early visibility on new package formats. Capital intensity and customer concentration are the constraints.

## Technology Exposure

- Wafer-level packaging and bumping
- Panel-level packaging development
- Test services

## What To Watch

- Panel-level packaging qualification
- Korean fabless customer growth
- Funding of capex
