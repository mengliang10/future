## Investment Thesis

Caribou Biosciences engineers donor-derived CAR-T and NK cell therapies using CRISPR, aiming for off-the-shelf treatments for cancer and autoimmune disease. Its chRDNA editing platform is designed to reduce chromosomal translocations. Clinical data and cash runway are critical.

## Technology Exposure

- Allogeneic CAR-T therapies
- CRISPR cell engineering
- Autoimmune and oncology programmes
