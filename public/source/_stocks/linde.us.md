## Investment Thesis

Linde supplies nitrogen, oxygen, argon, hydrogen and specialty gases through on-site plants and pipelines, including the ultra-high-purity gases fabs cannot operate without. Long-term take-or-pay contracts make revenue stable, while electronics and clean-energy projects add growth. Gas supply is a quiet precondition for both semiconductor capacity and rocket launch operations.

## Technology Exposure

- Ultra-high-purity gases for semiconductor fabs
- Hydrogen and industrial gases for refining and industry
- Cryogenic and launch-site gas supply

## What To Watch

- Hydrogen project economics
- Contract renewals and pricing
