## Investment Thesis

ispace attempted the first private lunar landing in 2023; the lander crashed in the final descent, and the company has since raised and rebuilt for further missions. It sells payload capacity to governments and companies rather than running its own science programme. The business is a bet that lunar delivery becomes a routine service with recurring customers.

## Technology Exposure

- Commercial lunar landers and payload services.
- Micro-rover delivery for national space agencies.

## What To Watch

- Landing success and schedule.
- Signed payload backlog versus cash burn.
