## Investment Thesis

Wonik IPS supplies deposition, etch and cleaning equipment to Korean semiconductor manufacturers, with a focus on memory processes. Domestic qualification gives it a cost and service advantage, but its customer base is narrow. The company has been expanding into advanced packaging and overseas customers.

## Technology Exposure

- Deposition and etch equipment
- Cleaning and thermal tools
- Advanced packaging equipment

## What To Watch

- Korean memory capex
- Customer diversification
- Competition from global toolmakers
