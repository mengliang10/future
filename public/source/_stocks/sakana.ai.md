## Investment Thesis

Sakana AI, founded by former Google researchers, works on model merging, evolutionary search and automated research rather than training ever-larger models from scratch. The approach assumes that combining capable open models can beat scaling a single one at a fraction of the compute. Its 'AI Scientist' work made automated paper generation a visible research topic, and commercialisation is still early.

## Technology Exposure

- Evolutionary model merging
- Automated research workflows
- Small efficient models for Japanese users

## What To Watch

- Commercial path for research output
- Funding and compute constraints
- Reproducibility of published methods
