## Investment Thesis

Repligen makes filtration, chromatography and process-analytics products used in biologic manufacturing, including protein A ligands and single-use systems. Its consumables scale with biologic output and process intensity. Bioprocessing destocking and customer concentration are the main risks.

## Technology Exposure

- Protein A chromatography ligands
- Filtration and single-use systems
- Process analytics

## What To Watch

- Biologic production volumes
- Customer inventory levels
- Competition
