## Investment Thesis

Tokyo Ohka Kogyo supplies photoresists, developers and high-purity process chemicals for semiconductor and display manufacturing. It has a long record in advanced lithography and has been qualifying EUV and high-NA resists with scanner and fab partners. The business is tied to wafer starts but benefits from rising resist intensity per wafer.

## Technology Exposure

- Photoresists for advanced lithography
- Developers and process chemicals
- Display materials

## What To Watch

- EUV and high-NA resist qualification
- Wafer-start volumes
- Raw-material and energy costs
