## Investment Thesis

Gerresheimer supplies vials, syringes, inhalers and injection devices to pharma and biotech, with a growing focus on high-value delivery systems for biologics and GLP-1 therapies. It has been expanding moulded-glass and device capacity. Energy costs and customer concentration affect margins.

## Technology Exposure

- Moulded and tubular glass packaging
- Injection and inhalation devices
- Medical plastic systems

## What To Watch

- GLP-1 device demand
- Energy costs
- Capacity utilisation
