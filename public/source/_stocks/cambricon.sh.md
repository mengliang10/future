## Investment Thesis

Cambricon designs machine-learning accelerators for cloud and edge inference. It was added to the US entity list in 2022, which cut access to advanced foundry and EDA tools, and its revenue is concentrated in government, telecom and research procurement. The company is a useful proxy for how far domestic AI silicon has progressed without access to the leading process nodes.

## Technology Exposure

- Domestic inference accelerators for public-sector workloads.
- Software stack for Chinese framework compatibility.

## What To Watch

- Revenue concentration and collection periods.
- Software maturity relative to CUDA.
