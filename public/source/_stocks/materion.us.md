## Investment Thesis

Materion makes beryllium products, specialty alloys, tantalum and precision optics for aerospace, defence, semiconductor and space customers. Beryllium's stiffness-to-weight ratio makes it useful in satellite structures and optical systems, and supply is tightly controlled. The company also supplies semiconductor deposition materials.

## Technology Exposure

- Beryllium and specialty alloys
- Precision optics and thin-film materials
- Semiconductor deposition materials

## What To Watch

- Defence and space demand
- Beryllium supply and regulation
- Semiconductor materials growth
