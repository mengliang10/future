## Investment Thesis

Infosys runs large engineering and operations contracts for Western enterprises and is embedding generative AI into delivery through its Topaz platform. The commercial risk is deflationary: if AI reduces the billable hours per project, revenue falls unless the firm sells outcomes instead of effort. It is the clearest test of whether services firms capture or lose the AI margin.

## Technology Exposure

- Application development and managed services.
- AI platform and transformation programmes.

## What To Watch

- Revenue per employee as AI tooling spreads.
- Large-deal total contract value.
