## Investment Thesis

JX Metals produces high-purity sputtering targets, bonding wire, copper foil and compound-semiconductor substrates used in chip fabrication and packaging. Its materials sit in deposition and interconnection steps where purity determines yield, and it has been expanding targets for advanced packaging and power devices. The company also retains mining and smelting operations.

## Technology Exposure

- Sputtering targets for deposition
- Bonding wire and copper foil
- Compound-semiconductor substrates

## What To Watch

- Target demand from advanced nodes
- Copper and precious-metal prices
- Power-device substrate demand
