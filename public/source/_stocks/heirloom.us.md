Heirloom accelerates the natural weathering of limestone to absorb CO2, then releases it in a concentrated stream for storage. The process uses widely available material and modular contactors.

The company operates pilot and commercial-scale facilities and sells removals to buyers including Microsoft.

**Why it matters**

- Enhanced weathering with limestone

- Modular contactors

- Removal contracts
