## Investment Thesis

PsiQuantum is building a fault-tolerant photonic quantum computer using silicon photonics and single-photon sources, with fabrication partners in the semiconductor industry. Its plan requires millions of physical qubits and significant cryogenic detector infrastructure, making it one of the most capital-intensive quantum programmes. Government and industrial investors underwrite the effort.

## Technology Exposure

- Photonic quantum processors
- Single-photon sources and detectors
- Cryogenic photonic packaging

## What To Watch

- Fault-tolerance milestone timing
- Manufacturing partnerships
- Government funding
