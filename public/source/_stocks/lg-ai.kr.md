## Investment Thesis

LG AI Research builds Exaone models with an emphasis on materials, chemistry and manufacturing applications across LG's affiliates, and has released open weights for some versions. The industrial focus is a genuine differentiator: a model that predicts material properties or diagnoses equipment has a buyer inside the group before it needs an external market. LG Corp is the listed parent.

## Technology Exposure

- Exaone foundation models
- Materials and chemistry AI
- Industrial and manufacturing applications

## What To Watch
