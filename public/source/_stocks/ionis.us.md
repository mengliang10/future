## Investment Thesis

Ionis develops antisense medicines that modulate RNA processing, with approved rare-disease drugs and partnered programmes with large pharma. Its pipeline spans cardiovascular, neurological and metabolic disease, and licensing revenue funds research. Commercial execution has historically lagged the science.

## Technology Exposure

- Antisense oligonucleotide therapies
- Neurology and cardiovascular pipeline
- Partnered programmes and licensing

## What To Watch

- Own-drug launch execution
- Partner milestone payments
- Pipeline readouts
