Sila produces silicon anode material, branded Titan Silicon, that replaces some or all of the graphite in a lithium-ion cell. Silicon stores far more lithium by weight, which translates into range.

The material is designed to drop into existing cell manufacturing, and the company has automotive qualification programmes with major carmakers.

**Why it matters**

- Silicon anode powder

- Drop-in cell compatibility

- Automotive qualification
