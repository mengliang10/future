QuantumScape has developed a solid-state battery that replaces the liquid electrolyte and graphite anode with a ceramic separator and a lithium-metal anode.

The company licenses its technology to battery manufacturers rather than mass-producing cells itself, and has shipped sample cells to automotive partners for testing.

**Why it matters**

- Ceramic separator

- Lithium-metal anode

- Licensing to cell makers
