SiFive develops RISC-V CPU cores and platform IP that chip designers license and integrate. RISC-V is an open instruction set, so customers avoid the licensing constraints of proprietary architectures.

The company's growth tracks the widening use of custom silicon in AI, automotive and embedded systems.

**Why it matters**

- RISC-V CPU IP

- Custom SoC platforms

- Automotive and AI designs
