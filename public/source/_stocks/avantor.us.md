## Investment Thesis

Avantor distributes laboratory chemicals, consumables and equipment, and supplies high-purity materials to bioprocessing and semiconductor customers. Its broad catalogue makes it a one-stop procurement channel for labs. Leverage and low margins are structural features.

## Technology Exposure

- Laboratory chemicals and consumables
- Bioprocessing raw materials
- Distribution services

## What To Watch

- Lab spending
- Biotech funding
- Debt reduction
