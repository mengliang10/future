## Investment Thesis

Tokamak Energy is developing a compact spherical tokamak using high-temperature superconducting magnets, a design it argues can reach fusion conditions in a smaller, cheaper machine. It demonstrated plasma temperatures above 100 million degrees in 2022 and is designing a pilot plant. Fusion timelines in private companies remain aspirational, and no design has produced net electricity.

## Technology Exposure

- Spherical tokamak and HTS magnet technology.
- Fusion pilot-plant design.

## What To Watch

- Magnet performance at scale.
- Funding runway to the next machine.
