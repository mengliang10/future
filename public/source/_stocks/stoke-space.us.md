Stoke Space is building a two-stage rocket whose second stage returns to Earth and lands, aiming at rapid and complete reuse rather than only booster recovery.

Its engine and heat-shield designs are aimed at aircraft-like turnaround, which is the main cost lever left after booster reuse.

**Why it matters**

- Reusable second stage

- Regeneratively cooled heat shield

- Rapid turnaround target
