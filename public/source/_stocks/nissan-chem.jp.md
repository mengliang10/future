## Investment Thesis

Nissan Chemical produces anti-reflective coatings, resist underlayers, CMP slurries and specialty chemicals for semiconductor and display manufacturing. These materials are less visible than photoresists but sit in every advanced patterning flow, and qualification is sticky once established. Agrochemicals and pharmaceuticals add balance.
