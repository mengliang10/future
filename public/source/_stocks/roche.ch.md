## Investment Thesis

Roche sells oncology, immunology, neuroscience and ophthalmology medicines and is the world's largest in-vitro diagnostics company. Diagnostics and pharmaceuticals reinforce each other in companion testing, and the group has a deep pipeline in oncology and haemophilia. Currency and pricing pressure weigh on results.

## Technology Exposure

- Oncology and haematology medicines
- In-vitro diagnostics
- Companion diagnostics and sequencing

## What To Watch

- Diagnostics reimbursement
- Oncology pipeline readouts
- Currency and pricing
