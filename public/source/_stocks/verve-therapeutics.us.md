Verve Therapeutics uses base editing to switch off genes that raise LDL cholesterol, aiming for a single treatment instead of lifelong medication.

Its lead programmes target PCSK9 and ANGPTL3, and the company is running early clinical studies.

**Why it matters**

- Base editing

- Cardiovascular gene therapy

- One-time dosing
