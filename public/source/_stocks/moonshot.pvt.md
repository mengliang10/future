## Investment Thesis

Moonshot AI built the Kimi assistant around long-context reading and then released open-weight mixture-of-experts models, including a trillion-parameter system with a small active parameter count per token. That design keeps inference affordable while remaining competitive on reasoning and coding tasks. The company is venture-funded and competes with DeepSeek and Alibaba for talent and compute rather than for consumer distribution.

## Technology Exposure

- Long-context document and code understanding.
- Open-weight mixture-of-experts models for cost-efficient serving.

## What To Watch

- Talent retention and funding runway.
- Serving cost at long context lengths.
- Enterprise adoption outside consumer chat.
