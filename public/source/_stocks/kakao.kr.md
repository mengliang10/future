## Investment Thesis

Kakao owns the dominant messaging app in Korea, which gives it a distribution channel for assistants, search and commerce features built on its own language models. The AI effort is defensive as much as offensive: if conversational interfaces absorb search and messaging, the platform must own the model layer to keep the user relationship. Competition from Naver and global assistants is direct.

## Technology Exposure

- Korean-language foundation models
- KakaoTalk assistant and search features
- Content and commerce services

## What To Watch

- Monetisation of AI features without user churn
- Competition with Naver's HyperCLOVA
- Content and platform regulation
