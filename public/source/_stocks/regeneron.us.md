## Investment Thesis

Regeneron develops antibody therapies for eye disease, eczema, asthma and cancer, and runs one of the largest human-genetics databases through its Regeneron Genetics Center. Its collaboration with Sanofi on Dupixent is a major profit source, and it is expanding into oncology and gene therapy. Pipeline concentration is a risk.

## Technology Exposure

- Antibody medicines for immunology and eye disease
- Oncology combinations
- Genetics-driven drug discovery

## What To Watch

- Dupixent economics
- Pipeline readouts
- Pricing and competition
