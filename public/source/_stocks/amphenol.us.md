## Investment Thesis

Amphenol produces electrical and fibre-optic connectors, sensors and cable assemblies for harsh environments, including launch vehicles, satellites and data-centre racks. Its connectors must survive vibration, vacuum and thermal cycling, which narrows the supplier field. The company grows through acquisitions and content gains in electronics.

## Technology Exposure

- High-reliability connectors
- Fibre-optic and RF interconnects
- Sensors and cable assemblies

## What To Watch

- Aerospace and defence demand
- Data-centre interconnect growth
- Acquisition integration
