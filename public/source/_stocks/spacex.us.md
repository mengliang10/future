## Investment Thesis

SpaceX designs and builds Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy and Starship, and operates the Starlink constellation. Its vertical integration is unusual: engines, avionics, structures and much of the ground equipment are made in-house, so external suppliers are concentrated in materials, components and machine tools. Starship's stainless-steel architecture and rapid reflight cadence are reshaping demand for cryogenic materials and propulsion test infrastructure.

## Technology Exposure

- Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy launch services
- Starship heavy-lift development
- Starlink satellite broadband

## What To Watch

- Starship flight-test cadence
- Starlink subscriber growth and capex
- Launch pricing and competition
