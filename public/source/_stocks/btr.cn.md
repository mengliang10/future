## Investment Thesis

BTR New Material produces anode materials, including natural and synthetic graphite and silicon-carbon composites, for lithium-ion cells. It is one of the largest anode suppliers globally and has been expanding overseas to serve non-Chinese battery plants. Customer concentration and graphite pricing shape results.

## Technology Exposure

- Natural and synthetic graphite anode
- Silicon-carbon anode materials
- Overseas anode capacity

## What To Watch

- Silicon-anode adoption
- Graphite pricing and export rules
- Overseas plant ramp
