## Investment Thesis

Wonik Materials produces specialty gases such as nitrous oxide, tungsten precursors and high-purity chemicals for semiconductor manufacturing. Korean memory fabs are its core customers, so node transitions and fab utilisation drive volume. The company has been broadening its product list to reduce dependence on a few gases.

## Technology Exposure

- Specialty gases for deposition and etch
- High-purity precursors
- Industrial gases

## What To Watch

- Memory fab utilisation
- New gas qualifications
- Competitor capacity additions
