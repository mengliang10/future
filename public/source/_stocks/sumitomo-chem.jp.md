## Investment Thesis

Sumitomo Chemical produces display materials, photoresists, battery materials and agricultural chemicals through its subsidiaries. Its OLED and touch-panel materials serve Korean and Chinese panel makers, while semiconductor materials serve fabs. The group has been restructuring to improve returns.

## Technology Exposure

- OLED and display materials
- Photoresists and electronic chemicals
- Battery and agricultural materials

## What To Watch

- Display-material demand
- Restructuring progress
- Petrochemical spreads
