## Investment Thesis

Replit combines a hosted IDE, deployment and an agent that can generate and run applications from a prompt. Its audience spans students, hobbyists and small businesses that want a working app without managing infrastructure, which gives it a distribution channel outside professional engineering. Infrastructure cost and the reliability of generated code at scale are the constraints on converting usage into margin.

## Technology Exposure

- Browser IDE and hosting
- Replit Agent app generation
- Education and small-business plans

## What To Watch

- Paid conversion and compute cost per user
- Agent output quality beyond prototypes
- Competition from model vendors' app builders
