## Investment Thesis

Glean indexes a company's documents, messages and applications, then exposes them through search and an assistant with permission-aware retrieval. That permission layer is the hard part: an assistant that leaks restricted content is unusable, and connecting dozens of SaaS tools takes real integration work. The company sells per-seat subscriptions and is expanding into agents that act on the retrieved context.

## Technology Exposure

- Enterprise search and assistant
- Connectors and permission model
- Agent actions across SaaS tools

## What To Watch

- Seat growth and renewal
- Differentiation from Microsoft and Google assistants
- Cost of indexing and retrieval
