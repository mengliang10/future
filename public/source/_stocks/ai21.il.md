## Investment Thesis

AI21 differentiates with a hybrid architecture that mixes state-space layers with attention, aiming to hold long context at lower inference cost than pure transformer models. It sells enterprise assistants and APIs rather than consumer products, and its home market brings security and government customers. Competing against far better-funded labs on general capability is the persistent problem.

## Technology Exposure

- Jamba hybrid models
- Enterprise assistants and APIs
- Hebrew and multilingual models

## What To Watch

- Enterprise traction against larger labs
- Cost advantage translating into contracts
- Funding runway
