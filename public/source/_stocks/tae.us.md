TAE Technologies pursues a field-reversed configuration, a compact plasma shape that can be heated and sustained with neutral beams. The company has run a series of prototype machines and is designing a larger demonstration.

It also develops power-management and beam technology that has applications beyond fusion.

**Why it matters**

- Field-reversed configuration

- Neutral-beam heating

- Compact reactor design
