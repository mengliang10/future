## Investment Thesis

KCC produces paints, silicone, quartz and specialty materials, including silicone products used in electronics and thermal management. Its construction and paint businesses are cyclical, while electronic materials offer higher margins. The company has been investing in semiconductor and battery materials adjacencies.

## Technology Exposure

- Silicone and thermal-management materials
- Paints and coatings
- Quartz and specialty materials

## What To Watch

- Electronic-materials qualification
- Construction and paint demand
- Silicone pricing
