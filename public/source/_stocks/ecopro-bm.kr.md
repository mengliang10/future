## Investment Thesis

Ecopro BM produces high-nickel cathode active materials for EV batteries and has been expanding capacity in Korea and through overseas ventures. High-nickel cathodes improve range but demand tighter process control and precursor quality. Customer concentration and metal-price swings drive earnings.

## Technology Exposure

- High-nickel cathode active materials
- Cathode precursors
- Recycling partnerships

## What To Watch

- High-nickel qualification
- Customer concentration
- Nickel and lithium prices
