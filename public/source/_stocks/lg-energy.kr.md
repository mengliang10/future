## Investment Thesis

LG Energy Solution supplies pouch and prismatic cells to major automakers and has built joint-venture plants across North America, where local-content rules support pricing. It is expanding lithium-iron-phosphate and storage products to defend share against Chinese cost leaders. Profitability is heavily influenced by US production tax credits, which makes policy a first-order variable.

## Technology Exposure

- EV cells for global automakers.
- Grid storage and LFP products.

## What To Watch

- Utilisation as EV demand growth slows.
- Exposure to changes in US credit rules.
