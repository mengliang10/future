## Investment Thesis

Lumus makes reflective waveguides, the optical layer that carries an image from a projector to the wearer's eye while keeping the lens transparent. Reflective designs can offer a wider field of view and better efficiency than diffractive alternatives, but manufacturing yield at large size has historically been difficult. The company licenses and supplies to glasses makers rather than selling to consumers, so its fortunes track the whole AR hardware cycle.

## Technology Exposure

- Reflective waveguides and optical engines
- Licensing to AR device makers
- Defence and industrial optics

## What To Watch

- Design wins at volume manufacturers
- Manufacturing yield and cost per unit
- Competition from diffractive waveguide suppliers
