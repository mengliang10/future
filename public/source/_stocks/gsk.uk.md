## Investment Thesis

GSK sells vaccines, HIV therapies through ViiV, respiratory and oncology medicines, and consumer-health products. It has been rebuilding its pipeline through acquisitions and is a major vaccine supplier globally. Litigation and patent expiries are recurring issues.

## Technology Exposure

- HIV and antiviral therapies
- Respiratory and oncology medicines

## What To Watch

- HIV franchise competition
- Litigation and patent expiries
