## Investment Thesis

Rohde & Schwarz makes signal generators, spectrum analysers, oscilloscopes and secure communications systems, and has extended into quantum control through Zurich Instruments. Its RF expertise maps directly onto qubit control and readout, where signal purity matters. The company is family-owned and also supplies defence electronics.

## Technology Exposure

- Signal generators and analysers
- Quantum control instrumentation
- Secure communications and defence electronics

## What To Watch

- Quantum instrumentation growth
- Defence and communications demand
- Competition from US instrument makers
