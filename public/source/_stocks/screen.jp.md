## Investment Thesis

SCREEN Holdings makes batch and single-wafer cleaning tools and coat/develop tracks that apply and develop photoresist. Cleaning steps multiply at advanced nodes, and its tracks are paired with leading-edge scanners. The company also has graphic-arts and display equipment businesses.

## Technology Exposure

- Single-wafer and batch cleaning
- Coat and develop tracks
- Display and graphic-arts equipment
