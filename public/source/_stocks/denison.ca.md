## Investment Thesis

Denison Mines is developing the Wheeler River uranium project in Saskatchewan and holds exploration and processing interests. Its Phoenix deposit is designed as an in-situ recovery operation, which could lower capital cost. Permitting and uranium prices determine the timeline.
