## Investment Thesis

UBTech listed in Hong Kong in 2023 and has positioned its Walker humanoids for inspection and material handling inside car plants, with pilot programmes at Chinese automakers. Revenue still comes mainly from education and logistics robots, so the humanoid is an option on factory automation rather than a shipping product line. The company is a useful read on how quickly Chinese factories accept embodied automation.

## Technology Exposure

- Humanoid pilots in automotive assembly and inspection.
- Service and education robotics revenue.

## What To Watch

- Conversion of pilots into paid fleet orders.
- Cost per unit and duty cycle in real plants.
