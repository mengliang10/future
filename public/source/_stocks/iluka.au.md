## Investment Thesis

Iluka produces zircon and titanium dioxide feedstocks and is developing a rare-earth refinery in Western Australia to separate neodymium, praseodymium and heavier elements. The project would add non-Chinese separation capacity for magnet supply chains. Mineral-sands pricing and project delivery drive the stock.
