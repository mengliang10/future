## Investment Thesis

Lintec produces adhesive films, release papers and semiconductor-processing tapes, including dicing and die-attach films. Its materials are used in wafer thinning, dicing and package assembly, and it has been developing films for panel-level packaging. The business is tied to semiconductor and display production volumes.

## Technology Exposure

- Dicing and die-attach films
- Release films and adhesives
- Panel-level packaging materials

## What To Watch

- Advanced-packaging material demand
- Display production
- Raw-material costs
