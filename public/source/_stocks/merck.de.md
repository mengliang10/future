## Investment Thesis

Merck's Electronics division supplies deposition precursors, photoresists, specialty gases, CMP slurries and delivery systems, making it one of the broadest semiconductor-materials portfolios in the industry. It has invested heavily in US, German and Asian capacity to support leading-edge fabs. Healthcare and Life Science provide stability.

## Technology Exposure

- Deposition precursors and specialty gases
- Photoresists and patterning materials
- CMP slurries and delivery systems

## What To Watch

- Electronics capacity additions
- Leading-edge node qualifications
- Healthcare and Life Science performance
