## Investment Thesis

Aleph Alpha positions its Pharia models around transparency and European hosting, targeting government, defence and regulated industry that cannot use US APIs. It has shifted emphasis from frontier-scale training toward applied products and partnerships, which is a more realistic path for a lab without hyperscaler compute. Public funding and German industrial customers underwrite the effort.
