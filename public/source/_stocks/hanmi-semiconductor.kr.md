## Investment Thesis

Hanmi Semiconductor supplies thermocompression bonders, sawing and packaging equipment used to stack and attach memory dies, including HBM assembly. Its tools are qualified with Korean memory makers, so HBM capacity additions translate into orders. Competition from larger packaging-equipment vendors is intensifying.

## Technology Exposure

- Thermocompression bonders
- Sawing and die-attach equipment
- HBM packaging tools

## What To Watch

- HBM capacity additions
- Customer concentration
- Competitive pricing
