## Investment Thesis

Pfizer sells vaccines, antiviral therapies, oncology drugs and internal medicines, and acquired Seagen to add antibody-drug conjugates. The company is working through the post-pandemic revenue decline and integrating large acquisitions while cutting costs. Pipeline execution and debt reduction are the near-term issues.

## Technology Exposure

- Vaccines and antiviral therapies
- Oncology and antibody-drug conjugates
- Internal medicine and inflammation

## What To Watch

- Post-COVID revenue normalisation
- Oncology pipeline execution
- Cost cuts and debt
