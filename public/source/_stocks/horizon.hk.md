## Investment Thesis

Horizon designs the Journey series of automotive processors and the accompanying software stack, and listed in Hong Kong in 2024. Its newest generation targets highway and city navigation-assisted driving at volume price points, competing with Nvidia, Mobileye and in-house programmes at Chinese automakers. Revenue depends on a handful of vehicle programmes, so design wins matter more than headline TOPS.

## Technology Exposure

- ADAS and navigation-assisted driving compute.
- Software tooling for automakers building on Journey chips.

## What To Watch

- Number of production vehicle programmes per chip generation.
- Price pressure from Nvidia's scaled-down China products.
