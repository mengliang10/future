## Investment Thesis

Shanshan produces anode materials, cathodes and related battery materials, and has been one of the largest Chinese anode suppliers. It also holds interests in display and other materials businesses. The company's results track cell production and graphite prices.

## Technology Exposure

- Anode materials
- Cathode and electrolyte materials
- Display and other materials

## What To Watch

- Cell-maker demand
- Portfolio restructuring
