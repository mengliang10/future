## Investment Thesis

Agilent sells liquid and gas chromatography, mass spectrometry, spectroscopy and genomics products to pharma, environmental and industrial labs. Its instruments are used to characterise drug substances and to run quality control in manufacturing. Instrument demand tracks lab capex and biotech funding.

- Genomics and pathology
- Laboratory services and consumables
