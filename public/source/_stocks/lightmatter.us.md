Lightmatter develops silicon-photonic interconnect (Passage) and a photonic inference engine (Envise). The interconnect uses light to move data between chips and memory, where copper links are hitting distance and power limits.

Its bet is that the bottleneck in AI systems is moving data, not multiplying it, so optics enters the package before it replaces the compute.

**Why it matters**

- Photonic chip-to-chip interconnect

- Optical I/O for accelerator packages

- Photonic compute research
