## Investment Thesis

Cohere sells language models for enterprise use, with an emphasis on deployment inside a customer's own environment and on retrieval over private documents. That positioning competes with open-weight self-hosting rather than with consumer chatbots. Its challenge is that open models keep improving, compressing the premium for a proprietary mid-size model.

## Technology Exposure

- Enterprise language models and retrieval.
- Private and sovereign deployments.

## What To Watch

- Enterprise contract growth.
- Price pressure from open-weight models.
