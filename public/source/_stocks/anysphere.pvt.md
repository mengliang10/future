## Investment Thesis

Anysphere's Cursor embeds model-driven editing, multi-file changes and agentic task execution directly into the developer's editor. Its growth came from developers adopting it individually before enterprises bought seats, a bottom-up motion that incumbent tooling struggles to replicate. The company depends on third-party models for capability and competes with the model labs' own coding tools, which can bundle at lower marginal cost.

## Technology Exposure

- Cursor editor and agent mode
- Team and enterprise subscriptions
- Model routing across providers

## What To Watch

- Enterprise seat expansion
- Model cost as a share of revenue
- Bundling by model labs and IDEs
