## Investment Thesis

Daeduck Electronics produces high-density PCBs and package substrates, with a customer base among Korean memory and module makers. It has been adding substrate capacity for advanced packages while managing a mature PCB business exposed to handset and display cycles. Profitability swings with utilisation because fixed costs dominate.

## Technology Exposure

- Memory package substrates
- High-density and multilayer PCBs
- Module boards

## What To Watch

- Substrate capacity conversion
- Memory customer inventory cycles
- Input-material and energy costs
