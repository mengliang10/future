## Investment Thesis

Nanya makes conventional DRAM for consumer, networking and industrial applications, a segment where it competes on cost against Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron. It has invested in advanced DRAM nodes and custom high-value memory, but lacks the scale of the Korean leaders and does not lead in HBM. Its results are a relatively pure read on commodity DRAM pricing.

## Technology Exposure

- Commodity and specialty DRAM
- Networking and consumer memory
- Advanced-node DRAM transition

## What To Watch

- DRAM contract pricing
- Node transition cost and yield
- Exposure to Chinese DRAM capacity
