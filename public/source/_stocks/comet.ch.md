## Investment Thesis

Comet Holding supplies RF power generators, matching networks and X-ray inspection systems used in semiconductor etch and deposition tools. Its RF generators control plasma, which is central to etch and deposition uniformity. The company also serves industrial X-ray and security markets.

## Technology Exposure

- RF power generators and matching networks
- X-ray inspection modules
- Vacuum components

## What To Watch

- Etch and deposition capex
- Semiconductor equipment build rates
- Industrial X-ray demand
