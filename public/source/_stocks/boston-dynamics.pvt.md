Boston Dynamics was founded in 1992 as an MIT spin-out and has been owned by Hyundai Motor Group since 2021. Spot is a quadruped used for industrial inspection; Stretch is a box-handling robot for warehouses; Atlas is a research humanoid now built with electric actuators.

The company's advantage is controls and reliability engineering built over decades, while its commercial challenge is that each deployment still needs integration work.

**Why it matters**

- Spot for inspection in hazardous sites

- Stretch for container and truck unloading

- Atlas as a research and platform programme
