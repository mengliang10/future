## Investment Thesis

Baidu funds autonomous driving from a search and advertising business that AI assistants are steadily eroding. Apollo Go operates paid driverless rides in Wuhan and other cities, giving Baidu the largest robotaxi footprint in China, while ERNIE models were partially open-sourced in 2025 to defend developer relevance. The tension is structural: the cash engine is shrinking while the autonomy and model bets consume it.

## Technology Exposure

- Robotaxi operations and fleet partnerships.
- ERNIE models and Baidu Cloud AI infrastructure.

## What To Watch

- Apollo Go ride volume and cost per ride excluding subsidies.
- Search advertising decline versus AI cloud growth.
