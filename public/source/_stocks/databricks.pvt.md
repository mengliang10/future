## Investment Thesis

Databricks built its business on cleaning and querying enterprise data, then extended into AI with Mosaic, model serving and its own open DBRX model. The strategic argument is that proprietary data, not raw model weights, is where enterprise value settles, and that customers will pay for a governed path from warehouse to fine-tune. That places it against both the hyperscalers and the independent labs.

## Technology Exposure

- Lakehouse storage and query engine
- Mosaic model training, fine-tuning and serving
- Open models such as DBRX

## What To Watch

- Inference gross margin
- Competition with Snowflake and the hyperscalers
- Path to a public listing
