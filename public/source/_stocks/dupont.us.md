## Investment Thesis

DuPont supplies electronic materials including connectors, thermal management materials, films and CMP consumables, alongside water, protection and industrial businesses. Its Kapton and Mylar films are used in flexible circuits, batteries and aerospace insulation. The company has been reshaping its portfolio toward higher-margin specialties.

## Technology Exposure

- Electronic materials and interconnection
- Kapton and specialty films
- Water and protection technologies

## What To Watch

- Electronics demand
- Portfolio separation progress
- Raw-material and energy costs
