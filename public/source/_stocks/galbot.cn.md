Galbot was founded in 2023 and develops wheeled humanoid robots for shelf stocking, picking and retail assistance. It combines a mobile base with arms and a learned manipulation stack.

The company is part of a dense Chinese ecosystem of humanoid makers competing on cost and fast iteration in real stores and warehouses.

**Why it matters**

- Wheeled humanoid for retail shelves

- Manipulation models trained on store data

- Cost-focused hardware
