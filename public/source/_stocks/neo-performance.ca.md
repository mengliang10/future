## Investment Thesis

Neo Performance Materials processes rare earths and produces magnets, rare-earth chemicals and specialty ceramics from facilities in Europe and Asia. It is one of the few non-Chinese magnet producers with established customers in automotive and industrial motors. The company is expanding magnet capacity in Estonia.

## Technology Exposure

- Sintered rare-earth magnets
- Rare-earth chemicals and oxides
- Specialty ceramics

## What To Watch

- Estonia magnet plant ramp
- Automotive and defence demand
- Feedstock supply
