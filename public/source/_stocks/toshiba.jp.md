## Investment Thesis

Toshiba builds nuclear reactors and power equipment, and retains semiconductor and storage businesses. Its nuclear unit supplies boiling-water reactor technology and maintenance, while its power business serves grid and industrial customers. The group has been reshaped after years of restructuring.

## Technology Exposure

- Nuclear reactors and services
- Power generation and grid equipment
- Semiconductor and storage products

## What To Watch

- Nuclear maintenance demand
- Power-equipment orders
- Restructuring progress
