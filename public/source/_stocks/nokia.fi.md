## Investment Thesis

Nokia sells radio access, optical and IP networking equipment, and has pivoted toward data-centre and defence connectivity as telecom capex flattens. Optical transport is in demand because AI clusters need to move data between buildings and campuses. The company's challenge is competing with larger rivals on scale while funding 6G research.

## Technology Exposure

- Optical and IP networking.
- 5G/6G radio access.

## What To Watch

- Data-centre networking orders.
- Telecom operator capex in Europe and India.
