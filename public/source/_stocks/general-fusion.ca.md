## Investment Thesis

General Fusion is developing a magnetised-target fusion approach that compresses plasma with liquid-metal pistons, and has built a demonstration facility in the UK. The design avoids solid first walls and uses liquid metal for heat extraction. Technical validation and funding are the risks.

## Technology Exposure

- Magnetised-target fusion
- Liquid-metal components
- Demonstration facility
