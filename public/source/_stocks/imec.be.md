## Investment Thesis

imec runs pre-competitive research on lithography, packaging and device architecture with every major chipmaker and tool vendor as a partner. Its pilot line took delivery of the first high-numerical-aperture EUV scanner, and its programmes set the timetable for sub-2nm manufacturing. It is a non-profit, but its research output is the closest thing the industry has to a shared roadmap.

## Technology Exposure

- High-NA EUV lithography research.
- Advanced packaging and device architecture.

## What To Watch

- Programme funding from industry and the EU.
- Talent and equipment access under export controls.
