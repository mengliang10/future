## Investment Thesis

Pilbara Minerals mines and concentrates spodumene at Pilgangoora in Western Australia and is expanding into downstream refining with partners. Its product feeds Chinese and other converters that make battery-grade lithium chemicals. Price realisations and Chinese demand set the outcome.

## Technology Exposure

- Spodumene concentrate
- Downstream lithium refining partnerships
- Mine expansion projects

## What To Watch

- Spodumene pricing
- Chinese conversion demand
- Expansion capex
