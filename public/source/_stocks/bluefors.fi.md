## Investment Thesis

Bluefors builds dilution refrigerators that cool superconducting qubits to millikelvin temperatures, and has become the dominant supplier to quantum-computing labs and companies. Each machine is a cryogenic plant with proprietary wiring, shielding and gas handling, and lead times have been a constraint on quantum hardware programmes. The company also supplies cryostats for physics and semiconductor research.

## Technology Exposure

- Dilution refrigerators
- Cryogenic wiring and measurement infrastructure
- Cryostats for research and metrology

## What To Watch

- Quantum-computing capex
- Manufacturing capacity and lead times
- Competition from other cryostat makers
