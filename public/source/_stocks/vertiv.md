## Investment Thesis

Vertiv sells uninterruptible power, thermal management, racks and busway to data-centre operators, and AI racks have sharply raised the content per megawatt. Liquid cooling, higher-density power distribution and on-site commissioning are the growth lines as accelerators push rack densities beyond what air cooling can handle. The business is capacity-constrained rather than demand-constrained, and backlog converts with installation schedules.

## Technology Exposure

- Thermal management and liquid cooling
- Power distribution and UPS
- Rack and commissioning services

## What To Watch

- Liquid-cooling revenue mix
- Backlog conversion and lead times
- Competition from Schneider, Eaton and Chinese suppliers
