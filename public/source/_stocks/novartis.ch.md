## Investment Thesis

Novartis sells oncology, cardiovascular, immunology, neuroscience and ophthalmology medicines, and has narrowed its portfolio to innovative drugs since the Sandoz spin-off. It is investing in radioligand therapy, gene therapy and targeted protein degradation through acquisitions and partnerships. Growth depends on launching new medicines as older ones lose exclusivity.

## Technology Exposure

- Oncology and radioligand therapy
- Immunology and neuroscience
- Gene therapy and targeted protein degradation

## What To Watch

- Launch execution for new medicines
- Patent expiries
- Acquisition integration
