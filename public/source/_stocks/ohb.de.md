## Investment Thesis

OHB builds satellites, payloads and space systems for institutional and commercial customers, and supplies structures for European launchers through MT Aerospace. Its order book is dominated by European Space Agency and national programmes. The company has been restructuring and exploring partnerships to improve scale.

## Technology Exposure

- Satellite systems and payloads
- Launcher structures and components
- Space science and navigation

## What To Watch

- ESA and institutional budgets
- Satellite programme execution
- Restructuring progress
