## Investment Thesis

SKC makes specialty films, chemicals and materials, and its Absolics subsidiary is commercialising glass-core substrates for advanced packaging. The company has been restructuring away from legacy film businesses toward semiconductor and battery materials. Execution on the glass-substrate ramp is the central question.

## Technology Exposure

- Glass-core substrates through SK Absolics
- Specialty films and materials
- Battery and semiconductor materials

## What To Watch

- Glass-substrate qualification and yield
- Restructuring proceeds
- Funding of the US plant
