## Investment Thesis

XREAL sells lightweight glasses that act as an external display for phones, handheld consoles and laptops, a narrower use case than spatial computing but one consumers understand immediately. The Air and One families compete on weight, price and optical quality, and the company has shipped in volume relative to enterprise-focused rivals. It remains dependent on phone ecosystems it does not control and on component costs it cannot set.

## Technology Exposure

- Consumer AR glasses
- Display and waveguide modules
- Accessory and developer ecosystem

## What To Watch

- Attachment to phone and console platforms
- Component cost and channel margin
- Competition from phone makers' own glasses
