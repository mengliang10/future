## Investment Thesis

Arcturus develops mRNA vaccines and therapeutics using lipid-nanoparticle delivery, with programmes in infectious disease and rare metabolic disease. It has partnered with vaccine manufacturers and government programmes. Clinical data and funding are the main swing factors.
