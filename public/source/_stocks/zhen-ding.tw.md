## Investment Thesis

Zhen Ding is the largest printed-circuit-board producer by revenue, with a base in iPhone mainboards and flexible boards and a growing IC-substrate business. It supplies substrate-like PCBs and module boards that sit just below the leading-edge substrate makers in density, and it is investing in substrate capacity for AI and networking packages. Its scale and Apple exposure make it a useful read on consumer and data-centre board demand.

## Technology Exposure

- Substrate-like PCBs and high-density interconnect boards
- Flexible and rigid-flex boards
- IC substrates for mobile and networking

## What To Watch

- IC-substrate capacity ramp
- Apple and handset board content
- AI-server board qualification
