## Investment Thesis

Centrus Energy supplies enriched uranium and fuel services to utilities and is rebuilding US enrichment capacity with government support. Enrichment is a strategic bottleneck, especially for high-assay low-enriched uranium needed by advanced reactors. Contract pricing and capacity build-out drive the story.

## Technology Exposure

- Uranium enrichment services
- Fuel sales to utilities
- HALEU production for advanced reactors

## What To Watch

- HALEU capacity build-out
- Government funding
- Enrichment pricing
