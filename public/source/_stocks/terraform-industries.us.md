Terraform Industries is developing a process that combines direct air capture with electrolysis to produce methane that can drop into existing gas infrastructure.

The company's thesis is that cheap solar electricity makes synthetic fuel cost-competitive with fossil gas, creating a drop-in decarbonisation path.

**Why it matters**

- Solar-driven synthetic methane

- Drop-in gas compatibility

- Modular production units
