## Investment Thesis

Hoya makes mask blanks and glass substrates for photomasks, along with optical lenses, medical endoscopes and hard-drive substrates. Mask blanks are a genuine bottleneck: every advanced photomask starts from a low-defect blank, and qualification takes years. The company's optics and medical businesses provide balance through semiconductor cycles.

## Technology Exposure

- EUV and DUV mask blanks
- Photomask glass substrates
- Optical lenses and medical endoscopes

## What To Watch

- EUV mask-blank demand
- Mask-shop capex
- Medical segment growth
