Climeworks builds direct-air-capture plants that pull carbon dioxide from the atmosphere using solid sorbents and geothermal or renewable heat.

Its Orca and Mammoth plants in Iceland sell removals to corporate buyers, and the company is developing larger modular plants in the United States.

**Why it matters**

- Solid-sorbent DAC

- Geothermal-powered plants

- Corporate removal contracts
