## Investment Thesis

Zurich Instruments produces lock-in amplifiers, impedance analysers and qubit controllers used in quantum-computing labs and materials research. Its instruments combine high-speed analogue front ends with FPGA processing, which is what qubit readout and control require. The company was acquired by Rohde & Schwarz, giving it scale and distribution.

## Technology Exposure

- Lock-in amplifiers and impedance analysers
- Qubit control and readout systems
- Precision measurement instruments

## What To Watch

- Quantum-lab instrumentation demand
- Integration with Rohde & Schwarz
- Competition in test and measurement
