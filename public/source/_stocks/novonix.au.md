## Investment Thesis

Novonix produces synthetic graphite anode material and supplies battery-testing equipment from facilities in Canada and the US. Synthetic anode can be tuned for fast charging and longevity, and the company is positioning for US local-content requirements. Commercial-scale output and customer contracts are still developing.
