## Investment Thesis

AIXTRON builds MOCVD and deposition equipment used to make compound semiconductors such as gallium nitride, silicon carbide and microLEDs. Its tools are central to power electronics, RF devices and display manufacturing. Order intake is lumpy and tied to a few large capacity programmes.

## Technology Exposure

- MOCVD and deposition equipment
- Power and RF compound-semiconductor tools
- MicroLED and display equipment

## What To Watch

- SiC and GaN capacity additions
- MicroLED adoption
- Order timing and concentration
