## Investment Thesis

Taiyo Nippon Sanso, part of the Nippon Sanso Holdings group, supplies electronic specialty gases, gas-delivery equipment and cryogenic systems in Japan and overseas. Its electronics business is tied to Japanese and Asian fab capex, while its cryogenic technology serves semiconductor, medical and space applications. The group has been expanding in the US and Europe.

## Technology Exposure

- Electronic specialty gases
- Cryogenic equipment and systems
- Industrial and medical gases

## What To Watch

- Asian fab capex
- Cryogenic and space-related demand
- Overseas expansion integration
