Solid Power makes the solid electrolyte powder that replaces liquid electrolyte in solid-state cells, and has worked with BMW and Ford on cell development.

Its strategy is to sell electrolyte material and licence cell designs to established battery makers rather than compete as a cell manufacturer.

**Why it matters**

- Sulfide solid electrolyte

- Licensing model

- Automotive partnerships
