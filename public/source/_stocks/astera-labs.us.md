Astera Labs makes connectivity chips and firmware that let processors, accelerators and memory talk reliably inside AI servers and racks. Its products include PCIe retimers, CXL memory controllers and fabric switches.

The company listed in 2024 and has become a pure-play way to own the rising connectivity content per AI system.

**Why it matters**

- PCIe and CXL connectivity

- Rack-scale fabric

- Software for link reliability
