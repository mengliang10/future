Physical Intelligence was founded in 2024 by researchers from Berkeley, Stanford and Google DeepMind. It develops vision-language-action models that are pretrained across many robot embodiments and then adapted to a specific task.

Its pi0 model is trained on a mixture of robot demonstrations, teleoperation and web data, and is intended to let a new arm or humanoid learn a task from a small number of demonstrations. The company sells the model layer, not the hardware.

**Why it matters**

- Cross-embodiment pretraining

- Small fine-tuning sets for new tasks

- Works with multiple robot makers
