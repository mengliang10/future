## Investment Thesis

Tempus AI provides genomic sequencing and data services to hospitals, pharma and researchers, and has built a large clinical and molecular database for AI-driven drug development. It also sells diagnostic tests and data licences. Path to profitability and data-privacy regulation are the key issues.

## Technology Exposure

- Genomic sequencing services
- Clinical data licensing
- AI diagnostics and trial matching

## What To Watch

- Revenue growth and margins
- Data licensing contracts
- Privacy regulation
