## Investment Thesis

IHI builds rocket engines, turbochargers and aerospace components, and has supplied propulsion for Japanese launch vehicles. Its engine work covers liquid-oxygen and hydrocarbon systems, which are central to modern launchers. The group's broader industrial business spans energy, ships and machinery.

- Aerospace components
- Energy and industrial machinery
