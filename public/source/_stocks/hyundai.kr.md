## Investment Thesis

Hyundai sells more electric vehicles than most legacy automakers and has paired that with hydrogen fuel-cell programmes and majority ownership of Boston Dynamics. Its US plant in Georgia began production to qualify for local-content incentives. The company is a test of whether a fast-following automaker can hold margin while funding three expensive transitions at once.

## Technology Exposure

- EV platforms and batteries.
- Hydrogen fuel cells and robotics through Boston Dynamics.

## What To Watch

- EV margin after incentives.
- Progress on hydrogen infrastructure economics.
