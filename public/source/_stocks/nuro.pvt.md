Nuro builds low-speed autonomous delivery vehicles and licenses its autonomy software. Its R3 vehicle is designed around cargo rather than passengers.

The company has run delivery pilots with grocery and restaurant partners and has shifted toward licensing its driving stack to other vehicle makers.

**Why it matters**

- Purpose-built delivery vehicle

- Autonomy stack licensing

- Low-speed urban operation
