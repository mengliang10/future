## Investment Thesis

Grab operates ride-hailing, food delivery and payments across eight Southeast Asian markets, and reached adjusted profitability in 2024 after years of subsidy-driven growth. Its advantage is local density and a multi-service app that spreads acquisition cost. The constraint is that each market has strong local competitors and low consumer loyalty.

## Technology Exposure

- Ride-hailing and delivery marketplaces.
- Digital financial services.

## What To Watch

- Incentive spend as a share of gross merchandise value.
- Financial-services credit losses.
