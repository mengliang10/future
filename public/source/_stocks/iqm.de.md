## Investment Thesis

IQM builds superconducting quantum processors and sells complete systems installed at customer sites, including a German-Finnish consortium and several national HPC centres. Its model is sovereignty-friendly: buyers own the machine rather than renting cloud time. The technology competes with IBM and Google at a much smaller scale, and error correction remains years away.

## Technology Exposure

- Superconducting quantum systems.
- Co-design with HPC centres.

## What To Watch

- Error-correction milestones.
- Public procurement across Europe.
