## Investment Thesis

DIC makes pigments, printing inks, display materials and electronic chemicals, including colour resists and polymers used in panels and packaging. Its display materials business is tied to panel makers in Korea, Taiwan and China, while packaging and industrial coatings provide volume. The company has been restructuring to improve returns.

## Technology Exposure

- Colour resists and display materials
- Pigments and printing inks
- Electronic and packaging resins

## What To Watch

- Panel-maker demand
- Restructuring progress
- Raw-material costs
