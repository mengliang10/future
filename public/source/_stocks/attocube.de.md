## Investment Thesis

attocube builds nanopositioners, cryogenic scanning microscopes and optical cryostats used to characterise qubits and quantum materials at low temperature. Its piezo-based positioning works at millikelvin temperatures, a niche capability that few companies offer. The company supplies research labs and quantum-hardware developers.
