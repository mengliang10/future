## Investment Thesis

EHang obtained type certification for its EH216-S passenger drone in 2023 and production certification shortly after, the first such approvals for an unmanned passenger aircraft. Commercial operations remain limited by airspace rules, vertiport infrastructure and public acceptance. The company is the test case for whether certification alone creates a viable urban air mobility business.

## Technology Exposure

- Certified autonomous passenger eVTOL.
- Low-altitude tourism and emergency response use cases.

## What To Watch

- Paid flight volume and route approvals.
- Unit economics with a safety pilot and ground crew.
