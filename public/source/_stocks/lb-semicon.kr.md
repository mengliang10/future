## Investment Thesis

LB Semicon provides bumping, wafer-level packaging and test for Korean fabless and memory customers. Its scale is modest, so utilisation on a few programmes moves revenue materially. The company has been adding capacity for advanced packages as domestic customers move to finer bump pitch.

## Technology Exposure

- Bumping and wafer-level packaging
- Test services
- Advanced package development

## What To Watch

- Customer programme ramps
- Utilisation and pricing
- Capex funding
