## Investment Thesis

Hansol Chemical makes hydrogen peroxide, deposition precursors and specialty chemicals for semiconductor and industrial customers. Hydrogen peroxide is a high-volume wet-clean chemical, while precursors carry higher margins and follow node transitions. The group also has paper and other chemical businesses.

## Technology Exposure

- Hydrogen peroxide and wet chemicals
- Deposition precursors
- Specialty industrial chemicals

## What To Watch

- Precursor qualification
- Memory capex
- Feedstock and energy costs
