## Investment Thesis

Onto Innovation makes optical inspection, metrology and lithography systems used to monitor film thickness, defects and overlay, including tools for advanced packaging. As packages become more complex, measuring bump height, warpage and die placement becomes essential to yield. The company has been growing its advanced-packaging exposure.

## Technology Exposure

- Optical metrology and inspection
- Advanced-packaging measurement
- Lithography and process control

## What To Watch

- Advanced-packaging metrology demand
- Leading-edge logic capex
- Competition in inspection
