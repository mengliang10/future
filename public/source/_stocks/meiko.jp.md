## Investment Thesis

Meiko Electronics produces multilayer and high-density PCBs for automotive, industrial and communications customers, with manufacturing in Japan, China and Vietnam. It is less exposed to leading-edge IC substrates than its Japanese peers but supplies the module and control boards that surround advanced chips. Automotive content growth is its steadiest demand driver.

## Technology Exposure

- Multilayer PCBs for automotive and industrial
- High-density interconnect boards
- Module and power boards

## What To Watch

- Automotive production volumes
- Capacity utilisation in Vietnam and China
- Copper and laminate costs
