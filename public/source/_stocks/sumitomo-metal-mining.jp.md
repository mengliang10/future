## Investment Thesis

Sumitomo Metal Mining produces nickel, copper, gold and battery cathode materials, with integrated mining and refining. Its nickel and cathode businesses supply Japanese and overseas battery chains, while copper and gold provide commodity exposure. The company is expanding cathode capacity outside Japan.

## Technology Exposure

- Cathode active materials
- Nickel and copper mining and refining
- Gold and precious metals

## What To Watch

- Cathode capacity and qualification
- Nickel and copper prices
- Mine operating performance
