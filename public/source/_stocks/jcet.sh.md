## Investment Thesis

JCET provides assembly and test services from plants in China and Singapore and has been developing fan-out and 2.5D packaging for domestic customers. It benefits from China's push to localise the semiconductor supply chain, though export controls limit access to some leading-edge tools and customers. Scale and state-linked demand underpin the business.

## Technology Exposure

- Advanced packaging and fan-out
- Wire-bond and flip-chip assembly
- Test services

## What To Watch

- Domestic advanced-packaging demand
- Export-control impact on tools and customers
- Utilisation and pricing
