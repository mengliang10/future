## Investment Thesis

Syensqo, spun out of Solvay, makes advanced composites, specialty polymers and battery materials for aerospace, defence and automotive customers. Its composites are used in launch vehicle structures and satellite components, while its polymers serve electronics and healthcare. The company is investing in battery materials and sustainable chemistry.

## Technology Exposure

- Composite materials and prepregs
- High-performance polymers
- Battery materials and electrolytes

## What To Watch

- Aerospace and defence demand
- Battery-materials ramp
- Portfolio investment
