## Investment Thesis

Circle earns reserve income on the cash and Treasuries backing USDC, plus fees from minting, redemption and developer services. Regulation that legitimises payment stablecoins expands its addressable market but also invites banks and payment networks to issue competing tokens. The business is therefore a spread business with a regulatory moat, not a speculative crypto protocol.

## Technology Exposure

- USDC issuance and reserve income
- Developer APIs and cross-border payments
- Tokenised finance infrastructure

## What To Watch

- Reserve yield as rates fall
- Bank-issued stablecoin competition
- Regulatory treatment of reserves and redemption
