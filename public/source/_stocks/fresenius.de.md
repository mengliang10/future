## Investment Thesis

Fresenius operates dialysis clinics, makes infusion and nutrition products, and supplies hospital services through its operating companies. Its dialysis business is a large, stable procedure-driven franchise, while its other units are being restructured. Reimbursement and demographic trends drive demand.

- Infusion and nutrition therapies
- Hospital services

## What To Watch

- Restructuring progress
- Emerging-market growth
