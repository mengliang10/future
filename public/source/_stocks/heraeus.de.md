## Investment Thesis

Heraeus processes precious metals into pastes, targets, sensors and medical components, and supplies quartz glass and specialty light sources. Its electronic-materials business provides conductive pastes and bonding materials used in chips and solar cells. The company is family- and foundation-owned and reports privately.

## Technology Exposure

- Precious-metal pastes and targets
- Quartz glass and specialty materials
- Sensors and medical components

## What To Watch

- Electronic-materials demand
- Precious-metal prices
- Solar and industrial demand
