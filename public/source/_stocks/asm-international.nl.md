## Investment Thesis

ASM International builds atomic-layer deposition and epitaxy tools used to form the thin films in advanced logic, memory and power devices. ALD intensity rises at each node as films get thinner and more conformal, making the company a structural beneficiary of leading-edge capex. It competes with larger deposition vendors but leads in several ALD niches.

## Technology Exposure

- Atomic-layer deposition tools
- Epitaxy equipment
- Deposition for power and memory devices

## What To Watch

- Leading-edge logic capex
- ALD intensity per node
- Competitive wins at gate-all-around
