## Investment Thesis

ENF Technology produces developers, thinners, etchants and cleaning chemicals used alongside photoresists in semiconductor and display fabs. These ancillary materials scale with lithography steps and are qualified together with the resist, giving incumbents an advantage. The customer base is Korean and concentrated.

## Technology Exposure

- Photoresist developers and thinners
- Etchants and cleaning chemicals
- Display process materials

## What To Watch

- Lithography intensity per node
- Korean fab utilisation
- Raw-material costs
