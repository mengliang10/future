## Investment Thesis

GlobalWafers supplies silicon wafers from plants in Taiwan, Japan, Korea, the US and Europe, having assembled a global footprint through acquisitions including SunEdison Semiconductor and, after a long regulatory fight, parts of Siltronic's planned combination. The geographic spread helps customers diversify supply, and long-term agreements underpin revenue. Wafer pricing remains cyclical.

## Technology Exposure

- 300mm and 200mm silicon wafers
- Epitaxial and specialty wafers
- Global manufacturing footprint

## What To Watch

- Long-term agreement pricing
- Capacity utilisation
- Regulatory outcomes in Europe
