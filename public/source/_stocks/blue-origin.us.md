## Investment Thesis

Blue Origin builds the New Glenn heavy-lift rocket, BE-4 engines, New Shepard suborbital vehicle and lunar-landing systems. It supplies BE-4 engines to United Launch Alliance as well as using them internally, making it both a competitor and a supplier to other launch providers. The company is also developing orbital infrastructure and commercial space-station concepts.

## Technology Exposure

- New Glenn launch vehicle
- BE-4 and BE-7 engines
- Lunar landers and space systems

## What To Watch

- BE-4 engine deliveries
- Lunar programme milestones
