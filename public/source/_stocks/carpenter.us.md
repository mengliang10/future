## Investment Thesis

Carpenter Technology makes specialty alloys, powder metals and high-strength steels for aerospace, defence, medical and energy applications. Its alloys are used in rocket hardware, landing gear and engine components, and its powder-metal capability serves additive manufacturing. Aerospace and defence demand dominate results.

## Technology Exposure

- Specialty alloys and stainless steels
- Powder metals for additive manufacturing
- Aerospace and medical materials

## What To Watch

- Aerospace demand
- Additive-manufacturing powder growth
- Alloy pricing and energy costs
