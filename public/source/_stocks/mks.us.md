## Investment Thesis

MKS Instruments makes vacuum components, gas-delivery systems, lasers, optics and process-control instrumentation used inside semiconductor tools. Its products are specified into equipment from multiple OEMs, so revenue follows fab capex with a lag. The company has been reducing debt after large acquisitions.

## Technology Exposure

- Vacuum and gas-delivery subsystems
- Lasers, optics and photonics
- Process-control instrumentation

## What To Watch

- Fab capex
- Advanced packaging and power-device demand
- Debt reduction and integration
