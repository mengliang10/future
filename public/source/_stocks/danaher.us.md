## Investment Thesis

Danaher sells bioprocessing equipment and consumables through Cytiva, microscopy and analytical instruments, and clinical diagnostics. Its bioprocessing business supplies the filters, bags and chromatography resins used to make biologics, making it a levered play on biologic drug volumes. China exposure and biotech funding are the key variables.

## Technology Exposure

- Bioprocessing equipment and consumables
- Microscopy and analytical instruments
- Clinical diagnostics

## What To Watch

- Biologic production volumes
- China demand
- Biotech funding
