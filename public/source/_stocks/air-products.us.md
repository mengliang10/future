## Investment Thesis

Air Products supplies industrial and specialty gases, including the cryogenic gases used in launch vehicles and the ultra-high-purity gases used in fabs. It has committed heavily to large clean-hydrogen projects, which add execution risk alongside the steadier gases business. Electronics is a smaller but high-margin segment.

## Technology Exposure

- Industrial and specialty gases
- Liquid hydrogen and cryogenic supply
- Electronics gases and delivery

## What To Watch

- Hydrogen project execution and offtake
- Electronics demand
- Capital allocation and activist pressure
