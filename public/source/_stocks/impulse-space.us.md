Impulse Space builds small spacecraft that carry satellites from a rideshare orbit to their intended orbit, a service that launch brokers currently struggle to provide.

Its Mira vehicle has flown, and the company is developing a larger platform for deep-space and high-energy missions.

**Why it matters**

- Orbital transfer vehicles

- Rideshare orbit raising

- In-space propulsion
