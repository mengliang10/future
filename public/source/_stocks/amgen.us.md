## Investment Thesis

Amgen sells erythropoietin, immunology, oncology and bone-health biologics, and acquired Horizon to add rare-disease therapies. It is developing obesity drugs, including an injectable candidate acquired from a Chinese biotech. Patent expiries on legacy products and debt from acquisitions constrain flexibility.

## Technology Exposure

- Biologic medicines for immunology and oncology
- Biosimilars
- Rare disease and obesity pipeline

## What To Watch

- Obesity pipeline data
- Patent expiries
- Debt reduction
