## Investment Thesis

Biocon develops and manufactures biosimilars, generic active ingredients and specialty pharmaceuticals, with a focus on insulin, oncology and immunology. Its subsidiary Biocon Biologics sells biosimilars in developed markets. Regulatory approvals and price erosion drive results.

## Technology Exposure

- Biosimilar medicines
- Generic APIs
- Research services

## What To Watch

- US and EU biosimilar approvals
- Price erosion
- Manufacturing quality
