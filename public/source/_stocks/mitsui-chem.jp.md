## Investment Thesis

Mitsui Chemicals produces battery separators, electrolyte additives, optical films and semiconductor process materials. Its separator coatings and additives target safety and fast-charge performance in lithium-ion cells. The group also has a large petrochemical base exposed to spreads.

## Technology Exposure

- Battery separators and electrolyte additives
- Optical films and specialty resins
- Petrochemicals and industrial chemicals

## What To Watch

- Battery-materials qualification
- Petrochemical spreads
- Capacity investment
