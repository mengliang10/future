## Investment Thesis

Xanadu develops photonic quantum computers using squeezed light and integrated photonics, and maintains PennyLane, a widely used open-source quantum-software library. Photonic approaches can operate at room temperature, though they require low-loss detectors and precise interferometers. The company has published boson-sampling results and is working toward fault tolerance.

## Technology Exposure

- Photonic quantum processors
- Quantum software and PennyLane
- Photonic components and detectors
