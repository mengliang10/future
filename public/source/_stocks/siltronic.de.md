## Investment Thesis

Siltronic produces hyperpure silicon wafers for semiconductor fabs, with a new Singapore facility built to serve leading-edge capacity in Asia. Its business is contract-driven and capital-intensive, so utilisation and pricing on long-term agreements determine returns. The failed combination with GlobalWafers left it independent but with a concentrated customer base.

## Technology Exposure

- 300mm silicon wafers
- Polished and epitaxial wafers
- Singapore capacity

## What To Watch

- Ramp of the Singapore plant
- Wafer pricing and contract renewals
- Customer concentration
