## Investment Thesis

Doosan Enerbility builds nuclear reactor vessels, steam generators, turbines and large castings and forgings for power plants. It is a key supplier to Korean and overseas nuclear projects and is positioning for small modular reactors. Order intake from nuclear and gas power drives results.

## Technology Exposure

- Nuclear reactor vessels and steam generators
- Power-plant turbines and generators
- Large castings and forgings

## What To Watch

- Nuclear new-build orders
- SMR component contracts
- Power-plant capex
