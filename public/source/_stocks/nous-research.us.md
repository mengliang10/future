## Investment Thesis

Nous Research publishes Hermes models fine-tuned from open bases, and has worked on distributed training methods intended to let contributors train across scattered hardware. Its community distribution gives it reach disproportionate to its funding, and the open weights make its work a common starting point for other fine-tunes. Monetisation runs through hosted access and subscriptions rather than enterprise contracts.

## Technology Exposure

- Hermes open-weight models
- Distributed and decentralised training research
- Hosted model access

## What To Watch

- Funding versus community expectations
- Licensing constraints of base models
- Distributed training reaching competitive quality
