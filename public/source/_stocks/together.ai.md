## Investment Thesis

Together provides GPU capacity and an inference stack for open models, letting companies run Llama, Qwen or Mistral-class weights without owning clusters. Its business is a spread between rented compute and sold tokens, so utilisation and scheduling efficiency decide profitability. The company also funds open-model research, which keeps a supply of weights its platform can serve.

## Technology Exposure

- GPU clusters for training and fine-tuning
- Serverless inference for open models
- Research models and checkpoints

## What To Watch

- Gross margin under token price deflation
- GPU supply and contract pricing
- Differentiation from hyperscaler model catalogues
