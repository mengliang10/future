## Investment Thesis

Neuralink is developing a high-channel-count implant and a surgical robot designed to place flexible electrode threads without opening the skull widely. Its first human participants have used the device to control a cursor and play games, and the company is pushing toward speech decoding and higher channel counts. The technology is ambitious, but regulatory scrutiny, surgical risk and the long validation path for medical claims are the real constraints.

## Technology Exposure

- N1 implant and R1 surgical robot
- Cursor and speech decoding applications
- Clinical trial expansion

## What To Watch

- Trial approvals and adverse events
- Channel count and longevity in humans
- Reimbursement path for implantation
