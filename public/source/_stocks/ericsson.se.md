## Investment Thesis

Ericsson supplies mobile network infrastructure and derives most revenue from a handful of large operators. Its research spend is directed at 6G, AI-native radio and network APIs, bets that pay off only if operators find new revenue. The business is cyclical and geographically concentrated, with US and Indian operators driving volume.

## Technology Exposure

- Radio access networks.
- Network APIs and 6G research.

## What To Watch

- Operator capex recovery.
- Conversion of network APIs into revenue.
