## Investment Thesis

Oxford Instruments makes dilution refrigerators, superconducting magnets, X-ray and plasma systems for research and industry. Its cryogenics business supplies quantum-computing labs, and its magnets serve physics, medical and semiconductor applications. The company has a long record in low-temperature instrumentation.

## Technology Exposure

- Dilution refrigerators and cryostats
- Superconducting magnets
- Scientific and analytical instruments

## What To Watch

- Quantum research funding
- Semiconductor and materials-analysis demand
- Order timing
