## Investment Thesis

Lynas mines and processes rare earths in Australia and Malaysia and is building heavy-rare-earth separation capacity to reduce dependence on China. Its neodymium and praseodymium oxides feed magnet makers used in motors, wind turbines and defence. Price and policy exposure are both high.

## Technology Exposure

- Neodymium and praseodymium oxides
- Heavy rare-earth separation
- Rare-earth mining and processing

## What To Watch

- Heavy-rare-earth plant ramp
- Rare-earth pricing
- Malaysia licensing and regulation
