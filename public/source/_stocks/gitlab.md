## Investment Thesis

GitLab sells a single application for source control, CI/CD, security scanning and planning, which makes it the system of record for how code reaches production. Duo adds AI assistance and agents inside that workflow, and the company prices AI as an add-on to existing seats. Its challenge is competing with Microsoft's GitHub bundle on one side and point tools on the other.

## Technology Exposure

- DevSecOps platform subscriptions
- GitLab Duo AI add-ons
- Security and compliance scanning

## What To Watch

- Duo attach rate and pricing
- Net revenue retention against GitHub
- Free-to-paid conversion
