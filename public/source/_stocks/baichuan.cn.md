## Investment Thesis

Baichuan trained open and commercial Chinese-language models and has focused commercial effort on healthcare and enterprise assistants. It is one of several well-funded Chinese labs competing on domestic benchmarks while selling into regulated buyers that require local hosting. The competitive pressure from Alibaba, DeepSeek and Zhipu on price and quality is the main constraint.

## Technology Exposure

- Baichuan foundation models
- Healthcare and enterprise assistants
- Private deployment services

## What To Watch

- Domestic API pricing pressure
- Healthcare deployment conversion
- Funding runway
