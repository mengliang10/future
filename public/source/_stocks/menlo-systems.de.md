## Investment Thesis

Menlo Systems produces optical frequency combs, femtosecond lasers and synchronisation systems used in quantum computing, metrology and communications. Frequency combs provide the stable reference that links optical and microwave frequencies, which matters for ion and neutral-atom qubits. The company also supplies terahertz and spectroscopy instruments.

## Technology Exposure

- Optical frequency combs
- Femtosecond lasers
- Time and frequency synchronisation

## What To Watch

- Quantum and metrology demand
- Optical-clock deployment
- Competition from larger photonics groups
