## Investment Thesis

Rokid sells AR glasses and a companion computing station for entertainment, industrial inspection and museum or tourism deployments. It has positioned its platform for developers and enterprise integrators, with AI assistants layered onto the glasses. Domestic Chinese demand and public-sector digitisation projects are the base, and export markets are constrained by both competition and geopolitics.

## Technology Exposure

- Consumer and enterprise AR glasses
- Spatial OS and developer tools
- Cultural and industrial deployments

## What To Watch

- Domestic public-sector deployments
- Export market access
- Funding and unit economics
