## Investment Thesis

Rolls-Royce earns most of its revenue from civil aerospace and defence engines, with aftermarket services providing the durable cash flow. It is developing a 470 MWe small modular reactor, selected in 2025 for the UK's nuclear programme, which would add a long-dated infrastructure business. The SMR programme's value depends on repeat orders, not a single build.

## Technology Exposure

- Aero engines and defence power.
- Small modular reactor design and manufacturing.

## What To Watch

- SMR final investment decision.
- Civil aerospace engine flying hours.
- Defence order intake.
