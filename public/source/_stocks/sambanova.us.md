## Investment Thesis

SambaNova builds reconfigurable dataflow accelerators and sells them as systems and as a hosted inference service, arguing that token economics matter more than peak training throughput. Its composition-of-experts approach runs several open models behind one endpoint, which suits enterprises that want variety without managing infrastructure. The business competes for capital and customers against GPU clouds with far more supply.

## Technology Exposure

- Reconfigurable dataflow accelerators
- Hosted inference and model endpoints
- Enterprise AI systems

## What To Watch

- Funding and customer concentration
- Competitiveness against GPU clouds
- Software ecosystem maturity
