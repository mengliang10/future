## Investment Thesis

POSCO Future M produces cathode and anode materials and refractory materials, with integrated access to POSCO's lithium and nickel investments. It is building cathode capacity in Korea and North America to serve US battery plants and local-content rules. Utilisation and customer qualification determine returns.
