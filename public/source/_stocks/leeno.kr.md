## Investment Thesis

Leeno Industrial produces test probes, pins and sockets used in semiconductor and display testing. Its consumables are replaced as they wear, creating recurring demand tied to test volume rather than capex alone. Korean memory and display customers are the core base.

## Technology Exposure

- Test probes and pins
- IC test sockets
- Display test components

## What To Watch

- Memory and display test volume
- Advanced-packaging sockets
- Customer concentration
