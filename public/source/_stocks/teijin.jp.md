## Investment Thesis

Teijin produces aramid fibre, carbon fibre, polycarbonate and healthcare products, with materials used in aerospace, automotive and protective equipment. Its Twaron aramid competes with DuPont's Kevlar in ballistic and composite applications. The company has been restructuring its portfolio toward healthcare and high-performance materials.

## Technology Exposure

- Aramid and carbon fibre
- Polycarbonate and films
- Healthcare and pharmaceutical products

## What To Watch

- Aerospace and defence demand
- Aramid pricing
- Portfolio restructuring
