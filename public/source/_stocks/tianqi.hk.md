## Investment Thesis

Tianqi Lithium owns interests in the Greenbushes hard-rock mine and Kwinana refinery in Australia and in Chilean brine operations, and sells battery-grade lithium products. Its economics are highly sensitive to lithium prices and to the ramp of its refining assets. The balance sheet has been stretched by acquisition debt.

## Technology Exposure

- Lithium concentrate and battery-grade chemicals
- Australian and Chilean resource interests
- Lithium refining

## What To Watch

- Lithium pricing
- Refinery ramp and costs
- Debt reduction
