## Investment Thesis

TOPTICA builds tunable diode lasers, frequency combs and terahertz systems used in quantum computing, atomic clocks and spectroscopy. Trapped-ion and neutral-atom quantum computers rely on precise laser systems for cooling and manipulation. The company sells to research labs and quantum-hardware developers.

## Technology Exposure

- Tunable diode lasers
- Optical frequency combs
- Terahertz and spectroscopy systems
