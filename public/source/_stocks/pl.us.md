## Investment Thesis

Planet Labs operates hundreds of small imaging satellites and sells imagery, analytics and monitoring subscriptions to governments, agriculture and defence customers. Its daily-revisit dataset is the core asset, and defence and intelligence demand has grown. Constellation refresh capex and customer concentration are the constraints.

## Technology Exposure

- Earth-observation imagery
- Analytics and monitoring subscriptions
- Defence and intelligence services

## What To Watch

- Government contract growth
- Constellation refresh capex
- Competition from larger imagery providers
