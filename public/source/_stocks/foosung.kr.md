## Investment Thesis

Foosung produces nitrogen trifluoride and other specialty gases used for chamber cleaning and etching in semiconductor and display manufacturing. NF3 demand tracks fab utilisation and chamber-cleaning frequency, making the business cyclical but consumable-like. The company has also moved into battery materials.

## Technology Exposure

- Nitrogen trifluoride and specialty gases
- Electronic-grade chemicals
- Battery materials

## What To Watch

- NF3 pricing and capacity
- Fab utilisation
- Battery-materials execution
