Skild AI was founded in 2023 by former Carnegie Mellon faculty. Its approach combines large-scale simulated experience with real-world adaptation so that one model can drive different robot bodies.

The company targets industrial and warehouse tasks where a single general model can be fine-tuned cheaply, arguing that bespoke robotics software is the reason deployments stall.

**Why it matters**

- Simulation-first training

- One model across multiple embodiments

- Focus on industrial manipulation
