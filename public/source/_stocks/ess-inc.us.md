ESS makes iron flow batteries that store energy in tanks of iron, salt and water. The design separates power from energy capacity, so adding duration means adding electrolyte rather than cells.

Its customers are utilities, data centres and industrial sites that need six to twelve hours of storage.

**Why it matters**

- Iron flow chemistry

- Separate power and energy scaling

- Long-duration commercial systems
