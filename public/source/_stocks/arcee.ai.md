## Investment Thesis

Arcee focuses on small language models assembled through merging and continual pre-training, targeting enterprises that need cheap, private inference rather than frontier chat. Its 'model union' approach composes open weights into task-specific models, which can outperform a generic base of similar size. The market is crowded with inference platforms offering the same outcome as a service.

## Technology Exposure

- Small language models and model merging
- Domain adaptation and continual pre-training
- Enterprise deployment support

## What To Watch

- Enterprise adoption against managed platforms
- Quality ceiling of small models
- Funding
