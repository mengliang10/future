## Investment Thesis

SoftBank controls roughly 90% of Arm, whose instruction set underpins most mobile and embedded processors, and has become one of the largest financial backers of AI infrastructure, including the Stargate data-centre programme announced in 2025. Its value is a leveraged bet on AI compute demand, navigated through a holding-company discount and a heavy debt load.

## Technology Exposure

- Arm architecture licensing and royalties.
- AI data-centre investment vehicles.

## What To Watch

- Arm royalty growth from data-centre chips.
- Leverage and mark-to-market volatility of the portfolio.
