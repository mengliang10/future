## Investment Thesis

Waters sells chromatography columns, mass spectrometers and informatics used for drug characterisation and quality control. Its consumables and service revenue is recurring, while instrument sales follow lab capex. The company has been expanding into clinical and bioseparation applications.

## Technology Exposure

- Liquid chromatography
- Mass spectrometry
- Columns, consumables and service

## What To Watch

- Pharma R&D and QC spending
- Instrument replacement cycle
- China demand
