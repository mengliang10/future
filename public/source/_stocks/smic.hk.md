## Investment Thesis

SMIC is the country's largest foundry and the manufacturer behind Huawei's domestically produced advanced chips. Export controls restrict access to extreme-ultraviolet lithography, so the company reaches 7nm-class density through multi-patterning on older deep-ultraviolet tools, which raises cost and lowers yield relative to TSMC. Capacity is sold out on mature nodes, but leading-edge output is best understood as strategic capacity rather than a commercial business.

## Technology Exposure

- Domestic supply of advanced logic for Huawei and state programmes.
- Mature-node capacity for automotive, industrial and consumer chips.

## What To Watch

- Capex and depreciation against government support.
- Any evidence of yield improvement at 7nm-class without EUV.
