## Investment Thesis

Umicore makes cathode active materials, catalysts and recycling services, with battery materials at the centre of its growth plan. It operates cathode plants in Europe and Asia and recovers metals from battery scrap and industrial residues. The company has warned on battery-materials demand timing, making execution the key issue.

## Technology Exposure

- Cathode active materials
- Catalysts and precious-metal chemistry
- Battery and metals recycling

## What To Watch

- Cathode plant utilisation
- European battery demand
- Recycling economics
