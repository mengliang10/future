## Investment Thesis

PVA TePla builds vacuum furnaces, plasma treatment systems and crystal-growth equipment for semiconductor, industrial and medical customers. Its systems are used in silicon carbide crystal growth, wafer processing and materials analysis. The company benefits from compound-semiconductor capacity additions.

## Technology Exposure

- Crystal-growth furnaces
- Plasma and vacuum systems
- Metrology and analysis equipment

## What To Watch

- SiC capacity additions
- Industrial demand
- Order timing
