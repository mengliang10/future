## Investment Thesis

ArianeGroup, a joint venture of Airbus and Safran, builds the Ariane 6 launcher and supplies propulsion and missile systems. Ariane 6 is intended to restore European independent access to space at a lower cost than Ariane 5, though it faces competition from reusable US vehicles. The company is also studying a reusable European launcher.

## Technology Exposure

- Ariane 6 launch vehicle
- Propulsion systems
- Strategic and missile systems

## What To Watch

- Ariane 6 cadence and cost
- European institutional demand
- Reusable-launcher studies
