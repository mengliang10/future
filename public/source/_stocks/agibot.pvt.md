## Investment Thesis

AgiBot builds general-purpose humanoid robots and has demonstrated coordinated multi-robot manipulation in factory-like settings. It grew out of a Shanghai robotics ecosystem with municipal backing and has moved quickly from prototypes to small production runs. The company is a test of whether Chinese manufacturing depth can compress humanoid costs faster than the software matures.

## Technology Exposure

- Humanoid hardware for light assembly.
- Data collection for manipulation models.

## What To Watch

- Paid deployments and repeat orders.
- Reliability over full shifts.
- Funding through a hardware price war.
