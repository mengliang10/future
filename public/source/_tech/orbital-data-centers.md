## What This Is

The idea is to launch compute satellites that run AI workloads in orbit, powered by continuous sunlight and cooled by radiating heat into space. Downlinks would carry results rather than raw data.

## The Physics

Space solves solar availability and grid interconnection, but not heat rejection: in vacuum, heat must be radiated, which requires large surfaces. Launch cost and radiation tolerance of chips are the other constraints.

## Credible Niche

Rather than replacing data centres, the first use is likely onboard processing for Earth observation, so only insights are downlinked instead of raw imagery.
