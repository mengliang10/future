## What This Is

The Jiuzhang series, from the University of Science and Technology of China, used squeezed light and interferometers to perform Gaussian boson sampling faster than classical simulation. The 2023 iteration reported 255 detected photons. These machines solve a narrow sampling problem and are not programmable general-purpose computers, a distinction often lost in coverage.
