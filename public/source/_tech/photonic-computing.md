## What This Is

Photonic computing encodes information in light and performs operations with interferometers, modulators and detectors. Classical photonic processors target matrix multiplication and interconnect; photonic quantum computers use photons as qubits.

## The Trade-Off

Light is excellent at moving data and at certain linear operations, but it is poor at logic, memory and non-linear functions. Most realistic systems are hybrid: photonics for interconnect or specific kernels, electronics for control and storage.

## Where It Lands First

Optical interconnect is already commercial. Photonic compute is likely to appear first as an accelerator for inference or as a co-processor inside a conventional system rather than as a standalone computer.
