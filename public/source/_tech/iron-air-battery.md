## What This Is

An iron-air battery stores energy by reversibly rusting iron in the presence of oxygen and water. The materials are cheap and non-flammable, which suits stationary storage rather than vehicles.

## Why Duration Matters

Lithium-ion is economic for a few hours of storage. A grid with heavy wind and solar needs multi-day cover, and that requires a chemistry where adding hours costs little. Iron-air's target is 100 hours.

## Constraints

Round-trip efficiency is lower than lithium, so the battery needs cheap charging power. Site footprint is large, and the first projects must prove cycle life in real weather.
