## What This Is

Astroscale's ADRAS-J mission in 2024 approached and inspected a spent Japanese upper stage, demonstrating the navigation needed to match a tumbling object in orbit. Removal requires a docking or capture mechanism and a customer willing to pay for a public good. Insurers and regulators, not satellite operators, are the likely funders.
