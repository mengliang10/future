## What This Is

A category rather than one technology: iron-air, iron flow, sodium-ion, compressed air, thermal and pumped hydro all compete to cover multi-hour and multi-day gaps.

## The Real Constraint

Duration is only half the problem. Projects also need grid interconnection, land, water and a revenue contract that pays for capacity rather than only energy arbitrage.

## How It Wins

Long-duration storage wins when the alternative is new gas generation or transmission. That makes policy and utility planning the main demand driver.
