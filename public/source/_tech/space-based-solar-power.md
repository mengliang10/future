## What This Is

A solar array in geostationary or sun-synchronous orbit generates power continuously, then transmits it to a ground rectenna using microwaves. The attraction is baseload solar without weather or night.

## Why It Is Hard

The collector must be enormous, the transmission efficient at long range, and the launch cost low enough to matter. Ground rectennas also need large land areas and spectrum coordination.

## What Would Change It

Falling launch cost, lightweight deployable structures and high-efficiency solid-state transmitters. Even then, terrestrial solar plus storage is a moving target.
