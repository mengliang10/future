## What This Is

Rapidus, backed by Japanese industry and government, opened a pilot line in Chitose, Hokkaido, in 2025 with a target of 2nm mass production in 2027. It works with IBM and imec on process technology and aims at low-volume, high-value customers such as edge AI and custom silicon rather than smartphone volume. The venture's success depends on yield learning at a speed no new entrant has achieved in decades.
