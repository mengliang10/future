## What This Is

Announced in 2025, CloudMatrix 384 connects 384 Ascend 910C chips through a high-bandwidth fabric into a single logical system, trading power efficiency for scale. Huawei has claimed advantages over Nvidia's NVL72 on some aggregate metrics, largely because it uses more chips and more power to reach comparable throughput. It is the clearest example of China's system-level workaround for chip-level constraints.
