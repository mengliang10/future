## What This Is

IASO Biotherapeutics gained approval in China in 2023 for equecabtagene autoleucel, a BCMA-directed CAR-T therapy for relapsed multiple myeloma. It is one of several Chinese cell therapies competing on cost and speed of development, with domestic manufacturing and pricing well below US list prices. Access is limited by hospital capacity and reimbursement.
