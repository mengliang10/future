## What This Is

Pasqal builds neutral-atom processors using optical tweezers to arrange atoms in programmable arrays, an approach that has scaled past a thousand atoms in research settings. Neutral atoms are strong candidates for quantum simulation of materials and chemistry, where analogue operation can deliver value before universal error correction. System-level fidelity and uptime remain the constraints.
