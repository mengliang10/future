## What This Is

The e-CNY has been piloted across dozens of Chinese cities and used for retail payments, public subsidies and some cross-border tests. It is a liability of the central bank, designed for retail efficiency and state visibility rather than for wholesale settlement. Adoption remains modest relative to Alipay and WeChat Pay, which already solved retail payments.
