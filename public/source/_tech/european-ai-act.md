## What This Is

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 entered into force in August 2024. It bans certain practices, imposes transparency and documentation duties on general-purpose models from August 2025, and phases in high-risk obligations for products and regulated uses through 2026 and 2027. Compliance is a documentation burden that favours larger vendors with legal capacity, while giving buyers a common vocabulary for procurement.
