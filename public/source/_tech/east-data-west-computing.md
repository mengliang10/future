## What This Is

Launched in February 2022, the initiative designates eight national computing hubs and ten clusters, concentrated in Guizhou, Gansu, Ningxia and Inner Mongolia, where electricity is cheap and land is abundant. The aim is to locate training and storage capacity away from coastal demand centres and to seed local industry. It is a planning instrument, so utilisation and latency economics vary widely by cluster.
