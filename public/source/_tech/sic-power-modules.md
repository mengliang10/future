## What This Is

Silicon carbide devices switch faster and tolerate higher temperatures than silicon, which shrinks cooling and improves efficiency in traction inverters and fast chargers. Infineon, STMicroelectronics and Chinese entrants have expanded capacity sharply, and prices have fallen as 200mm substrates scale. The constraint has moved from device design to substrate supply and packaging.
