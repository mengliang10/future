## What This Is

Alibaba released Qwen models in a range of sizes under permissive licences, and by 2025 the family was among the most-fine-tuned bases on public model hubs. The strategy is distribution-led: broad weights availability pulls developers toward Alibaba's cloud and creates a local alternative to US APIs. Quality at small sizes matters as much as the flagship, because most deployments are cost-constrained.
