## What This Is

Tokamak Energy's ST40 reached plasma temperatures above 100 million degrees in 2022, a necessary but far-from-sufficient condition for fusion. The company argues that spherical tokamaks with high-temperature superconducting magnets can be smaller and cheaper than ITER-class machines. It is now designing a pilot plant, with no demonstration of net energy gain yet.
