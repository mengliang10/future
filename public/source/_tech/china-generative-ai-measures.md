## What This Is

Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services took effect on 15 August 2023. They require providers of services to the Chinese public to file algorithms, undergo a security assessment where required, and ensure training data and outputs comply with content rules. A labelling regime for AI-generated content took effect on 1 September 2025. The practical effect is that consumer-facing model services operate under licence, while enterprise and open-weight distribution sit in a greyer zone.
