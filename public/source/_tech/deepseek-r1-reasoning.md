## What This Is

DeepSeek released R1 in January 2025 under an MIT licence, with reasoning traces and distilled variants that could be run or fine-tuned locally. Its companion V3 model used a mixture-of-experts design with 671bn total parameters and about 37bn active per token. The published training figures — roughly 2.8m H800 GPU-hours — reframed the cost debate, although they exclude earlier research and data work.
