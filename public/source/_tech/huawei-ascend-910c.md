## What This Is

The Ascend 910C is Huawei's current data-centre accelerator, built on a domestically produced 7nm-class process and paired with Huawei's own interconnect and software stack. It is the main substitute for Nvidia hardware inside China after export controls restricted sales of advanced chips. Performance per chip is below the leading US parts; the system-level design compensates by scaling out.
