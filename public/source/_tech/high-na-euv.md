## What This Is

High-numerical-aperture EUV scanners use larger mirrors and a different optical path to print finer features, and cost well over €300m per tool. ASML shipped early systems to research and logic customers, with imec's pilot line providing a neutral testbed. Adoption depends on whether chipmakers can justify the cost against multi-patterning on existing EUV tools.
