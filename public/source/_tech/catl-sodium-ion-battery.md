## What This Is

CATL launched a sodium-ion product line in 2025, claiming around 175 Wh/kg and usable range in cold conditions where lithium-ion loses capacity. Sodium is abundant and avoids lithium and cobalt price risk, but lower energy density means the cells suit small cars and stationary storage before long-range vehicles. Commercial scale depends on cathode and hard-carbon supply chains.
