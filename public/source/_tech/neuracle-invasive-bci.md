## What This Is

Neuracle, working with Tsinghua University, implanted wireless epidural electrodes in patients with spinal cord injury and demonstrated control of cursor and robotic devices through decoded motor intent. The approach avoids penetrating the cortex, trading signal quality for safety. Trials remain small and long-term stability is unproven.
