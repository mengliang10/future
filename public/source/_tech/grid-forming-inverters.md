## What This Is

Conventional grids rely on the physical inertia of spinning generators. As wind, solar and batteries replace them, grid-forming inverters synthesise voltage and frequency to provide stability services.

## Why It Matters

Without inertia and fast frequency response, a high-renewable grid becomes fragile. Grid-forming controls are the electronic substitute, and they increasingly determine how much renewable capacity can be connected.

## Where It Bites

Battery projects, data-centre power systems and islanded microgrids are the first adopters. Standards and utility tests are the gating factor.
