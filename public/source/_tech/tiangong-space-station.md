## What This Is

Tiangong was assembled in orbit in 2022 and has hosted continuous crews since, with a three-module configuration and a planned expansion. It carries experiments in life science, materials and fluid physics, and China has invited foreign astronauts and payloads. For countries excluded from the International Space Station, it is the available alternative.
