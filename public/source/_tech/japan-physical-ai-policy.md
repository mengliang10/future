## What This Is

Japan's ministry of economy, trade and industry has promoted 'physical AI' — robots and machines with learned control — as a way to defend its industrial base against Chinese hardware and US software. Programmes fund data collection in factories, simulation environments and robot foundation models. The bet is that manufacturing depth and real operational data offset a weaker position in frontier models.
