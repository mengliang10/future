## What This Is

After the 2018 CRISPR pregnancy case, China criminalised unauthorised gene editing and introduced ethics review requirements for human research, with the Criminal Law amendment and subsequent regulations tightening oversight. The rules sit inside a broader biosafety framework and are enforced unevenly across provinces. They shape what Chinese biotech firms can test and what foreign partners will accept.
