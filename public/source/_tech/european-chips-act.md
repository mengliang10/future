## What This Is

The European Chips Act, in force since 2023, combines €43bn of public and private funding with a target of 20% of global semiconductor production by 2030, up from roughly 10%. It subsidises fabs, packaging and design capacity, with Germany, France and Italy as the main recipients. The gap between announced capacity and actual output is the number to watch.
