## What This Is

The Micius satellite, launched in 2016, demonstrated satellite-based quantum key distribution and entanglement distribution, and a 2,000km fibre link connects Beijing and Shanghai. A smaller satellite launched in 2022 extended the network, and China has reported intercontinental key exchange. QKD secures key distribution only; it does not encrypt stored data.
