## What This Is

Wayve trains neural networks to map sensor input directly to driving behaviour, arguing that this generalises better than hand-coded perception and planning pipelines. It raised US$1.05bn in 2024 and licenses its software to automakers instead of operating fleets. Validation is the hard part: an end-to-end system is harder to inspect when something goes wrong.
