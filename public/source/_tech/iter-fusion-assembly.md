## What This Is

ITER is a 35-nation project to build a tokamak designed to demonstrate tenfold energy gain, with first plasma targeted in the mid-2030s after repeated schedule slips. Its value is component qualification and physics data at burning-plasma scale, not electricity production. Cost overruns have made it a cautionary tale as well as a research asset.
