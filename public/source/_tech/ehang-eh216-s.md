## What This Is

The EH216-S received type certification from China's civil aviation authority in 2023, followed by production and airworthiness certificates, and has flown demonstration tourist routes. Certification covers the aircraft; airspace integration, vertiports and insurance remain unresolved. It is the clearest test of whether urban air mobility can become a service rather than a demonstration.
