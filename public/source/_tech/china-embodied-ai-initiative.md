## What This Is

Chinese ministries and city governments have funded humanoid innovation centres and set targets for deploying embodied AI in manufacturing, logistics and eldercare. Local subsidies lower the cost of pilots for automakers and electronics plants, which generates real operating data. The risk is that deployment targets are met with subsidised units rather than economically useful ones.
