## What This Is

BYD's Blade battery packs long lithium-iron-phosphate cells directly into the pack, raising volume utilisation and passing nail-penetration tests that made LFP credible for passenger cars. The architecture helped BYD integrated vertically from cells to vehicles and pushed the industry back toward iron chemistry. Its weakness is cold-weather performance and energy density versus nickel-rich cells.

## Investment Exposure
