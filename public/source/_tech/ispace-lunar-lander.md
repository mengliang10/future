## What This Is

ispace's first lander reached lunar orbit in 2023 but crashed during descent after a software and sensor issue; the company has since rebuilt for further missions with government and commercial payloads. The business model is delivery capacity sold per kilogram, with national space agencies as anchor customers. Landing reliability is the whole product.
