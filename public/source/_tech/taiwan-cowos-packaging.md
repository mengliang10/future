## What This Is

Chip-on-wafer-on-substrate packaging places accelerator dies and HBM stacks on a silicon interposer, which is how high-bandwidth memory reaches the GPU. TSMC has doubled CoWoS capacity repeatedly and still cannot meet demand, making packaging a gating factor for AI accelerator shipments. The technology is a moat as much as a process node.
