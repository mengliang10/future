## What This Is

Douyin, ByteDance's Chinese short-video platform, links algorithmic content recommendation to in-app storefronts, live selling and advertising, creating a closed loop from discovery to purchase. The system's advantage is behavioural data at scale and a recommendation engine tuned for engagement and conversion. It has become the template that TikTok Shop, Reels and YouTube Shorts are adapting outside China.
