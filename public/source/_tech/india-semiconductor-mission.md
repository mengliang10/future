## What This Is

The India Semiconductor Mission, launched in 2021 with about US$10bn of incentives, has approved assembly and test plants and a fabrication project, with Micron's Sanand facility and Tata's Dholera fab among the anchors. The strategy starts with packaging, which is capital-light relative to leading-edge fabs, and hopes to move up. Execution depends on water, power and skilled labour.
