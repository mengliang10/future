## What This Is

Unitree released the G1 humanoid in 2024 at roughly US$16,000, an order of magnitude below research platforms from Western vendors, alongside larger H1 models and a quadruped line. The robots are used heavily in university labs, which has made Unitree hardware a de facto standard for embodied-AI research. Commercial durability and safety certification remain unproven outside controlled settings.
