## What This Is

The Experimental Advanced Superconducting Tokamak in Hefei sustained high-confinement plasma for 1,066 seconds in January 2025, a duration record rather than a power record. A companion facility, the Burning Plasma Experimental Superconducting Tokamak, is under construction with a target of demonstrating energy gain around 2027. Long pulses matter because they test materials, heat exhaust and control, the engineering problems that stand between experiments and plants.
