## What This Is

M-PESA launched in Kenya in 2007 as a way to send money by SMS and became the country's dominant payment and store-of-value system, long before smartphones. It proved that a mobile network, an agent network and simple regulation could bank the unbanked. Newer systems in Africa and Asia copy the agent-based model rather than the app-based one.
