## What This Is

The Nuri, or KSLV-II, flew successfully in 2022 and 2023, making Korea a country with an independently developed orbital launch capability. It is a three-stage liquid rocket and has carried commercial and government payloads. Korea is now planning a reusable successor and has stood up a national space agency, shifting from a test programme to an industry policy.
