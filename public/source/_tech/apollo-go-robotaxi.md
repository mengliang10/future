## What This Is

Apollo Go operates paid fully driverless rides in Wuhan and several other Chinese cities, with a fleet in the high hundreds and millions of cumulative rides reported. Fares are subsidised and vehicles are concentrated in geofenced districts, so the service is a learning system as much as a business. Cost per vehicle and remote-assistance ratios determine whether it can be run without subsidy.
