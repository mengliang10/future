## What This Is

Rolls-Royce SMR is a factory-built pressurised-water reactor design of about 470 MWe, selected by Great British Nuclear in 2025 as the preferred technology for the UK programme. SMRs aim to cut construction risk through modular manufacturing, but the design still requires generic design assessment and site licensing. First power is a 2030s proposition.
