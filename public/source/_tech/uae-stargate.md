## What This Is

Announced in 2025, the UAE Stargate project is planned as a multi-gigawatt AI campus in Abu Dhabi, linking G42, MGX, OpenAI and Oracle with US government backing for chip exports. It is the largest attempt to make a Gulf state a compute jurisdiction rather than a customer. Power, cooling and chip export licences are the constraints, and the announced capacity is far ahead of built capacity.
