## What This Is

Bio-manufacturing programmes microbes or enzymes to make molecules that are normally derived from petrochemicals or plants. Products range from flavours and cosmetics to nylon precursors and alternative proteins.

## The Economic Test

Fermentation must beat the incumbent on cost at plant scale, not in a lab. Most failures come from scale-up, contamination and downstream purification rather than from the strain itself.

## Why It Matters

It offers a route to materials that are supply-constrained, geographically concentrated or emissions-intensive, and it uses existing fermentation infrastructure.
