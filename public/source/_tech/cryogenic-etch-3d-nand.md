## What This Is

Cryogenic etch cools the wafer far below room temperature during deep reactive-ion etching, improving profile control and throughput for the high-aspect-ratio channels in 3D NAND. Tokyo Electron has positioned the technique as a route to more than 400 layers, where conventional etch struggles to reach the bottom uniformly. The approach matters because NAND scaling now happens vertically, not laterally.
