## What This Is

Chang'e 6 launched in May 2024 and returned 1,935.3 grams of lunar far-side material in June 2024, the first samples from that hemisphere. The mission required a relay satellite for communications and demonstrated precision landing, ascent and rendezvous. China has since invited international researchers to apply for access, using sample distribution as scientific diplomacy.
