## What This Is

Samsung introduced gate-all-around transistors at 3nm and is scaling the approach at SF2, aiming to win foundry customers who want a second source to TSMC. Gate-all-around improves electrostatic control at small dimensions but demands new integration steps and defect control. Samsung's challenge has been yield and customer confidence rather than the underlying device architecture.
