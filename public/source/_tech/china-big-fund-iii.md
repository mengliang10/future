## What This Is

The National Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund's third phase was registered in May 2024 with RMB 344bn, larger than the first two phases combined. Its stated focus is on equipment, materials and advanced packaging — the bottlenecks that export controls made visible — rather than on fab construction alone. State capital of this size changes the cost of failure for domestic toolmakers.
