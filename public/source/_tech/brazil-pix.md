## What This Is

Pix launched in 2020 and became the country's dominant payment method within four years, with free person-to-person transfers and mandatory participation by large banks. It lowered the cost of digital transactions and forced incumbents to compete on service rather than float. Pix also created a base for credit and for open-finance products.
