## What This Is

AgiBot, also known as Zhiyuan Robotics, builds general-purpose humanoids and has run demonstrations of coordinated multi-robot tasks in simulated and real settings. It emerged from a Shanghai robotics ecosystem with strong local government backing and has attracted attention for rapid iteration on hardware. Production volume and reliability data remain limited.
