## What This Is

NAVER released HyperCLOVA X in 2023, trained with a strong emphasis on Korean language and local context, and has pushed it into search, commerce and enterprise services. The strategic case is that global models under-serve Korean and Japanese, and that public-sector buyers prefer domestic jurisdiction. Compute access and model quality at scale remain the constraints.
