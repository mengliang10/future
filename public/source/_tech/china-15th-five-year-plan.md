## What This Is

The Fourth Plenum of the 20th Central Committee, meeting in October 2025, adopted recommendations for the 15th Five-Year Plan, which governs 2026 to 2030. The text elevates 'self-reliance and strength in science and technology' and the construction of a modern industrial system, with advanced manufacturing, AI and energy security as recurring themes. Five-year plans are directional rather than binding budgets, but they align ministries, banks and state-owned buyers behind a small number of priorities.
