## What This Is

The Unified Payments Interface, launched in 2016, lets any licensed app move money instantly between bank accounts using a virtual address, with near-zero merchant cost. It processed more than 16 billion transactions a month by late 2024, and its open architecture allowed PhonePe, Google Pay and Paytm to compete on the same rail. It is the clearest example of public infrastructure creating private innovation.
