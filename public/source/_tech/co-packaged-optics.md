## What This Is

Co-packaged optics places the optical engine next to the switch or accelerator die instead of at the edge of the board. Electrical traces get shorter, so signal loss and power per bit fall.

## Why Now

Rack-scale AI systems are pushing copper links to their distance and power limits. Optics has already replaced copper for long-haul and data-centre links; co-packaging is the step that brings it inside the box.

## What to Watch

Manufacturing yield, laser reliability and serviceability. A failed optical engine inside a package is much harder to replace than a pluggable module, which is why operators are cautious.
