
# Rest of world

Outside the US, China, Europe and Northeast Asia, the most consequential technology stories are not model launches. They are public payment rails, subsidy programmes, compute geography and a single packaging chokepoint in Taiwan. Each changes the cost of doing business for everyone else.

## Public rails

India's UPI and Brazil's Pix both made instant payments a utility, and both did it by mandating interoperability rather than picking a winner. UPI processed more than 16 billion transactions a month by late 2024; Pix became Brazil's dominant payment method within four years. Kenya's M-PESA is the older proof: an agent network, not an app, is what banks people who deal in cash.

The commercial consequence is the same in all three. When payments are near-free, banks and fintechs must earn margin from credit, deposits and relationships. Nubank's model is built on exactly that shift.

## Subsidy and assembly

India's Semiconductor Mission started with assembly and test rather than leading-edge fabs, which is the realistic entry point: packaging is capital-light, builds skills and plugs into global supply chains. Whether it progresses to fabrication is a decade-long question.

## Compute geography

The UAE's Stargate campus is the largest attempt to make a Gulf state a compute jurisdiction rather than a customer, pairing sovereign capital with US partners and chip export licences. The risk is announced capacity that never gets powered; the opportunity is cheap energy plus political alignment.

## The chokepoint

Every AI accelerator still passes through Taiwan. TSMC's CoWoS packaging attaches HBM to the GPU, and capacity there has gated shipments more than any wafer node. Taiwan's position is simultaneously the strongest and most exposed in the supply chain.

## What would change this view

UPI or Pix monetisation collapsing for participants, Stargate failing to secure power and chips, or CoWoS capacity ceasing to be the binding constraint. Until then, the rest of the world's influence runs through rails, subsidies and geography — quieter than a model launch, and harder to route around.
