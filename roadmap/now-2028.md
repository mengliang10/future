## The Big Picture

We are in the early innings of the largest infrastructure buildout in human history. AI is the demand driver. Chips, data centres, power, and networking are the supply response. Every milestone below has named companies and stock implications.

      <span><span class="tag" style="margin-right:0.75rem;">2027</span> Wafer-on-Wafer 3D Integration: Pilot Production</span>
      <span class="acc-arrow">&#9660;</span>
    </button>
    <div class="accordion-body">
      <p>True 3D chip stacking without interposers reaches pilot volumes. TSMC SoIC-X and Intel Foveros Direct are the leading implementations. This approach doubles effective transistor density versus planar chiplet approaches by eliminating the interposer entirely and bonding wafers directly. Critical enabler for post-2nm scaling where traditional planar area scaling is exhausted.</p>
      <p><strong>What it means:</strong> This technology is the most direct path to continuing Moore's Law gains when 2D scaling stops. TSMC leadership here is not guaranteed: Intel Foveros has demonstrated comparable bond pitch. AMAT's wafer bonding equipment is required for both approaches. This is a 2027-2030 catalyst, not 2026.</p>

NVIDIA benefits from higher bandwidth enabling larger clusters.</p>
