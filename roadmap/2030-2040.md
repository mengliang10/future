## Milestones

<div class="roadmap-timeline">

  <div class="roadmap-item">
    <div class="roadmap-year">~2032</div>
    <div class="roadmap-event">First Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computer</div>
    <div class="roadmap-desc">A quantum computer with sufficient logical qubits to run Shor's algorithm on commercially relevant key sizes. RSA-2048 at risk. Post-quantum cryptography migration becomes critical infrastructure.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="roadmap-item">
    <div class="roadmap-year">~2033–2035</div>
    <div class="roadmap-event">Nuclear Fusion Net Energy Gain at Commercial Scale</div>
    <div class="roadmap-desc">Following NIF's 2022 ignition demonstration, private fusion companies (Commonwealth Fusion, TAE, Helion) targeting commercial pilot plants. First grid-connected fusion power by mid-decade best case.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="roadmap-item">
    <div class="roadmap-year">~2035</div>
    <div class="roadmap-event">AGI Debate Becomes Empirical</div>
    <div class="roadmap-desc">AI systems capable of matching human performance across virtually all cognitive tasks. Whether this constitutes "AGI" depends on definition, but economic and labour market impacts will be unambiguous and large.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="roadmap-item">
    <div class="roadmap-year">~2035–2038</div>
    <div class="roadmap-event">Brain-Computer Interfaces Go Consumer</div>
    <div class="roadmap-desc">Following Neuralink's medical trials, non-invasive BCI devices reach consumer markets for augmented cognition, gaming, and accessibility applications.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="roadmap-item">
    <div class="roadmap-year">~2040</div>
    <div class="roadmap-event">AI Designs Next-Generation Chips Autonomously</div>
    <div class="roadmap-desc">AI chip design tools advance to the point where the next node's architecture, physical design, and verification are completed primarily by AI systems with minimal human intervention.</div>
    <div class="roadmap-tickers"><span class="ticker-badge">NVDA</span><span class="ticker-badge">TSM</span><span class="ticker-badge">ASML</span></div>
  </div>

</div>
