<div class="roadmap-timeline">

  <div class="roadmap-item">
    <div class="roadmap-year">~2040–2045</div>
    <div class="roadmap-event">Post-Silicon Computing Architectures</div>
    <div class="roadmap-desc">Silicon transistor scaling reaches fundamental physical limits. Neuromorphic chips, optical computing, carbon nanotube transistors, and 2D material (graphene, MoS2) devices begin replacing silicon in leading-edge applications.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="roadmap-item">
    <div class="roadmap-year">~2043</div>
    <div class="roadmap-event">Molecular Manufacturing Prototype</div>
    <div class="roadmap-desc">Nanoscale assemblers capable of arranging individual atoms into functional structures demonstrated in laboratory conditions. Feynman's 1959 "plenty of room at the bottom" becomes engineering reality.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="roadmap-item">
    <div class="roadmap-year">~2045</div>
    <div class="roadmap-event">Human Longevity: Biological Age Reversal in Trials</div>
    <div class="roadmap-desc">Senolytics, epigenetic reprogramming, and AI-designed therapeutics begin demonstrating measurable biological age reversal in human clinical trials. Life expectancy projections begin to be revised upward materially.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="roadmap-item">
    <div class="roadmap-year">~2045–2050</div>
    <div class="roadmap-event">Intelligence Explosion Scenarios</div>
    <div class="roadmap-desc">If AGI is achieved by ~2035 and can recursively improve its own design, the resulting acceleration of AI capability could make all previous technology forecasting irrelevant. The 2040s may look as different from today as today looks from 1850.</div>
  </div>

</div>
