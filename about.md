## What This Site Is

Future Trends is a personal research and publishing platform tracking the technologies, companies, and stocks that matter most to the future:

- **Semiconductors**: chips, foundries, equipment, and the geopolitics of advanced manufacturing
- **Artificial Intelligence**: models, infrastructure, applications, and investment implications
- **Quantum Computing**: hardware platforms, milestones, and the path to commercial utility
- **Energy Tech**: clean energy, grid infrastructure, and the power demands of the AI era
- **Robotics & Space**: physical AI, autonomous systems, and humanity's expansion beyond Earth
- **Technology Roadmaps**: from today's breakthroughs to the far future, mapped to investment themes

Everything on this site is linked to stocks, fundamentals, and real investment implications. The goal is to translate deep technical analysis into clear, actionable intelligence.
