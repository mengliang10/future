## Why Energy Is a Tech Story

AI data centres are the fastest-growing electricity consumer in history. A single H100 GPU cluster draws as much power as a small city. This creates a massive, durable demand signal for power generation, transmission, and grid infrastructure — and it makes energy stocks part of the AI investment thesis.

## Key Sub-Themes

**AI Data Centre Power** — Hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta) have committed to trillions in capex. Power procurement, co-location, and grid interconnection are the binding constraints. Grid infrastructure stocks (ETN, PWR, ANET) are derivative AI plays.

**Nuclear Renaissance** — Tech companies signing PPAs with nuclear plants (Microsoft/Three Mile Island, Google/Kairos). SMR (Small Modular Reactor) stocks gaining attention. Uranium demand rising.

**Solar & Storage** — ENPH and SEDG under pressure from Chinese competition and interest rate headwinds. FSLR (First Solar) benefits from domestic content requirements.

**Hydrogen** — PLUG, BE facing commercial challenges. Hydrogen economics remain difficult outside industrial applications.
