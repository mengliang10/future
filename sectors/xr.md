## Why This Sector Matters

Spatial computing replaces the flat screen with an immersive layer overlaid on reality. When the hardware is cheap, comfortable, and connected — AR glasses become the new smartphone. Every app, interface, and social interaction gets redesigned. That transition is 5–8 years away at consumer scale, but the infrastructure investment is happening now.

## Key Sub-Themes

**Hardware Platforms** — Meta Quest (dominant VR), Apple Vision Pro (premium spatial computing), Magic Leap (enterprise AR). No listed pure plays on hardware except through META and AAPL.

**Virtual Worlds & Platforms** — Roblox (RBLX) is the largest user-generated virtual world — 88M daily active users, predominantly Gen Z and younger. First-generation metaverse without the headset. Unity (U) provides the engine that builds most VR/AR experiences.

**Social & Creator Platforms** — Snap (SNAP) has invested heavily in AR lenses and Spectacles AR glasses. Their AR developer platform has 300K+ developers. Snap is the hidden AR infrastructure play.

**Enterprise XR** — Training simulations, virtual design reviews, remote expert assistance. PTC (PTC), Siemens, and Microsoft (HoloLens) serve this market. Lower risk, paying customers now.

**Spatial Audio & Haptics** — Immersion, Dolby, and Qualcomm (QCOM) are enabling layers. XR without spatial audio is half an experience.

## Civilization Dependency Chain

```
Flat Screens → Mobile Touchscreen → VR Headsets (Closed) → Mixed Reality
→ AR Glasses (Lightweight) → Persistent Spatial Layer
→ Digital-Physical Merge → Neural Interface Replacement
```

Hardware weight is the gating factor: from 600g (Quest 3) → 100g → 30g. At 30g, AR glasses replace smartphones. At $300 price point, mass market. Estimated arrival: 2030–2032.

## Technology Roadmap Link

- **Now–2028:** Enterprise AR adoption, Vision Pro ecosystem build, Roblox economy maturation
- **2028–2030:** Consumer AR glasses at mass-market price, spatial internet emergence
- **2030+:** Persistent AR layer over physical world, AI-populated spatial environments
