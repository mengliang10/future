## Why This Sector Matters

Blockchain infrastructure is separating into two distinct investment theses. The first is Bitcoin as a treasury reserve asset: MicroStrategy defined the playbook; now every CFO has the conversation. Bitcoin ETF approval in 2024 opened the institutional floodgates. The second is the tokenisation of real-world assets — BlackRock's BUIDL fund passed $1B in months. When bonds, real estate, and private credit settle on-chain with institutional custody, the $500T global asset market gains a new infrastructure layer. The exchanges, custodians, and compliance infrastructure that enable this are the durable equity plays.

## Causal Signals

- **Bitcoin price** is the primary driver of every equity in this sector. MSTR has ~1.7× daily BTC beta; miners run 1.5–2×. The correlation with BTC is higher than any fundamental model — sizing for BTC exposure, not earnings.
- **ETF flow data** (weekly Bloomberg/CoinShares reports) is the cleaner institutional signal than BTC spot price. Sustained inflows → institutional demand building. Outflows at key price levels → distribution phase. The ETF changed the signal quality.
- **Hash rate and mining difficulty** adjustments are the leading indicator for miner margin. Difficulty rising faster than BTC price compresses margins. Watch monthly difficulty adjustments and the new generation ASIC delivery schedule (Bitmain S21 Pro, MicroBT M66S).
- **Federal regulatory posture** — SEC enforcement actions, CFTC jurisdiction clarity, and stablecoin legislation are the key regulatory catalysts. A US stablecoin bill (in progress) is a structural positive for COIN's custody and settlement business.
