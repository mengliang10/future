## Why Hardware Is Back

The software-dominant era of zero interest rates is giving way to a capex supercycle. Building AI requires physical infrastructure at unprecedented scale: custom servers, ultra-high-bandwidth networking, and massive storage systems. Hardware is no longer boring.

## Key Sub-Themes

**AI Servers** — SMCI (Super Micro) is the fastest-growing AI server ODM. DELL is winning hyperscaler AI rack contracts. HPE repositioning toward AI infrastructure.

**Networking** — ANET (Arista Networks) is the dominant high-speed networking vendor for AI clusters. Ethernet vs InfiniBand is the key architectural debate as GPU cluster sizes grow.

**Storage** — AI training generates and requires massive storage throughput. PSTG (Pure Storage) and NTAP (NetApp) positioned for AI-adjacent storage growth. Traditional HDDs (STX, WDC) being displaced by flash in AI workloads.

**CDN / Edge** — Cloudflare (NET) sits at the intersection of hardware infrastructure and software delivery.
