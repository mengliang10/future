## Why This Sector Matters

Every company in the world is a marketing company — acquisition economics determine who survives. The programmatic advertising stack, customer data platforms, and AI-generated creative are the picks-and-shovels of the digital economy. Third-party cookie deprecation forced a fundamental rethink of identity and measurement. The winners are building privacy-first data infrastructure that works without cookies — and they are taking share from the losers who can't. The shift from linear TV to connected TV is migrating a $60B US linear ad market into programmatic pipes that favour The Trade Desk and its demand-side peers.

  <div class="accordion-body open">
    <p>The Trade Desk (TTD) is the dominant independent demand-side platform — the software through which brands and agencies buy programmatic advertising across display, video, audio, and increasingly connected TV. Its structural advantage is independence: unlike Google's DV360 or Amazon's DSP, TTD does not compete with advertisers by selling its own inventory. This neutrality, combined with the best data management tools (UID2, OpenPath) and the most connected publisher relationships, makes TTD the default choice for sophisticated media buyers.</p>

The CTV tailwind is structural: linear TV ratings are declining 8–10% annually while streaming (Netflix, Disney+, Max, Peacock, Hulu) is adding ad-supported tiers. Every linear TV dollar that migrates to CTV flows through programmatic pipes. TTD takes 20% of gross spend as revenue share — a high-margin, volume-driven business with 10%+ annual volume growth baked in.</p>

  <div class="accordion-body">
    <p>Google's third-party cookie deprecation (delayed to 2025) forces the industry to find alternative identity resolution methods. Two solutions are competing: The Trade Desk's UID2 (an open-source, email-based identity standard adopted by 95%+ of major publishers in the US) and Google's Privacy Sandbox (cohort-based targeting that benefits Google's own DV360 platform). UID2 is winning publisher adoption precisely because it does not require advertisers to buy through Google. ZoomInfo (ZI) is the intent data layer for B2B: tracking which companies are researching which products, enabling sales teams to contact buyers at peak intent rather than cold-calling.</p>

## Causal Signals

- **CTV ad market share shift** — each percentage point of US ad spend migrating from linear TV to streaming translates directly into programmatic volume growth. Nielsen and eMarketer publish quarterly share data. Watch for linear TV share dropping below 25% of total video ad spend as the tipping point.
- **UID2 publisher adoption** — The Trade Desk discloses UID2 publisher count. When UID2 achieves 90%+ of US programmatic inventory, the identity advantage becomes insurmountable for non-UID2 platforms.
- **AI creative cost deflation** — as AI-generated ad creative (Midjourney, Firefly, Sora video) reduces production cost from $50K/campaign to $500/campaign, total ad volume expands because more advertisers can afford to test and iterate. Volume expansion benefits DSPs more than creative agencies.
- **Retail media network monetisation** — Amazon quarterly ad revenue (disclosed in earnings as "Other" revenue) is the leading indicator of whether retail media premium pricing is sustainable. Watch take-rate trends.
