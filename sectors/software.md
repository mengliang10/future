## The Software Monetisation Layer

While hardware and chips get the headlines, the long-term value of AI will accrue to the software companies that embed it into workflows, sell access to it at scale, and defend moats through data network effects. The question for every software stock is: does AI accelerate your growth, or does it commoditise your product?

## Key Sub-Themes

**AI-Enhanced SaaS** — NOW (ServiceNow), CRM (Salesforce), ADBE (Adobe), WDAY (Workday) all embedding AI copilots into workflows. Now-linked to ROI stories, not just feature releases.

**Cybersecurity** — CRWD, PANW, ZS, OKTA. AI makes both attacks and defenses more capable. Cybersecurity budgets growing structurally regardless of macro environment.

**Data & Analytics** — SNOW (Snowflake), DDOG (Datadog), MDB (MongoDB), ESTC (Elastic). The infrastructure layer for AI-ready data pipelines.

**Developer Tools** — TEAM (Atlassian), GTLB (GitLab). GitHub Copilot and AI code generation are simultaneously a threat and an opportunity.

**Cloud Platforms** — MSFT Azure, GOOGL GCP, AMZN AWS. The three hyperscalers capturing the vast majority of AI workload spend.
