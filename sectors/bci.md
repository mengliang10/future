## Why This Sector Matters

When a paralysed patient types 40 words per minute using only thought: as BrainGate demonstrated: brain-computer interface graduates from science fiction to medical fact. Neuralink's first human implant (January 2024) and Synchron's stentrode (implanted via blood vessel, no open-brain surgery) are two radically different engineering solutions to the same goal: read, decode, and eventually write to the human brain. The therapeutic market is large and nearer-term: Medtronic already has approved deep brain stimulation devices for Parkinson's, epilepsy, and depression generating billions in annual revenue. The consumer and cognitive enhancement market is 15+ years out: but the infrastructure, IP, and trained models being built today will determine who owns it.

  <div class="accordion-body">
    <p>Meta AI published research in 2023 demonstrating decoding of full perceived speech from non-invasive brain recordings using magnetoencephalography (MEG). While MEG is not portable, the same techniques are being applied to EEG. Consumer non-invasive BCI headsets (Emotiv, Neurosity, InteraXon Muse) exist today for biofeedback, meditation tracking, and gaming: but bandwidth is severely limited compared to invasive approaches. The AI decoding layer (transformers trained on neural signals) is advancing rapidly; each year the decode accuracy improves substantially as more labelled neural data becomes available. This is the consumer path: low-risk, lower bandwidth, but potentially mass-market when miniaturised into hearing-aid form factor.</p>

## Causal Signals

- **FDA approval milestones**: Breakthrough Device Designation and FDA PMA approval for closed-loop DBS or the first commercial speech decoding BCI are the catalysts that move the sector from research to revenue. Watch FDA device approval calendars.
- **Neuralink IPO / Precision Neuroscience IPO**: Neuralink's eventual listing would create direct listed BCI exposure for the first time. Pre-IPO secondary market activity and any disclosed funding round valuation updates are signals of commercialisation timeline.
- **Clinical trial readouts**: BrainGate, Synchron, and Precision Neuroscience publish results from ongoing feasibility trials. Metrics that matter: words per minute decoded, time to proficiency, device longevity, and adverse events.
- **AI neural decoding benchmark**: published annually by BCI research labs. Words-per-minute decoded from non-invasive signals is the clearest metric of the technology's progress toward consumer utility.
