## Why This Sector Matters

Quantum computing is not a near-term threat to classical computing — but it is the next fundamental shift in processing capability. Fault-tolerant quantum computers, when achieved, will break current encryption, accelerate drug discovery, and optimise logistics at scales impossible today.

## Key Sub-Themes

**Hardware Platforms** — Superconducting (IBM, Google, Rigetti), trapped-ion (IonQ, Quantinuum), photonic (PsiQuantum, Xanadu), and neutral atom (Atom Computing) approaches all have different qubit count, error rate, and temperature requirement profiles.

**Error Correction** — The single biggest barrier to useful quantum computing. Google's 2024 Willow chip demonstrated below-threshold error correction for the first time — a landmark milestone. IBM targets 10,000+ logical qubits by 2033.

**NISQ Era vs Fault-Tolerant** — We are currently in the Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) era. True commercial advantage requires fault-tolerant systems, likely 2030+.

**Quantum Cryptography** — A nearer-term commercial opportunity: quantum key distribution and post-quantum encryption standards (NIST finalised in 2024).

## Research Pipeline Signals

Investment framework for quantum sector positioning:

- Quantum stocks function as **long-duration venture options**, not as earnings stories. Traditional macro signals (yield curve, credit spreads) affect them primarily through risk appetite — when HY spreads widen, speculative long-duration positions including quantum stocks face multiple compression.
- **Technical milestone tracking** matters more than quarterly earnings for quantum pure-plays. The key milestones: error-corrected logical qubit demonstrations (2026 target), first 100+ logical qubit systems (2027-2028), and first commercially viable quantum advantage demonstration on a real-world problem.
- **Quantum Error Correction (2026)** is the most important near-term catalyst in this sector. A convincing QEC demonstration from IBM, Google, or IonQ that validates below-threshold error rates at meaningful qubit counts would trigger significant re-rating of the entire sector.

Position sizing for quantum stocks: treat as venture-style allocations (1-3% of portfolio at most), monitor milestone cadence not earnings, and be willing to hold through multi-year periods of apparent stagnation while the underlying science advances.
