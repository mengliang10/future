## 2. Core Research Themes

*   **Moore's Law 2.0:** Performance is now driven by **Packaging** (Vertical Stacking) rather than just Transistor Shrink.
*   **Dark Silicon:** The cooling crisis in data centers is limiting how many transistors can be active simultaneously.
*   **RISC-V vs. ARM:** The battle for the edge. Watch **ARM** for mobile dominance and **SiFive** for the open-source rebellion.

## 3. Trading Insight
Core Research indicates that **Design Cycle Times** are shrinking. Companies that can iterate on silicon yearly (like NVIDIA) will trade at a permanent multiple premium over those on a 2-year cycle (Intel).
